# The world

What a player meets in DAEMONS beyond the battles: a world that keeps time, changes with the seasons and the weather,
and has things in it the game never points to. **Nothing in the game explains any of it**, so this page explains only
how it works, never where things are. Every new word in the game is still a **draft** until it is approved.

## The clock

DAEMONS reads a **cartridge clock** if there is one (an emulator's, or a flash cart's RTC switched on). Without one
the game still plays, and its days follow your play time: an hour of play is a day.

| | hours |
|---|---|
| **dawn** | 5 to 7 |
| **day** | 7 to 18 |
| **dusk** | 18 to 20 |
| **night** | 20 to 5 |

The light tints the outdoor maps as the day goes, and the **weekday** is on your USER card. The terminal in your
bedroom answers **TIME** with a CLOCK line when it is reading a clock.

## The seasons

The seasons **repaint the world**, and the two editions live in **two hemispheres**: CONTENT keeps a northern year and
CONTEXT a southern one, so in the same week one player's world is in spring and the other's in autumn.

On fifteen of the early roads the grass changes with them: **one slot belongs to the season and one to the hour** --
dawn, dusk or night -- so some daemons are met only at a time of year, or a time of day.

## The weather

Since **v11.302.1** the weather follows the season, over the ordinary outdoor maps:

| season | the weather |
|---|---|
| spring | rain, and storms |
| summer | storms -- most days are clear |
| autumn | fog, and WIND |
| winter | snow |

It is drawn from the date, so it costs nothing in the save and two players on the same day in the same edition see
the same sky. A spell of weather can begin at any minute, last from ten minutes to a whole day, and cover one town or
both regions -- the islands can have their own. Most of the time the sky is clear.

Weather reaches into battle without touching the type chart: **WIND lifts VECTOR routines**, and snow falls as hail.

## PLAY: the HARP and the FLUTE

Since **v11.302.3** each edition has an instrument: **the HARP in CONTENT, the FLUTE in CONTEXT.** Take it out and a
**KEY WHEEL** of twelve keys opens, then folds into a **STAFF**:

| | |
|---|---|
| the notes of the scale | DOWN, LEFT, UP, RIGHT, B, A, SELECT -- one to seven |
| the octave | hold L or R |
| put it away | START |

Every note has its own colour -- C red, D orange, E yellow, F green, G blue, A indigo, B violet -- and stays bright
whatever the light. **Some things in the world listen** -- and to them the right notes in the wrong key are not quite
the same tune. What you play is written in the **SONGBOOK**.

## The NOTEBOOK

The NOTEBOOK keeps the pages you find and write, in sections -- school notes, lab notes, letters, the SONGBOOK and
more. A page not yet read carries a dot, and each says where it was found. A few end on a QR code, which opens a song.

## CALLOW SCHOOL

CALLOW SCHOOL has a TEXTBOOK, an EXAM you can leave and come back to, and a DIPLOMA for passing it.

## The MARKS

There are eight MARKS, each won at a BENCHMARK, and each prints a **virtue over its shadow**. A MARK is a lesson as
much as a badge: a BENCHMARK's lesson is mechanical, so it cannot be skipped by grinding. Some of what the game
teaches arrives quietly and is never counted.

## The INDEX

The INDEX has **395 entries**, and every one is written twice -- the two editions say something different about each
daemon. Its area page says where a daemon is **NATIVE**: its region and, when it keeps to them, its edition, hours
and seasons.

## Places the game never points to

There are places in DAEMONS that no sign and no character mentions. Finding them is the game. Please keep what you
find out of issues and pull requests.

## SEND

A party daemon can be **SENT** out of the game to the companion, where your phone or a board hosts it -- and it comes
home with what it learned. See [The companion](The-companion).
