# Books

DAEMONS has two books to find and read: **THE PROGRAMMER'S GUIDE TO THE HUMAN MIND** and **THE SUBTLE LAYER**. Both are
adapted from the author's own books and rewritten for the game's world, and every word of both is still DRAFT.

Each stays hidden until you find it. The game never says where, and neither does this page. Once your save holds one,
you can read it in the game and in the [companion](The-companion)'s LIBRARY (the app, the site and the Tab5), as an
open book: its cover, its contents, then each page in turn.

The full text of each lives in one file, the one the game and the companion are both built from. It is linked
below, so the words here never fall behind the game's.

## THE PROGRAMMER'S GUIDE TO THE HUMAN MIND

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CodeMusic/daemons-companion/main/server/data/art/guide_cover.png" width="480" alt="THE GUIDE's cover">

The mind treated as software, written as an engineer's manual. The brain is the hardware, and thoughts, feelings and
habits are code that can be read, debugged and rewritten. Its tools come from NLP, CBT, mindfulness, hypnotherapy and
journaling, each set out as a way of working on the system. 28 chapters:

- **Introduction:** Understanding the Mind as Software · Overview of Tools and Techniques · The Programmer's Role in
  Re-architecting the Mind
- **Part 1, Understanding the System:** The Human Brain: Hardware for the Mind · The Mind as Software: An Introduction
  to Mental Programming · Feelings in Parallel: the Machine Learning Perspective · Awareness as a Feedback Loop
- **Part 2, Core Programming Techniques:** Rewriting the Mental Code (NLP) · Debugging Thought Processes (CBT) ·
  Mindfulness: System Monitoring and Maintenance · Energy Flow and Chakras: Optimizing the Power Supply
- **Part 3, Advanced Mental Engineering:** Virtues and Shadows: Securing the System · Habit Formation: Automating
  Positive Routines · Thought and Emotion Integration
- **Part 4, System Failures and Recovery:** Recovering from System Crashes · Hypnotherapy: Direct Access Programming ·
  System Overhauls and Safe Operating
- **Part 5, Ongoing Maintenance and Optimization:** Flow States: Optimizing the Processor · Journaling: System Logs and
  Version Control · Lucid Dreaming: Nighttime Debugging and Testing
- **Part 6, Real-World Applications:** Interpersonal Dynamics: Networking Protocols · Conflict Resolution: Debugging
  Relationships
- **Conclusion:** The Future of Mental Programming · Ethical Considerations · The Path Forward: Continuous Development
- **Appendices:** Glossary of Terms · Case Studies · Further Reading and Resources

**Full text:** [docs/guide.md](https://github.com/CodeMusic/DAEMONS/blob/main/docs/guide.md)

## THE SUBTLE LAYER: a trainer's handbook

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CodeMusic/daemons-companion/main/server/data/art/subtle_cover.png" width="480" alt="THE SUBTLE LAYER's cover">

A handbook of practice, not of cosmology: what trainers do with the layer under the solid world, and what they notice
when they do it. It holds the craft and the physics side by side and chooses neither. It covers reading (the clairs),
steering (the psi) and bonding, what the physics allows, belief and the words that shape it, balancing mind and body,
a routine for the week, and how to stay correctable. Every page has a picture beside its words, and in TWO VOICES a
second, asking voice is printed faint. 51 pages in ten sections:

- **Introduction:** A Word First · The Container · The Solid and the Subtle · What Keeps the Seam Narrow · Read, Write
  and Link · Closure · Attunement and Voltage
- **The Read Family, the Clairs:** The Read Family: the Clairs · Clairvoyance and Clairaudience · Clairsentience and
  Claircognizance · Clairtangency and Clairalience · Telling a Read from Imagination · Reading Another
- **The Write Family, the Psi:** Steering, Not Writing · What Comes Back · Telekinesis and Pyrokinesis · Mending ·
  Fortune and Weathering
- **What the Physics Allows:** Ripples · All Paths · The Lever You Cannot Pull · The Two Slits · No One Spoon
- **The Link Family, the Bonds:** Two Seams on One Lean · Telepathy and the Empathic Bond · The Kindred Bond · The
  Chorus
- **Belief and Spelling:** Belief · The Cost of Hacking Belief · Spelling · Things Are Processes · Better Spelling
- **Balancing the Mind:** Virtues and Shadows · The Seven Pairings · One Pairing a Day
- **Balancing the Body:** The Seven Centres · Root, Sacral and Solar Plexus · Heart and Throat · Third Eye and Crown ·
  The Third Eye and the Crown
- **The Daily Routine:** The Week Is a Climb · At Waking, and Through the Day · At Rest · The Day for Reads · The Day
  for Looking Back · Before You Steer
- **Staying Correctable:** A Misread · The Mask · The Same Drive · Two Voices · Recognition, Not Force · A Last Word

**Full text:** [docs/subtle-layer.md](https://github.com/CodeMusic/DAEMONS/blob/main/docs/subtle-layer.md)

## The covers

The covers above are the companion's copies, and they carry no lettering. The game's font belongs to Nintendo, and the
companion publishes nothing of Nintendo's, so the app, the site and the Tab5 each set the title over the art in their
own type.
