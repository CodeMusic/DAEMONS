CRYSTAL AND AL, REDRAWN UNDER THE FABLE RULE (vision 9.4)
=========================================================

Scorn is done -- gfx/characters/style_d_scorn.jpeg IS the new sprite. These
five are what remain.

THE RULE, and it is worth reading before each one:

  1. INTEGRATED, never grafted -- paw hands, a tail, a head proportioned to
     the body. Never an animal head on a human body.
  2. DRAWN PLAINLY -- flat colour, cel shading, NO fur texture, no gloss, no
     musculature, no fluff. This is what separates fable from furry.
  3. HEAD-TO-BODY RATIO CARRIES AGE -- large head for children, SMALL for
     adults. Left unstated everyone comes out cute.

Generate CRYSTAL first. Then attach her result as the reference for AL, so
the family resemblance 1619 asks for survives: three foxes a palette apart,
readable as family with no text.

TWO THINGS THE LAST ROUND GOT WRONG, WORTH NOT REPEATING:

  * CRYSTAL IS A WOMAN IN HER SIXTIES. Two earlier generations came back as a
    young man. Her being a woman is load-bearing -- 4.20's injustice is a
    WOMAN recorded as incoherent -- so if the result reads male, regenerate.
  * DO NOT LET TWO LARGE AREAS SHARE A VALUE. The player's first pass put a
    cream hat on a cream coat at identical luminance and the brim, which is
    the one shape that identifies the character, dissolved into the garment.
    Crystal is golden-amber under a WHITE coat, which is a wide gap; keep it
    that way.

==========================================================================
CRYSTAL CLEAR -- the intro portrait.  Save as crystal_speech.jpeg
==========================================================================
Full-body character in the style of a Game Boy Advance RPG sprite. Flat cel
shading, thick dark outline, no gradients, no anti-aliasing. Plain flat
green background, no scenery, no shadow under the feet.

Subject: an adult female FOX, a senior research scientist in her SIXTIES,
drawn in the classic cartoon funny-animal tradition -- animals through and
through, built from simple confident shapes, the style of Carl Barks or
Disney's ROBIN HOOD.

SHE IS AN OLDER WOMAN AND SHE IS NOT CUTE. Adult proportions: a head SMALL in
proportion to her body. Narrow, level, intelligent eyes. Golden-amber fur with
SILVER at the muzzle and temples. Warm, quick, and a step ahead of you.

She is a fox ALL OVER. The same golden-amber runs from her face to her hands
and feet. Her hands are simple rounded PAWS. A fox tail, held low and
unobtrusive, NOT a large showy brush.

DRAWN PLAINLY: no fur texture, no individual hairs, no tufts, no fluff, no
muscle definition. Flat solid colour, the way a newspaper cartoon animal is
drawn.

Clothes: a long white lab coat worn open over a purple shirt, dark trousers,
plain shoes. A small tablet held against her side.

Pose: standing, turned slightly to her right, one hand extended toward the
viewer in a welcoming gesture. Full body, feet included, TALL frame. Under
about twenty colours.

Shared opening for all four -- paste it, then one pose block:

    Using the attached image as the family reference, draw a DIFFERENT
    character in the same style: a young male FOX of about eleven, her
    GRANDSON. Same drawing tradition, same flat plain rendering, same plain
    green background.

    He is a fox all over -- russet, a shade darker than hers, no silver.
    Rounded PAW hands, a fox tail held close, not a large brush. A head
    slightly LARGER in proportion than hers, because he is a child.

    Clothes: a dark olive-grey short-sleeved shirt, purple-mauve trousers, a
    brown belt, brown boots. Cocky rather than cruel -- a half smile, chin
    up, entirely sure of himself.

    DRAWN PLAINLY: no fur texture, no individual hairs, no fluff, no muscle
    definition. Flat solid colour with simple cel shading.
