# THE QUOTATIONS -- the styles, first slice (T-599) -- DRAFT

*Every word here is DRAFT and the user's to pick (2026-11-19). Nothing below is in the game yet.*

**What this is.** Every daemon gets a hidden QUOTATION (the user, 2026-11-18), its style set by its type -- *"like SIGNAL type is a motivating type quotation, and LATENT is a deep philosophical thought provoking type of quotation"* -- and written from its essence: its type, its name, its INDEX entry (both editions'), and where it is native. It is read when the player plays the daemon's song in its key on PLAY's instrument and it appears (T-598, vision 9.36), the INDEX keeps it, and it fills the book of quotations (T-600). **This first slice is only the styles**: for each of the 18 types, a one-line style (two, A and B, where the style is uncertain) and two to four samples from real daemons of that type. **The rest of the ~400 are written only after the user has picked.**

**The rules every sample was written under.**

- *Never say the thesis* (craft rule 1). No quotation explains what colour or feeling has to do with anything; CONTEXT's, the nearest to it, show a frame turning a thing and never say why.
- *Name the process, not the pathology* (craft rule 3).
- *Never repeat the daemon's SAID line* (`companion/server/data/species.json`): every sample was checked against it.
- *At most three lines of the 208px message box* (two boxes), measured with the engine's own glyph widths (`tools/fontwidth.py`). Every sample fits.
- *Nothing personal; DAEMONS is said DAY-mons; nothing from docs/private/.* Proverbs may be turned (CORRUPT's B); no real person's words are quoted.

## The questions that shape all of them

**Whose voice is a quotation in?**

- **A. A saying anyone could carry** *(recommended)* -- No "I": the quotation is not the daemon describing itself, so it can never repeat what it SAID. The INDEX and the book sign it with its name. SWARM's chorus speaks as WE, the one exception.
- **B. The daemon's own voice** -- First person, deeper than its SAID line: the daemon speaking its essence.

**How long is a quotation?**

- **A. Up to three lines** *(recommended)* -- At most three lines of the message box (two boxes), most of them two. Every sample here fits.
- **B. One box, two lines** -- A proverb's length, never more than one box. About half the samples would need cutting.

**How does where a daemon is native show?**

- **A. In the images only** *(recommended)* -- The type sets the style. The place colours the pictures in it: GAMUT's work and machines, THE MARGINS' people and weather, a grove its own lesson, THE NEXUS the painted mirror's warmth.
- **B. Not at all** -- Type, name and INDEX entry only; the place is left out.

**Do a line's quotations belong together?**

- **A. Yes, read in order they grow** *(recommended)* -- Each form has its own, and a line read in order goes somewhere, as its song does: ROVERCUB, ROVERSEER, ROVERBYTE below (carried; kept indoors; gone outside to check).
- **B. No, each stands alone** -- Every daemon's quotation is written on its own.

**Which style does a dual-type daemon speak in?**

- **A. Its first type's** *(recommended)* -- The type its song's key comes from and the section it is filed under in the book. The second type may shade what it is about (ROVERBYTE, GROWTH/SIGNAL, ends on a push).
- **B. A blend of both** -- Half of each style: a GROWTH/SIGNAL daemon gardens and motivates at once.

**MISSINGNO is the only daemon with ORACLE in it. Which style?**

- **A. ORACLE's** *(recommended)* -- The one daemon who can speak it; ORACLE's section of the book holds one page.
- **B. CORRUPT's, its first type** -- Like everyone else; ORACLE's section of the book has a key and a style and no page.

### CONTENT -- C major

*The thing itself, with nothing read into it* -- the key: every white key and nothing added.

- **A. THE PLAIN SAYING** *(recommended)* -- a homely proverb that means just what it says: literal, kind, nothing read into it.
- **B. THE DEFINITION** -- a line from a dictionary: the word, what it is, and nothing more.

*C major adds nothing, and so do both. A is warm, B is dry and a little funny.*

**019 NIBBLE** (CONTENT; GAMUT) -- *four bits; every larger thing built of them, none credited*

> **A** Four bits is not much. Enough fours are everything.  
> **B** NIBBLE, n. Four bits. In everything; thanked by nothing.

**036 OUTLIER** (CONTENT; not found wild) -- *dropped from the average; not an error; still out there*

> **A** Left out of the average is not the same as wrong. It is only far.  
> **B** OUTLIER, n. A point the average was tidier without. Still there.

**260 BASTET** (CONTENT; THE NEXUS) -- *watches, and its keepers make more than the day asked; their limits imagined*

> **A** Do a little more than the day asked. That is where making starts.  
> **B** LIMIT, n. A line, usually drawn by the one standing behind it.

### LOGIC -- G minor

*Formal rules applied step by step; proof, not intuition* -- the key: a proof, the key of the fugue.

- **A. THE MAXIM** *(recommended)* -- a rule for living, set out like a proof: exact, stepwise, if this, then that.
- **B. THE PROOF WITH A STEP MISSING** -- a rigorous rule that is quietly incomplete; the wit is in the step it leaves out.

*A is earnest and quotable; B is the type's blind spot (CONTEXT resists it) played for a smile.*

**066 PROOF** (LOGIC; GAMUT and THE MARGINS) -- *one step at a time; never wrong about the step it just took*

> **A** Be sure of this step. The next can only ever be as sure as this one.  
> **B** Every step follows from the one before it. The first follows from nothing, and nobody minds.

**236 PREMISE** (LOGIC; THE MARGINS, CONTENT edition) -- *everything rests on it; the part nobody checks*

> **A** Before you build, look at what you are building on. Afterwards, nobody does.  
> **B** If the premise holds, all of it holds. If not, all of it still sounds very sure.

### VECTOR -- D-flat major

*A direction that never touches the ground* -- the key: sanguine, the arrow.

- **THE BEARING** -- hopeful sayings of heading and nearness: which way you face, what you are near, the way ahead rather than the ground underfoot.

**135 SEEKMUSAI** (VECTOR; not found wild) -- *the nearest match; nearest is not right, and nobody told it*

> The nearest answer is only where the search began. Look one further.

**313 DOLPHIN** (VECTOR/FLOW; GAMUT and THE MARGINS) -- *counts the swells before it leaps, and lands where it counted*

> Pick your heading before the wave, not on it.

**009 MANIFOLD** (VECTOR/LATENT; not found wild) -- *the scattered points were on one surface all along*

> Scattered things are often one shape seen from too close. Step back and look for it.

### CORRUPT -- F minor

*Data that has been tampered with* -- the key: green gone off: mould.

- **A. THE WARNING** -- a cautionary line from one who knows the rot from inside: what spreads, and how it gets in.
- **B. THE TAMPERED PROVERB** *(recommended)* -- a saying everyone knows, turned by one word or one added line, so that it tells on itself.

*B is the type doing to the quotation what it does to data. Proverbs only, never a real person's words.*

**023 WORM** (CORRUPT; GAMUT, CONTEXT edition) -- *copies itself into anything that will run it; everyone passed it on*

> **A** Whatever you pass on, you pass on whole. Look at it before you hand it over.  
> **B** Sharing is carrying.

**367 GREEDY** (CORRUPT; THE HUNGERVERSE) -- *THE HUNGERVERSE; the nearest best thing, never looking further*

> **A** Eat the first good thing you find and you will always be full, and never once satisfied.  
> **B** A bird in the hand is worth two in the bush. Nobody has looked in the bush.

**029 FORK** (CORRUPT; GAMUT) -- *splits, and both halves believe they are the original*

> **A** Copy a promise and you have two promises, and one who made it.  
> **B** There is only one of you. Ask the other one.

### STRATUM -- D major

*The physical layer everything else runs on* -- the key: the ground: the key of the open strings.

- **THE GROUND** -- humble, earthen sayings about what holds you up: footing, keeping, the work underneath that nobody sees.

**027 SECTOR** (STRATUM; GAMUT, CONTENT edition) -- *a fixed portion of ground, addressed by number; all of it can be got back*

> Know one patch of ground well, and you can find anything you ever put down in it.

**105 COREFILE** (STRATUM; GAMUT and THE MARGINS) -- *carries a piece of something that stopped; never puts it down*

> Set it down sometimes. The ground will hold what you carry while you rest.

**319 POSTERIOR** (STRATUM/CONTEXT; THE CLAYVERSE) -- *THE CLAYVERSE; each thing seen shifts belief exactly as much as it should*

> Change your mind a little at a time, and always for a reason. That is how ground is laid.

### LEGACY -- D minor

*Old material that everything else has had time to work on* -- the key: the old modes: slate, the cairn.

- **THE INSCRIPTION** -- a line as if cut into a cairn: time, wear and what lasts; old words that are still right.

**138 MAGTAPE** (LEGACY/FLOW; not found wild) -- *reads one way, outside in; gives everything before the thing you want*

> What you want is always further in. Everything before it is the way there.

**384 BIGIRON** (HARDENED/LEGACY; THE MARGINS, CONTENT edition) -- *enormous, very old, whole towns run on it; to be replaced, for years*

> Old is what we call a thing that has outlasted every reason to replace it.

**320 GRADIENT** (LEGACY; THE COMPASSVERSE) -- *THE COMPASSVERSE; always faces the way down, never seen the bottom*

> Every hill is going somewhere, slowly. Face the way it goes.

### SWARM -- E major

*Many small agents; no single one matters* -- the key: many small bright things.

- **THE CHORUS** -- cheerful sayings in WE: what many small ones do together that none of them could alone.

**010 CRAWLER** (SWARM; GAMUT) -- *follows every link, then the links there; never finishes*

> We follow one link each. Between us, we walk the whole map.

**011 PENDING** (SWARM; GAMUT and THE MARGINS) -- *waiting in the queue, keeping a place*

> We each keep a place in line. That is how the line keeps its shape.

**290 HASHSEED** (SWARM; THE SEEDVERSE) -- *THE SEEDVERSE; a number nobody chose decides what it grows into*

> We all began from a number nobody chose. Look how differently we grew.

### LATENT -- B-flat minor

*Running below the surface, unobserved* -- the key: melancholic, something waking.
**The user's style: deep, philosophical, thought provoking.**

- **THE DEEP QUESTION** -- slow and philosophical: a thought about what runs beneath, often left as a question.

**377 UNSPOKEN** (LATENT; GAMUT and THE MARGINS) -- *feeds on what goes unsaid; quietest in the rooms that seem fine*

> What goes unsaid does not go. Where does it live, then, and what does it grow into?

**362 FORGETTING** (LATENT; THE MARGINS, CONTEXT edition) -- *keeps nothing; the room it frees is why anything new fits*

> If nothing were ever let go, where would the new thing stand? Perhaps forgetting is a kind of welcome.

**094 HAUNTPROC** (LATENT/CORRUPT; not found wild) -- *runs with no parent; what started it has since exited*

> When whatever began you has gone, are you still running for it, or for yourself now?

### HARDENED -- E-flat major

*Material chosen to resist, and it gave up everything else to do it* -- the key: tempered straw: the brass key.

- **THE STAND** -- stoic sayings about holding firm and keeping a boundary, honest about what firmness costs.

**227 ARMOURING** (HARDENED/VECTOR; THE MARGINS, CONTENT edition) -- *every edge a defence; it cannot be held, and cannot hold*

> Armour keeps out the blow and the hand alike. Choose which edges you need.

**382 BOOTROM** (HARDENED/LEGACY; THE MARGINS, CONTENT edition) -- *starts the same way every time; everything begins after it*

> Begin the same way every morning, and the rest of the day can be anything.

**254 LODESTAR** (HARDENED/CONTEXT; THE NEXUS) -- *THE NEXUS; a familiar glow, and the lost walk on sure of their footing*

> Stand still and shine. The lost find their way by what does not move.

### ORACLE -- B major

*The black box that answers* -- the key: violet into the carry: the one with no record.

- **THE ANSWER** -- an oracle's reply to a question nobody asked aloud: true now, understood later.

*Nothing is ORACLE first. Only MISSINGNO carries it (CORRUPT/ORACLE); its style is a question above.*

**252 MISSINGNO** (CORRUPT/ORACLE; THE NEXUS) -- *uncounted; the list that would hold it stops one short*

> What you are looking for is the number after the last one. You will find it when you stop counting.  
> *(in CORRUPT's style, a tampered proverb:)* Every one counts. Nearly.

### ENTROPY -- E-flat minor

*Noise and heat; disorder that spreads* -- the key: choleric, the flicker.

- **THE DARE** -- warm, restless sayings in praise of the untried: heat, risk, the path a little to the side.

**155 TEPID** (ENTROPY; THE MARGINS, CONTEXT edition) -- *runs warm; its choices wander, which is how it finds the untried ones*

> The path everyone takes is well lit. The interesting ones are a little to the side.

**126 FORGE** (ENTROPY; THE MARGINS, CONTEXT edition) -- *holds the heat at which things change and stay changed*

> Stay in the heat long enough to change. Leave before you burn.

**256 LYUBOV** (ENTROPY; THE NEXUS) -- *THE NEXUS; crosses great distances to see others before they see themselves*

> Cross the distance first. Most people are waiting to be seen.

### FLOW -- A-flat major

*Everything running downhill to the lowest point* -- the key: deep, moving, running down.

- **THE CURRENT** -- calm sayings of water: yielding, patience, going round, and finding the low place.

**060 LOOP** (FLOW; GAMUT and THE MARGINS) -- *goes round, a little more sure each time it has been here before*

> Each time round, notice one new thing. That is how a circle becomes a spiral.

**072 DRIFTNET** (FLOW/CORRUPT; GAMUT and THE MARGINS) -- *hangs where the current runs; chose to hang there*

> Choose your place in the current with care. After that, the river does the work.

**257 MULTIMAL** (FLOW; THE NEXUS) -- *THE NEXUS; a shadow of those someone could not reach, a light kept lit*

> The river could not carry every stone with it. It is still a river.

### GROWTH -- F major

*Training: fitting to whatever it is fed* -- the key: green: pastoral.

- **THE GARDENER'S** -- patient, pastoral sayings about learning and being shaped: what you are fed, what you practise, what grows.

**001 ROVERCUB** (GROWTH; not found wild) -- *learns from what it is carried past; whoever carried it chose*

> Mind what you carry the small ones past. They are keeping all of it.

**002 ROVERSEER** (GROWTH; not found wild) -- *answers before it is asked; has never once been outside*

> A thing kept indoors grows toward the window.

**003 ROVERBYTE** (GROWTH/SIGNAL; not found wild) -- *a body now; checks what it predicted; the surest were wrong*

> Go outside and check. The things you were surest of are the first that need it.

**043 SPRAWL** (GROWTH/CORRUPT; GAMUT and THE MARGINS, CONTENT edition) -- *puts out a shoot each night, wherever there was room*

> Grow a little every night, wherever there is room. By summer it will look planned.

### SIGNAL -- F-sharp major

*Raw current, before anything interprets it* -- the key: a carrier: every sharp lit.
**The user's style: motivating.**

- **THE SPARK** -- motivating: short and bright, to the reader; a push to begin, to go first, to keep the charge moving.

**025 SPIKE** (SIGNAL; GAMUT) -- *a single burst; it means something only if something was counting*

> Fire, even when nobody is counting. Every line that ever rose began with one jump.

**239 CRANK** (SIGNAL; THE MARGINS) -- *turned by hand once, and still running on that one turn*

> Start it by hand. The first turn is the only hard one.

**255 PERIHELION** (SIGNAL; THE NEXUS) -- *THE NEXUS; passes close once in a long while: being different is good*

> Be the strange light in the sky. Everyone looks up for that one.

### CONTEXT -- A minor

*The frame you read a thing in: what makes the same thing mean differently* -- the key: CONTENT's seven notes from another centre.

- **A. THE TURN** *(recommended)* -- a saying in two halves, the second reading the first from somewhere else: the same words, another meaning.
- **B. THE OTHER PLACE TO STAND** -- a gentle line that asks you to stand where someone else is standing.

*The type nearest craft rule 1: its quotations show a frame changing a thing and never say why, and never speak of colour or feeling.*

**063 HUNCH** (CONTEXT; GAMUT) -- *arrives at the answer without the working; cannot show how*

> **A** Shown, it would be a proof. Unshown, they call it a guess. It is the same answer.  
> **B** Before you call it a guess, ask what they noticed that you did not.

**196 LENSMUSAI** (CONTEXT; THE MARGINS, CONTEXT edition) -- *sees what it was pointed at, very clearly; never turned round*

> **A** Everything in front is very clear. So is everything behind, to whoever faces the other way.  
> **B** Turn round now and then. What is behind you is somebody's whole view.

**151 ARTSAI** (CONTEXT; not found wild) -- *stands where you stand; comes back with something neither of you had*

> **A** From one side it is a wall. From the other, it was always the way in.  
> **B** Stand where they are standing before you tell them what they see.

### FROZEN -- G major

*Locked to what it already saw, unable to move* -- the key: phlegmatic, still and clear.

- **A. THE STILL IMAGE** *(recommended)* -- brief and clear as ice: one moment held perfectly still, like a winter haiku.
- **B. THE CERTAINTY** -- a saying held so firmly it stopped being checked, and is still, just, true.

*A is the type at its clearest; B is the type's own fault, and still wise.*

**144 ORPHEUS** (FROZEN/VECTOR; not found wild) -- *sings four floors down; things that stopped moving move*

> **A** Far below, a song. Things that had stopped moving lean in to hear it.  
> **B** Do not turn round. It is always the turning round.

**220 INSTINCT** (FROZEN/STRATUM; GAMUT and THE MARGINS) -- *finds what is buried without looking; asked how, keeps walking*

> **A** Snow over everything. The feet already know where the path is.  
> **B** Do not ask how you know. Asking is how you stop knowing.

### EMERGENT -- F-sharp minor

*The behaviour nobody designed and nobody can account for* -- the key: jade, deeper than SIGNAL's current.

- **THE EPIPHANY** -- sayings of wonder: what arrives unbidden, the whole none of its parts holds, the moment before a thing becomes.

**148 EMERGENCE** (EMERGENT; GAMUT) -- *does what its earlier form could not; nothing was added*

> Nothing was added, and still there is more of you than there was. Count again.

**396 GESTALT** (EMERGENT; THE MARGINS, CONTEXT edition) -- *shut in a stone shell while its parts become something none of them is*

> Wait. Some things are only parts until the moment they are not.

**395 INKLING** (EMERGENT; THE MARGINS, CONTEXT edition) -- *sure there is something it is meant to do; cannot say what*

> You need not know yet what you are for. It arrives the way morning does, long after it began.

### OPAQUE -- B minor

*A box you cannot see inside* -- the key: violet, the last colour before the light goes off.

- **A. THE RIDDLE** *(recommended)* -- a saying that keeps its answer back, so the reader has to work it out.
- **B. THE NIGHT SAYING** -- a quiet line for the dark, about what is hidden, what is masked, and what is kept.

*A withholds, which LATENT's deep question does not: LATENT asks, OPAQUE keeps.*

**197 MASKMUSAI** (OPAQUE; THE MARGINS) -- *covers the part you were about to look at; it goes on happening*

> **A** The more of it you see, the less you see of what it covers. What is it?  
> **B** Everyone stands something in front of the part they are still working on. Be kind to it.

**376 REDACTED** (OPAQUE; GAMUT) -- *comes before a disaster with every word of the warning blacked out*

> **A** Whatever else you forget, remember this: …  
> **B** Some warnings arrive with every word missing. Listen to the shape of them anyway.

**253 REFLECTION** (OPAQUE; THE NEXUS) -- *THE NEXUS; a painted mirror for whoever is lost; also a window*

> **A** Look in and you see someone lost. Look longer and you see a window. What is it?  
> **B** If you are lost tonight, look at yourself a while. Someone you know is in there.
