CRYSTAL CLEAR — the intro portrait, and SCORN
=============================================

Target: graphics/oak_speech/oak/pic.png, 64x96, indexed.
The vanilla sprite it replaces is the FRLG professor: a full-body figure,
hand extended, standing on a pale ellipse, thick dark outline, flat cel
shading, no gradients and no anti-aliasing.

Attach as reference: the vanilla professor sprite (that exact style is the
target), plus your opera art of the character.

Do not ask for a specific pixel size. Gemini will not hit it and it does not
need to; the reduction is done here. What must be right is the POSE, the
CROP and the STYLE.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CRYSTAL CLEAR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full-body character portrait in the style of a Game Boy Advance RPG
sprite. Flat cel shading, thick dark outline, no gradients, no
anti-aliasing, no background detail.

Subject: an anthropomorphic golden-amber fox, standing, facing the
viewer and turned slightly to her right. She wears an open white
laboratory coat over a plain darker top, and plain trousers. One hand
is extended in front of her at waist height, palm up, offering
something. The other rests at her side.

She is a scientist, not a mascot: the posture is composed and still,
the expression level and unsmiling, the eyes direct. She is older —
this is a researcher near the end of a long career.

Fox features are structural, not decorative: upright ears, a narrow
muzzle, a full brush tail hanging behind her. The coat and trousers
are ordinary human clothing and fit properly.

Colour: golden amber fur, light on the muzzle and chest, darker at
the ears and legs. White coat. Keep the total number of distinct
colours low — under about fifteen.

Plain flat background, one colour, no scenery. Full body, feet
included, standing on a pale flat ellipse of shadow.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCORN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same style, same framing, same instruction set. Then:

Subject: an anthropomorphic red cobra in a well-cut grey three-piece
business suit, white shirt, red tie. Standing squarely, facing the
viewer, hands at his sides or one in a pocket. The hood is up and
open behind his head. The expression is pleasant.

He is not menacing and must not be drawn as a villain. He is a
senior executive who is having a perfectly good day.

Colour: deep red hood and scales, pale underside at the throat, grey
suit, red tie. Under about fifteen distinct colours.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY BOTH, AND WHY NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
vision.md 10 (amended 2026-09-04): three tailed characters among an untailed
cast reads as OTHERNESS rather than LINEAGE. The fix is not to convert
everyone -- it is to make fox one species among several. Scorn is the first
of that handful, and he has to arrive with her, not after her.

The game never says anyone is a fox. Nobody notices, ever.
