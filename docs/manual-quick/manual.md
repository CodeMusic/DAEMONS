# What DAEMONS is {#what .t-CONTEXT}

DAEMONS is a game about a world where the machines came alive. You play it on an iPad, an iPhone, a computer or a
Game Boy Advance, and you go at your own pace.

{{screens hello crystal | CRYSTAL CLEAR, who studies DAEMONS, says hello.}}

## A world of creatures {#world}

Long ago, the machines in this world began to live. They grew into creatures, each with a shape, a way of thinking
and a mind of its own. They are called **DAEMONS** (say it [*DAY-mons*]{style="white-space: nowrap"}).
It is a very old word: to the ancient Greeks, a *daimon* was a guiding spirit, one that keeps you company and helps
you along. Much later the word forked into a second, very similar one for a particular class of them: the helpers
that work quietly inside a computer.

Some live wild, out in the long grass and in caves. Others live alongside people, as partners.

**Everyone you meet in this world is an animal.** The scientist who helps you at the start is a fox. And you are a
**USER**: someone who looks after DAEMONS and travels with them.

## Your journey {#journey}

You begin at home, in a small town. A scientist called **CRYSTAL CLEAR** gives you your first DAEMON, and then you set
out to see the world.

Along the way you will:

- meet DAEMONS, and make friends with some of them;
- keep a record of every one you meet. That record is called the **INDEX**;
- and visit eight towns where a leader tests you. Pass a test and you earn a **MARK**, a little emblem, like a badge.

There is no rush. You can wander, talk to everyone, and play a little each day.

## The stories underneath {#stories}

DAEMONS has stories under its surface. People say things that mean more than they seem to. Places remember what
happened in them. The game never stops to explain itself: you piece it together as you go, and that is half the
pleasure.

**It comes in two editions, CONTENT and CONTEXT**, like two sides of one coin. They tell the same story in the same
towns. They differ in some of the DAEMONS you meet, and in what the INDEX says about them. Either is a good place to
start.

## The books {#books}

Somewhere in the world are two books you can find, and then read inside the game, a page at a time.

::: covers
{{cover guide}}

{{cover subtle}}
:::

- **THE PROGRAMMER'S GUIDE TO THE HUMAN MIND** looks at the mind as if it were a computer program: something you can
  read, understand and gently improve.
- **THE SUBTLE LAYER** is a handbook about noticing the quiet things under the everyday world. Carry it, and the game
  begins to show you small things it never showed you before.

Both were written by the person who made the game, and rewritten for its world.

::: rumour
And some players whisper about a third book. The full player's manual tells what is said of it -- and that is all
anyone will say.
:::

# Getting it going {#going .t-SIGNAL}

DAEMONS is made by fans, so you will not find it in a shop. It never comes with Nintendo's own game: it is made from a
copy of one you own. **So the first time, ask whoever gave you this booklet to set it up for you.** They only need to
do it once.

After that, there are two easy ways to play.

## On an iPad or an iPhone {#ipad}

The DAEMONS companion app has the game inside it.

1. Open the app.
2. **On an iPad**, turn it on its side. The game is on the left, and the companion on the right.
   **On an iPhone**, tap **GAME**.
3. The buttons are drawn on the screen. Touch them with a finger, just as you would press real ones.

{{pictures ipad | An iPad on its side: the game on the left, and the companion beside it.}}

::: note
The app is still being tested, so it is not in the App Store yet. Whoever set it up for you can add it to your iPad
or iPhone.
:::

## In a web browser {#browser .newpage}

Whoever runs the DAEMONS **playground** for you can give you an address to open in Safari or Chrome.

1. Open the address they gave you.
2. Choose an edition: **CONTENT** or **CONTEXT**.
3. Leave the save empty, for a new game, and press **PLAY**.
4. On a tablet or a phone, tap **TAP TO PLAY**. On a computer, use these keys:

| key | is the button |
|------|------|
| [←]{.btn .key} [→]{.btn .key} [↑]{.btn .key} [↓]{.btn .key} | the cross, to walk |
| [X]{.btn .key} | [A]{.btn}, yes |
| [Z]{.btn .key} | [B]{.btn}, no |
| [Enter]{.btn .key} | [START]{.btn .pill}, the menu |

::: tip
There are other ways too -- a real Game Boy Advance, or an emulator app on an iPhone. The full player's manual
explains each one.
:::


# How to play {#play .t-GROWTH}

Ten short steps, one to a page. Take them in order the first time, and keep this booklet beside you while you play.

::: remember
**If you remember only one thing:** [A]{.btn} means **yes**, and [B]{.btn} means **no**.
:::

## Step 1: The buttons {#buttons .step}

| button | what it does |
|------|----------|
| [✚]{.btn .cross} the cross | walks you about, and moves the pointer in a list |
| [A]{.btn} | yes; talk to someone; read the next line |
| [B]{.btn} | no; go back; close a menu |
| [START]{.btn .pill} | opens the menu |
| [SELECT]{.btn .pill} | you will hardly need it at first |
| [L]{.btn .shoulder} [R]{.btn .shoulder} | help, outside a battle |

{{diagram buttons | Where each one is, on a Game Boy Advance. On a phone or a tablet, the same buttons are drawn on the screen.}}


## Step 2: Reading what people say {#reading .step}

When someone speaks, their words appear in a box at the bottom of the screen.

- **A small red arrow** ▼ in the corner means there is more. Press [A]{.btn} to read the next line.
- **Take your time.** The game always waits for you.
- **When it asks a question**, like YES or NO, a pointer ▶ shows your choice. Move it with the cross and press
  [A]{.btn} to choose.

{{screens hello reason | A line with the red arrow, and a question with YES and NO.}}

::: tip
If the words appear too fast, press [START]{.btn .pill}, choose **OPTION**, and set **TEXT SPEED** to **SLOW**.
:::

## Step 3: A new game {#newgame .step}

1. Choose **NEW GAME**.
2. CRYSTAL CLEAR says hello and tells you about the world. Press [A]{.btn} to go on after each line.
3. She asks a question: when these two disagree, which do you follow -- **REASON** or **INSTINCT**? (Thinking it
   through, or trusting your gut.) There is no wrong answer. It decides who you play.
4. She asks your name. Pick each letter with the cross and [A]{.btn}, then choose **OK**.
5. Then you name a young USER from your town, who sets out at the same time as you.

{{screens crystal bedroom=Your_room | CRYSTAL CLEAR, and your room, where the game begins.}}

## Step 4: Walking about {#walking .step}

- **Press the cross** to walk. Hold it down to keep walking.
- **Walk into a door** to go through it. Stairs work the same way.
- **To talk to someone**, walk up to them, face them, and press [A]{.btn}.
- **Anything that looks interesting** -- a sign, a bookshelf, a machine -- press [A]{.btn} in front of it.

{{screen town | Out in town. Everyone here has something to say.}}

::: tip
**Feeling lost?** Talk to people. They often tell you where to go next.
:::

## Step 5: Your first DAEMON {#first .step}

CRYSTAL CLEAR keeps a lab in your home town. There she lets you choose one of three DAEMONS, each of which learns in
its own way:

{{sprites LABEL CLUSTER ROVERCUB}}

- **LABEL** learns from examples someone gave it, with the answers.
- **CLUSTER** puts things that look alike together, and works out the groups on its own.
- **ROVERCUB** is small enough to carry, and learns from wherever you take it.

**Choose the one you like the look of.** Any of them can go all the way.

From then on your DAEMON walks behind you. Face it and press [A]{.btn}, and it tells you how it is doing.

## Step 6: A battle {#battle .step}

Walk through **long grass** and, now and then, a wild DAEMON jumps out. The game calls a wild one **UNBOUND**. The
screen changes to a battle: theirs at the top, yours at the bottom.

**The green bar is health**, or **HP**. When a DAEMON's bar runs out, it stops.

{{screens battle battle-routines | The battle begins; then you choose a ROUTINE.}}

**To fight:** choose **ROUTINES**, then one of the routines, with [A]{.btn}. You take turns. Keep going until one bar
is empty.

| choose | to |
|----|------------|
| **ROUTINES** | do something. A **ROUTINE** is one of the things your DAEMON can do. |
| **POOL** | use something from your bag. The **POOL** is your bag. |
| **DAEMON** | swap in another of your DAEMONS |
| **DETACH** | run away |

## Step 7: Winning, and resting {#win .step}

**When their bar empties, you win.** Your DAEMON grows a little stronger. Now and then it learns a new ROUTINE: read
what the game says, and answer with [A]{.btn} or [B]{.btn}.

**Sometimes, after a battle, your DAEMON changes** into a new, bigger form. That is called **RECOMPILING**. It is a
good thing: let it happen, and do not press [B]{.btn}.

**When your DAEMON's bar empties**, it has **HALTED**. It is not gone: it needs a rest. Send in another, or run away.
If all of yours halt, you find yourself back at the last **CHECKPOINT** you visited (step 9), with everyone rested.

{{screen party | Your team. You can have six DAEMONS with you.}}

## Step 8: Making friends with a new DAEMON {#binding .step}

To add a wild DAEMON to your team:

1. **Weaken it first.** Bring its bar down -- but not all the way.
2. **Choose POOL**, and offer it a **USERBOX**: a little box a DAEMON can come to live in.
3. **If it stays in the box, it joins you.** If it pops out, try again.

This is called **BINDING**. The DAEMON is written into your INDEX as yours.

You can have **six** with you. The rest wait safely in **COLD STORAGE**, which you reach from the computer -- a **PORT**
-- in any CHECKPOINT.

::: tip
USERBOXES are sold at **THE REPO**, the shop. You will find one in most towns.
:::

## Step 9: Resting at a CHECKPOINT {#checkpoint .step}

A **CHECKPOINT** is where your DAEMONS rest and recover, and it is free. Most towns have one.

1. Walk in, and go up to the counter.
2. Press [A]{.btn} to talk.
3. Answer **YES**. In a moment, every one of your DAEMONS is well again.

{{screen checkpoint | Welcome to a CHECKPOINT.}}

**Visit one whenever your DAEMONS look tired**, and always before a long walk.

## Step 10: Saving your game {#saving .step}

**The game does not save by itself.** Save before you stop playing, and before anything big.

1. Press [START]{.btn .pill}.
2. Choose **SAVE**.
3. Answer **YES**. Wait until it says you saved the game.

{{screen start-menu | The menu. (This picture comes from the testing build, so it shows DEBUG; yours will not.)}}

**Next time, choose CONTINUE**, and you carry on exactly where you saved.

::: remember
**Save often.** It takes a moment, and it means you never lose your place.
:::


# When you want more {#more .t-LEGACY}

You know enough now to play for a long time. When you are curious, here is where to look.

## Little things worth knowing {#tips}

- **Help is always there.** Press [START]{.btn .pill} and choose **HELP**, or, outside a battle, press
  [L]{.btn .shoulder} or [R]{.btn .shoulder}.
- **Who is that?** In a battle, press [L]{.btn .shoulder} to read about the DAEMON you are facing, in your INDEX.
- **Your card.** Press [START]{.btn .pill} and choose your own name. It shows your MARKS, and the time.
- **Some ROUTINES work better than others.** Every DAEMON and every ROUTINE has a **type**, and some types are strong
  against others. You will get a feel for it; the full manual has the whole chart.
- **The world keeps time.** Day and night, the weather and the seasons follow the real clock.

# Words to know {#glossary .t-OPAQUE}

Every word the game uses, the first time you will meet it.

::: words
**BINDING** -- making friends with a wild DAEMON, so it joins your team.

**CHECKPOINT** -- a building where your DAEMONS rest and recover, free.

**COLD STORAGE** -- where your other DAEMONS wait while you carry six.

**CONTENT, CONTEXT** -- the game's two editions: one story, told two ways.

**CRYSTAL CLEAR** -- the scientist who gives you your first DAEMON.

**DAEMON** -- one of this world's living machine creatures.

**DETACH** -- running away from a battle.

**HALTED** -- out of health, and needing a rest.

**HP** -- health: the green bar in a battle.

**INDEX** -- your record of every DAEMON you have met.

**MARK** -- the emblem you earn by passing a town leader's test.

**POOL** -- your bag.

**PORT** -- the computer in a CHECKPOINT, where COLD STORAGE is.

**RECOMPILE** -- when a DAEMON grows into a new form.

**ROUTINE** -- something a DAEMON can do, in a battle or out of one.

**THE REPO** -- the shop.

**UNBOUND** -- wild: a DAEMON that has no USER.

**USER** -- you: someone who travels with DAEMONS.

**USERBOX** -- the little box you offer a wild DAEMON, to bind it.
:::

:::
