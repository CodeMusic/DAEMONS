AL CLEAR — four sprites
=======================

FOUR PROMPTS. Each block below is COMPLETE — copy one whole block, paste it,
generate, then come back for the next. Nothing has to be joined together.

Ty needs none. vision 4.3: "TY P. CLEAR, printed, never spoken." He exists in
the register, the Quicksilver ruins and a leitmotif, never in a room. That is
the device; giving him a face would spend it.

Targets, for reference only — do not put sizes in the prompt, the reduction is
done by tools/gbachar.py:

    graphics/oak_speech/rival/pic.png                64x96  intro portrait
    graphics/trainers/front_pics/rival_early_...     64x64  first battles
    graphics/trainers/front_pics/rival_late_...      64x64  later battles
    graphics/trainers/front_pics/champion_rival_...  64x64  the last fight

Save the results in gfx/characters/ as al_speech.jpeg, al_early.jpeg,
al_late.jpeg, al_champion.jpeg.

Two notes on WHY the wording is what it is, so it is not edited away:

  * KEEP HIS CLOTHES. Crystal's sprite worked because it kept the professor's
    purple shirt and extended hand -- the swap read as the same shot of a
    different person rather than a new asset. Same trick here.
  * THE BOX IS NOT A BALL, and prompt 2 says so twice. This project has no
    Poke Balls; 3.1's line is USERBOX -> ADMINBOX -> SUPERBOX -> ROOTBOX and
    tools/gbabox.py already draws one. The model reaches for a ball unless
    told not to, because everything it has seen in that pose holds one.

PROMPT 1 of 4 — the intro portrait
==========================================================================
Full-body character in the style of a Game Boy Advance RPG sprite. Flat cel
shading, thick dark outline, no gradients, no anti-aliasing. Plain flat green
background, no scenery, no shadow under the feet.

Subject: an anthropomorphic mid-amber fox, a confident young man. Spiky
orange-blond head fur. Dark olive-grey short-sleeved shirt, purple-mauve
trousers, a belt. Upright ears, a narrow muzzle, a full brush tail. Ordinary
human clothing that fits properly. He is cocky rather than cruel: a half
smile, chin up, entirely sure of himself. Under about fifteen distinct
colours.

Pose: standing at ease, turned slightly to his left, one hand resting on his
hip and the other holding a bag tucked against his side. Weight on one leg.
Full body, feet included, in a tall frame.
