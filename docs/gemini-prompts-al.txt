AL CLEAR — four sprites
=======================

Ty needs none. vision 4.3: "TY P. CLEAR, printed, never spoken." He exists in
the register, the Quicksilver ruins and a leitmotif, and never in a room. That
is the device; giving him a face would spend it.

Al needs four, and they are two different formats:

    graphics/oak_speech/rival/pic.png                64x96  intro portrait
    graphics/trainers/front_pics/rival_early_...     64x64  first battles
    graphics/trainers/front_pics/rival_late_...      64x64  later battles
    graphics/trainers/front_pics/champion_rival_...  64x64  the last fight

Do not ask for pixel sizes; the reduction is done by tools/gbachar.py. What
must be right is POSE, CROP and STYLE.

KEEP HIS CLOTHES. Vanilla Al wears a dark olive-grey shirt and purple-mauve
trousers, and his hair is spiky orange-blond. Crystal's sprite worked because
it kept the professor's purple shirt and extended hand -- the swap read as the
same shot of a different person. Do the same here: same palette, same clothes,
same attitude, and a fox instead of a boy.

HE IS BETWEEN. vision 10: Crystal golden-amber, Ty darker, Al somewhere
between. So a mid-amber fox -- warmer and darker than hers, and it is the
FAMILY RESEMBLANCE that has to read, not the species. Same ear shape and
muzzle geometry as Crystal.

Shared instruction for all four:

    Full-body character in the style of a Game Boy Advance RPG sprite. Flat
    cel shading, thick dark outline, no gradients, no anti-aliasing. Plain
    flat green background, no scenery, no shadow under the feet.

    Subject: an anthropomorphic mid-amber fox, a confident young man. Spiky
    orange-blond head fur. Dark olive-grey short-sleeved shirt, purple-mauve
    trousers, a belt. Upright ears, narrow muzzle, full brush tail. Ordinary
    human clothing that fits properly.

    He is cocky rather than cruel -- the expression is a half-smile, chin up,
    entirely sure of himself. Under about fifteen distinct colours.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INTRO PORTRAIT (tall, 2:3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ...standing at ease, turned slightly to his left, one hand resting on his
    hip and the other holding a bag tucked against his side. Full body, feet
    included. Weight on one leg.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. EARLY BATTLE (square)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ...leaning forward, holding a small round container out in front of him
    in one hand, about to throw it. Eager, up on the balls of his feet.
    Full body inside a square frame.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LATE BATTLE (square)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ...standing tall and square to the viewer, one arm raised above his head
    with the hand open. Less eager than before and more certain. Full body
    inside a square frame.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CHAMPION (square)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ...mid-motion with one arm thrown forward toward the viewer, the other
    back for balance, leaning in. The largest, most committed pose of the
    four. Full body inside a square frame.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
