AL CLEAR — four sprites
=======================

ONE image is generated from text. The other THREE are generated from THAT
image plus a short pose note. Four separate text prompts would give four
different foxes; only the reference keeps him one character.

Ty needs none. vision 4.3: "TY P. CLEAR, printed, never spoken." He exists in
the register, the Quicksilver ruins and a leitmotif, never in a room. That is
the device; giving him a face would spend it.

Save the results in gfx/characters/ as al_speech.jpeg, al_early.jpeg,
al_late.jpeg, al_champion.jpeg. tools/gbachar.py does the reduction -- do not
put pixel sizes in any prompt.

    al_early     -> graphics/trainers/front_pics/rival_early    64x64
    al_late      -> graphics/trainers/front_pics/rival_late     64x64
    al_champion  -> graphics/trainers/front_pics/champion_rival 64x64
    al_speech    -> graphics/oak_speech/rival/pic.png           64x96

==========================================================================
THE REFERENCE — al_early — GENERATED, keep it
==========================================================================
This is the one that came from text, and it is now the source for the other
three. Two things about it are load-bearing:

  * The BOX. This project has no Poke Balls; 3.1's line is USERBOX ->
    ADMINBOX -> SUPERBOX -> ROOTBOX, and tools/gbabox.py draws the same crate
    as the Index's caught marker -- dark rim, pale face, two bands. The model
    reaches for a ball unless told not to, twice.
  * The CLOTHES. Crystal's sprite worked because it kept the professor's
    purple shirt and extended hand; the swap read as the same shot of a
    different person. Olive-grey shirt, purple-mauve trousers, brown belt,
    brown boots -- keep them in all four.

The text that produced it, for regeneration only:

    Full-body character in the style of a Game Boy Advance RPG sprite. Flat
    cel shading, thick dark outline, no gradients, no anti-aliasing. Plain
    flat green background, no scenery, no shadow under the feet.

    Subject: an anthropomorphic mid-amber fox, a confident young man. Spiky
    orange-blond head fur. Dark olive-grey short-sleeved shirt, purple-mauve
    trousers, a belt. Upright ears, a narrow muzzle, a full brush tail.
    Ordinary human clothing that fits properly. He is cocky rather than
    cruel: a half smile, chin up, entirely sure of himself. Under about
    fifteen distinct colours.

    Pose: leaning forward, up on the balls of his feet, holding a small BOX
    out in front of him in one hand as though about to throw it.

    The BOX is a pale cream cube about the size of his palm, with a dark
    wooden rim around its edges and two darker bands running across its face.
    Flat-sided and square. It is a small crate. It is NOT a ball, NOT round,
    and it has no button on it.

    Full body, feet included, in a square frame.

==========================================================================
THE OTHER THREE — attach al_early, then paste one of these
==========================================================================
Every one of them opens the same way. ATTACH THE REFERENCE IMAGE FIRST.

The shared opening:

    Using the attached image as the character reference, draw the SAME fox
    character in a new pose. Identical face, fur colour, spiky orange-blond
    head fur, olive-grey short-sleeved shirt, purple-mauve trousers, brown
    belt, brown boots. Same flat cel shading, same thick dark outline, same
    plain flat green background, same limited palette.

    Keep him COMPACT in the frame: the tail curved close behind his legs
    rather than swept out sideways, arms inside the width of his shoulders
    where the pose allows. He should fill a square frame from head to feet
    without long horizontal reach.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
al_late — the later battles
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    New pose: standing tall and square to the viewer, one arm raised above
    his head with the hand open, empty. No box. Less eager than the
    reference and more certain -- he has stopped leaning in.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
al_champion — the last fight
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    New pose: mid-motion, one arm thrown forward toward the viewer, the
    other back for balance, leaning in. Empty hands, no box. The largest and
    most committed pose of the three.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
al_speech — the intro portrait (TALL frame, not square)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    New pose: standing at ease, turned slightly to his left, one hand resting
    on his hip and the other holding a bag tucked against his side. Weight on
    one leg. Calm, not mid-action.

    Draw this one in a TALL frame rather than a square one -- taller than it
    is wide, with him filling the height.
