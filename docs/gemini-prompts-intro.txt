======================================================================

square up and fight. Vanilla uses Nidorino and Gengar.

Ours is CODEMUSAI against CAREMUSAI -- Penphin's two hemispheres pulled apart
into two creatures, and 2's chart makes LOGIC fail against CONTEXT. The game
opens on its own central argument, with no dialogue, years before the player
can read it. The one that loses is the one everybody expects to win.

Three notes.

They face EACH OTHER. CODEMUSAI faces right, CAREMUSAI faces left. Getting one
of them backwards ruins the scene and is easy to miss.

The visor and the ear cups are the whole distinction between them at this size.
Everything else about the two robots is identical on purpose -- one framework,
many modules -- so those two features have to survive.

CODEMUSAI lunges and CAREMUSAI opens. That is the argument: one of them is
attacking and the other is not, and the chart says the second one wins.


======================================================================
THE SECOND SEAT  -  the CONTEXT edition's six frames
======================================================================

Everything above is the CONTENT edition. CONTEXT shows THE SAME EVENT FROM
THE OTHER SIDE OF IT.

The intro has two slots, and they are not interchangeable: the front slot is
48x48 and its creature faces RIGHT, the back slot is 56x56 and faces LEFT.
The front slot is the player's side. So swapping which creature sits in which
slot is exactly what "the other seat" means.

  CONTENT   front 48x48 CODEMUSAI (right)   back 56x56 CAREMUSAI (left)
  CONTEXT   front 48x48 CAREMUSAI (right)   back 56x56 CODEMUSAI (left)

THE POSES DO NOT SWAP WITH THE SEATS. Read that twice.

CODEMUSAI lunges and CAREMUSAI opens IN BOTH EDITIONS. One of them is
attacking and the other is not, and the chart says the second one wins. If the
poses travel with the slot instead of with the creature, CAREMUSAI ends up
lunging, CODEMUSAI ends up opening, and the argument silently inverts. That is
the one way to get this wrong, and it looks fine on screen.

So the six new frames are the six above, redrawn at the other size and the
other facing, with each creature keeping its own three poses:

  CAREMUSAI  3 frames at 48x48, facing RIGHT   -> gfx/intro/caremusai_r_1..3.png
             the same three: seated and waiting; risen, leaning back, arms
             open and low as if listening; standing tall and square with both
             arms raised wide. Unhurried throughout.

  CODEMUSAI  3 frames at 56x56, facing LEFT    -> gfx/intro/codemusai_l_1..3.png
             the same three: seated upright and composed; risen off the
             ground, leaning forward, both arms up in front; lunging with one
             arm thrown out straight ahead, body low and extended.

Take the CONTENT frames from the same conversation and ask for each again
mirrored, so the creature stays the same creature. Attach the matching frame
every time. The visor bar and the ear cups still have to survive - they are
the whole distinction between the two at this size.

Same four tones, same hard black outline, same plain white background, same
