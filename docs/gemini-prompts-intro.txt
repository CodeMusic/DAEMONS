GEMINI PROMPTS - the intro face-off
======================================================================

The scene between the CodeMusic splash and the title screen: two daemons
square up and fight. Vanilla uses Nidorino and Gengar.

Ours is CODEMUSAI against CAREMUSAI -- Penphin's two hemispheres pulled apart
into two creatures, and 2's chart makes LOGIC fail against CONTEXT. The game
opens on its own central argument, with no dialogue, years before the player
can read it. The one that loses is the one everybody expects to win.

SIX FRAMES. Attach the existing sprite to frame 1, and each frame to the next.
They must be the same creature throughout.

Three notes.

They face EACH OTHER. CODEMUSAI faces right, CAREMUSAI faces left. Getting one
of them backwards ruins the scene and is easy to miss.

The visor and the ear cups are the whole distinction between them at this size.
Everything else about the two robots is identical on purpose -- one framework,
many modules -- so those two features have to survive.

CODEMUSAI lunges and CAREMUSAI opens. That is the argument: one of them is
attacking and the other is not, and the chart says the second one wins.
