# The glasses {#glasses .t-CONTEXT}

A daemon you **SEND** out of your game lives on the companion: the app on your phone, the site at home, and any device
you give it. This book is about one of those devices, **the glasses**: LilyGO's **T-Glass**, a small screen held before
one eye. They carry your daemon where you can see it without looking down. They show the time and the next step, a
note you want in view, your daemon and how it is, and in the morning the dream it had.

The glasses show **one thing at a time**, and you choose it from the app. They have one button, no speaker and no
menus: the phone is their remote, and the glasses are its window.

## What you need {#need}

- **The glasses**, with the companion's own software on them. Glasses from the shop run LilyGO's; the companion's is
  put on them once, by their cable ([Putting the software on](#flashing)).
- **The companion's app on your phone.** The glasses hear it over Bluetooth, and through it reach the hub at home.
- **A daemon to carry.** In the game, choose **SEND** in a daemon's menu and save; then **SYNC** in the app.

# First steps {#first .t-SIGNAL}

## Charging {#charging}

The glasses charge by their USB-C cable. The battery's charge shows in the corner of **HOME**, and on the glasses' row
in the app's **PLACES**.

## Starting them {#starting}

Switch them on at the side. The first time they have no clock and no daemon yet, so the time reads *--:--*. Everything
the glasses know -- the time, your daemon, what to show -- comes from the companion, so linking them is next.

## Fitting the picture {#fitting}

The glasses throw their picture onto a prism, and different glasses throw it differently: it may come out turned,
mirrored, or too high. It is set **once**, by eye, with the glasses on and their cable in the computer that runs the
companion. Four settings, each kept on the glasses:

| setting | what it does |
|---|---|
| **TURN** | turns the picture a quarter at a time, until it stands upright |
| **MIRROR** | flips it, if words read backwards |
| **ROW** | moves the square along the screen, until it sits in the middle of what you see |
| **BRIGHT** | how bright it is, from dim to full |

The companion's wiki, *The T-Glass*, has the words to type for each.

## Pairing your phone {#pairing}

**Hold the button for three seconds.** The glasses buzz and show six numbers. In the app, pair the device; when iOS
asks, type the numbers. *Paired.* -- the glasses now talk to your phone whenever it is near, at home or away. A tap
gives up; after two minutes they give up by themselves.

The glasses can also be linked **by their cable**, at the computer that runs the hub, with the companion's link
script. They learn where the hub is, its key and the time, as any device does.

::: note
**Bluetooth, not Wi-Fi.** The glasses can join your Wi-Fi as any device can, but Wi-Fi is hungry and their battery is
small: an hour or two of it would empty them. Through your phone, and dark between glances, they last much longer.
:::

## Giving them a daemon {#daemon}

SEND a daemon in the game and SYNC in the app, then choose it for the glasses in **PLACES**, as for any device. It
appears on **HOME**, and on **DAEMON**.

# What they show {#show .t-FLOW}

{{diagram screens | The four screens, as the square looks through the prism. Black is the lens: everything else is light on it.}}

## HOME {#home}

The time, large; the battery in the corner; your daemon standing at the foot; and beside it **today's next step**, the
one thing the companion has for you now. In the morning, if your daemon has dreamt, the step gives way to *A dream is
waiting. Tap.*

## A NOTE {#note}

One of your notes. Its **first line is its title**, across the top; under it, the rest, five lines a page. A tap turns
the page, and after the last it starts again. The page number sits in the corner.

## DAEMON {#daemon-screen}

Your daemon, large, with its name and **how it is in a word** -- the same word the app shows on its card. It looks as
it has been cared for: well rested, hungry, glad, sleepy.

## DARK {#dark}

Nothing. The screen turns down and the lens is clear, until you want it again. Two taps take you there; one tap brings
you back HOME.

## Over whatever is showing {#over}

Two things lie over the screen for a while, then leave it as it was:

- **A care's reaction.** Tend your daemon from the app, and the glasses show it with what it says, for eight seconds.
  A note you were reading stays where it was.
- **The morning's dream.** When your daemon has dreamt, the glasses wake with it: *WHILE YOU SLEPT*, then the dream,
  five lines a page. A tap turns the page; past the last, *good morning* puts it away.

**Something new sent buzzes the glasses once**, so you know to look.

# The button {#button .t-LOGIC}

The glasses have one button, on the side, worked by touch, and a second, **BOOT**, which does what a tap does.

| | |
|---|---|
| **a tap** | turns a note's or a dream's page; on DARK, goes HOME; on HOME with a dream waiting, opens it |
| **two taps** | DARK |
| **a hold** | HOME: it buzzes when it goes |
| **held on, three seconds** | shows the numbers that pair your phone |

Asleep, the glasses' first tap only wakes them. Left alone they sleep, as every device does; how soon is set in the
app, on the glasses' own settings.

# GLASSES, in the app {#app .t-SWARM}

In the app's **PLACES**, once the companion has heard from the glasses, a card called **GLASSES** chooses what they
show.

## What the glasses should show {#preview}

At the top, a picture of the glasses' square, drawn from what the glasses are told. It is what they *should* be
showing: if the glasses are out of reach, they will show it when they are back.

## SHOW {#choose}

**HOME**, **DAEMON** or **DARK**: tap one, and the glasses change.

## CARE {#care}

**Drink**, **Feed** and **Train**, as on your daemon's own card: your daemon is tended, says what it thinks of it here,
and the glasses show its reaction for a few seconds before going back to what they showed.

## NOTES {#notes}

**NEW NOTE** opens a page whose **first line, tinted, is the title**. Type the title, press Return, and write the rest.
**Save** keeps it.

**Tap a note to send it to the glasses.** Its row is marked *ON THE GLASSES* and stays selected for as long as the
glasses show it -- leave the app, come back tomorrow, it is still selected. Send anything else, from the app or with
the glasses' own button, and it is not.

**Edit** changes a note; to rename it, change its first line. If the glasses are showing it, they take the new words at
once. **Remove** asks for a second tap.

::: note
**Notes for the glasses are short by nature**: a list, a few lines for a talk, a code you keep forgetting. The glasses
take the first nine hundred letters of a note; the app keeps the whole.
:::

# Looking after the glasses {#care-of .t-HARDENED}

## The battery {#battery}

The glasses' battery is small, and LilyGO does not say how small, so this book makes no promise of hours. To make it
last:

- **Stay on Bluetooth**, through your phone. Wi-Fi spends it quickly.
- **Go DARK** between glances: two taps.
- A shorter time before they sleep, in the app's settings for the glasses.

## Putting the software on {#flashing}

The glasses' software is put on, and brought up to date, by their cable, from the computer that runs the companion:
the companion's **update script**, told which device it is. The companion's wiki, *Flashing and linking*, has every
step. If the computer cannot reach them, hold **BOOT** while plugging the cable in.

## When something is wrong {#trouble}

| it says, or does | try |
|---|---|
| **The picture is turned, mirrored, or cut off** | fit it by eye ([Fitting the picture](#fitting)) |
| **The time reads *--:--*** | the glasses have not heard from the companion yet: pair your phone, or link them by their cable |
| **Tapping a note in the app changes nothing** | the glasses hear the app through your phone: is it paired, near, and the app opened lately? |
| ***No daemon here yet*** | SEND a daemon in the game, SYNC in the app, and choose it for the glasses in PLACES |
| **The button does nothing, or presses itself** | the button is a touch; the companion's wiki, *The T-Glass*, has how to read what it feels |
| **The screen stays dark** | two taps went DARK: tap once. Or they are asleep: tap to wake them |

## Still to come {#coming}

- **A nod** as a second button, and a tilt to turn a page -- the glasses feel their own movement, and will.
- **Cue cards**: a note as a prompter, a page a tap.
- **A voice note**, said to the glasses' microphone.
