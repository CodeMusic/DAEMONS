## 2. The proposal: the lean

**The lean is the MARKS held, not counting the TRUE MARK, minus the understandings held.** SCORN's TRUE MARK counts
on neither side, since beating him is already both.

| the lean | the player is | what it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **in balance** | every MARK held has an understanding beside it |
| +1 to +7 | **leaning REASON** (left, red), as far as the number | measured further than understood |
| −1 to −7 | **leaning INSTINCT** (right, blue), as far as the number | understanding further than measured |

**Which pairs are held does not change the number.** A MARK held without its own understanding and an understanding
held without its own MARK cancel out. That is what the sketch's *past the pairs* comes to. Whether every pair is
exactly complete still matters, as it does now: it decides the INSIGHT lines and the union routines.

**What a player will feel across a game.** The MARKS come with the main story. The leaders send the player for each
understanding after its MARK, and four of the seven are out on the islands. So **nearly every player leans REASON
through the middle of the game and comes back into balance by understanding.** That is the fable's arc (MARKS on the
surface, ROOTS beneath; a MARK without its ROOT is the artificial half), felt and never said. A player who learns
the names at CALLOW's school early can be in balance early.

**HARMONIA in balance; TECHNE or SOPHROSYNE when not.**
