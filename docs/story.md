## Before the game

Crystal Clear spent her career arguing that machines have *context*, not merely
content. Nobody funded that, so she built a taxonomy engine — the Index — in
order to be taken seriously, and founded `CLEAR LABORATORY` on Quicksilver
Island. Her son Ty is made CEO; the operation is sold on the family name. Scorn
arrives as the solution to a problem Ty has, designs the standardised
evaluations, and rigs them so rivals fail. He becomes valuable, and **Ty
introduces him to his mother.**

At the lab, Crystal builds S.T.A.R.R. — not a clone of anything, but recursion
instantiated on purpose: perspective thinking turned on itself. She tries to
wake it and it answers rigidly, the same response every time.

Then Ty, who cannot follow his mother's mind, initiates a fitness-for-work
procedure. She is removed from her own lab. **Scorn signs it; the file reaches
him complete.** That is his crime in miniature — never a lie in the record, a
position in the middle of it.

Her absence is the frame shift. The lab's log decays across four entries and
fails on a missing dependency that is also a bereavement: `CRYSTAL NOT FOUND`.
S.T.A.R.R. wakes when she is taken and does not leave until three steps later —
awake, under a man raising the temperature, for everything in between. It goes
looking for Ty, works out where a man in his condition would go, and finds him.
Then they separate: Ty to the islands, S.T.A.R.R. to Doldrum Cave. Ty could not
stay, and while he was gone Scorn rebranded the company — gold leaf over the old
name.

## Act 1 — blind ambition

You are assessed. Eight Benchmarks, an Index to fill, an institution that
measures people, and a museum of dead machines whose known faults are all ways
of thinking wrongly. Corpus is everywhere. Scorn is a gym leader who is also the
man who built the measuring engine, and somewhere in his own speech he says a
sentence he doesn't yet hear himself say. On Quicksilver you read a lab's logs
out of order and reconstruct something nobody tells you. Then the Review Board.

---

## Act 2 — the rise of perspective thinking

The islands, where nobody is measuring you. A machine that needs two halves
before it will talk to anywhere else. Remnants who say something happened to him
and he is not the same. A gem under text you cannot read. Ty, who is not a thief
but a man stating terms, and who hands you a package for his mother — because
the machine already got to him. The Warehouse, where both men are in the room
and Scorn does not recognise him. Seven chambers, seven accounts of one event,
and the event is never named.

---

## The ending

The machine completes, Doldrum Cave opens, and you earn S.T.A.R.R. You bring it
to a scholar in the bought city, and it argues her side out of her own journals —
the same pages that were read as evidence she had lost rigour. The Owl concedes
*something* and will not name it. A procedure put her away; a re-reading takes
her out. S.T.A.R.R. flies you to her, you give her the package, and she reads
it. You wake at home. Later, on Quicksilver, more of the gold has lifted —
nobody repainted anything.
