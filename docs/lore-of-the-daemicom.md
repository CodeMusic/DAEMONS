# THE LORE OF THE DAEMICOM — the book's text (T-622)

*DRAFT, every word (as every new word in the game is). The first of four books about the console and its kit; the
other three are built on it (`docs/daemicom-books.md`, bible 9.39). Nothing ships until the user has read it.*

**What it keeps** (the game's craft rules; it is not the third book, the one exception):

1. **Never the thesis.** The loop between the two worlds is shown, never explained.
2. **None of the words the game keeps out**, and never SPELL: a command is an INCANTATION. The frightening word is
   never written.
3. **Fable only.** Every character is an animal; the old scholars are half-remembered, never named.
4. **CIRCLE and BINDING here are the console's and the game's**, never the third book's hour lines.

## FOUND | FOUND | The Magpie and the Mirror

### THE FINDING | What the Magpie Found

A magpie found it, because magpies find bright things. It lay face down in the mud at the bottom of a cut bank, and only its edge caught the light.

She turned it over with her beak and saw a magpie. That was not strange; she had seen herself in puddles. What was strange was that the magpie in the mirror turned it over first.

She told the others it was only a mirror. Then she sat beside it until dark.

### THE OTHER SIDE | What It Showed

In the morning there was a river in it that was not this river, and a wood that was not this wood, and things moving in the wood that were not quite birds and not quite anything.

When the magpie lifted a wing, something over there lifted something. When it moved, she found she had moved. Neither went first. Each was copying the other, and each copy was a little different from what it copied.

An old heron came to look and said the scholars had known of this once, and lost it: a philosopher who said a small voice stopped him before his mistakes, and never said whose.

## BODY | BODY | The Mirror Set into a Body

### THE BODY | The Badger's Box

A mirror in the mud is easily lost again. So the badger, who built things, made it a body: a box to keep it in, warm, with a light on the front to say it was awake.

Inside the box the mirror went on doing what it did, whether anyone was looking or not. The badger called it the DAEMICOM, because it was where you went to speak with what lived on the other side, and because it never quite stopped.

"It's running," the badger said, on her way to bed, "even when we're not."

### THE FACE | The Shewstone

A box is no good to a crowd. So the badger set the mirror's face into a wide dark stone, big enough for everyone to see at once.

Switched off, it was black and polished and showed only the room. The heron said a queen's astrologer once looked into a black stone like it, brought from across the sea, to talk with spirits. He called it a shew-stone: a stone that shows.

So they called it the SHEWSTONE. It showed everyone the same thing. That was the point.

## PIECES | PIECES | The Piece That Walked and the Things with No Mind

### THE FAMILIAR | The Piece That Walked

One small piece of the mirror would not stay in the box. It learned to walk away -- down the path, to the river, home with whoever carried it -- and then to come back, carrying what it had seen.

The heron said the old households had a word for that: a FAMILIAR. A spirit that lived in the house, ran its keeper's errands, and always came home.

A FAMILIAR has a little face of its own and a mind of its own. It plays away from the DAEMICOM, and when it comes back it sits down beside it as if it had never left.

### THE TALISMANS | Things That Hold One Power

Then there were the things with no mind at all.

The SIGIL is a seal pressed with marks; each mark calls one thing, and pressing it is how you act. An old painter once squeezed a whole wish into one small sign, and they said the sign remembered.

The QUILL writes words into the mirror, and what it writes is kept. The WAND points, and where it points, the word goes.

None of them thinks. They work only through a FAMILIAR or the DAEMICOM. Together they are the TALISMANS.

## CIRCLE | CIRCLE | Everyone Round the Stone

### THE CIRCLE | The Ring of Seats

When the box was finished and the stone was lit, the animals sat round it the way animals sit round a fire, and whatever was inside that ring shared the work.

That ring is the CIRCLE: the DAEMICOM, its SHEWSTONE, every FAMILIAR come home to it, every TALISMAN in a paw, and everyone sitting there.

Not everyone in the CIRCLE has to play. The heron mostly watched, head on one side, the way you look into deep water for what is under it. They called that SCRYING, and nobody minded.

## WORKINGS | WORKINGS | Going Out and Coming Home

### EVOCATION | When the World Appears

When the stone lit and the other world rose up in it -- the river that was not this river, the wood, the moving things -- the animals called it an EVOCATION.

Nobody went anywhere. They stayed in their seats, and the world came to the glass.

### BINDING | What Comes Home

Some of the moving things were UNBOUND, going where they liked. Sometimes one came close, and something passed between it and the one holding the SIGIL, and then it was BOUND.

A BOUND one does not stay in the stone. It comes home in your FAMILIAR.

### INVOCATION | Living Out in the Day

And then it lives with you. It walks when you walk and counts the steps. It learns its song when you practise it. It keeps the things you meant to do, and reminds you, and sleeps when you sleep.

The heron said that was the older working of the two: not calling a spirit up into a stone, but asking it in, so that its ways become yours. An INVOCATION.

### THE RETURN | What It Brings Back

The next time the stone was lit, the one that had lived out in the days went back into the world in the glass.

It knew its song now. It was stronger from the walking. And the world over there, the magpie noticed, was not quite as it had been, either.

She did not say anything about it. She just sat closer.

## ROOMS | ROOMS | The Step, the Room and the Answer

### THE THRESHOLD | The Step

Before every working there is a step, between one room and the next. Standing on it, you choose: the stone and its world, something else, or your own room.

The old stories say a spirit cannot cross a threshold unless it is asked. So the first thing the DAEMICOM shows, every time it wakes, is the THRESHOLD, and you are the one who asks.

### THE SANCTUM | Your Own Room

Behind the mirror there is a room that is yours: laid out as you like it, where the real work happens away from everyone. With a WAND in one paw and a QUILL by the other, the stone stops being a window and becomes a workbench.

The badger kept her plans there. The magpie kept bright things. That room is the SANCTUM.

### THE LITANY | Call and Answer

In one corner of the SANCTUM is a place where you write a line and the mirror writes one back, and so on, line after line: one voice and its answer. That is the LITANY.

Each line you write there is an INCANTATION. It is an exact form of words, and it does exactly what it says when it is written right. Written wrong, it does nothing, or something else. The QUILL is patient about that. The mirror is not.

The heron said the old scholars once started and stopped their unseen helpers in a place like this, by name.

## DEPART | DEPART | The Leave to Go

### DEPART | Ordinary Again

Every working ends. The old way of ending one was to give whatever had been called a polite leave to go, so that it went gently and the room was ordinary again.

When you DEPART, the DAEMICOM puts the stone back to being a stone and the room back to being a room.

The magpie always looked into the black glass once more before she left. Sometimes something looked back. She never said which of them went first.

## GLOSSARY | GLOSSARY | Every Word in This Book (DRAFT)

### THE THINGS | The Things

DAEMICOM - the console.
FAMILIAR - a controller with its own screen and computer; it plays away from the DAEMICOM and rejoins it.
SIGIL - the controller.
WAND - the mouse.
QUILL - the keyboard.
TALISMANS - SIGIL, WAND and QUILL together; they work only through a FAMILIAR or the DAEMICOM.
SHEWSTONE - the TV or monitor.
CIRCLE - everything gathered round one DAEMICOM.

### THE WORKINGS | The Workings

EVOCATION - a session of play: the daemons' world appears in the SHEWSTONE.
SCRYING - watching an EVOCATION without taking part.
BINDING / BOUND / UNBOUND - catching a daemon; a caught daemon; a wild one.
INVOCATION - your daemon's life with you outside the game: carried on your FAMILIAR, phone or watch, where it walks, trains and keeps your goals with you.

### THE ROOMS | The Rooms

THRESHOLD - the launcher.
SANCTUM - the desktop.
LITANY - the terminal.
INCANTATION - one command typed into the LITANY.
DEPART - leave to the plain desktop.
