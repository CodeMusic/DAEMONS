# THE BLANK TABLET -- the words (T-547, DRAFT)

*Every word here is DRAFT. Decided by the user, 2026-11-12, by multiple choice: the stone stands in BLANCHE by the path;
each half of the third book reads one face (the night half the front, the dawn half the back; mended, both); each face
shows its thirteenth line in its own hour; the witch is an old toad. Design: vision 4.39 (the Blank Tablet) and 4.40.*

**How it reads.** A spell's saying appears on a face the first time that spell is cast (VAR_SPELLS_CAST), and its voice
is the player's mind lean in the never-shown ledger: reason (declarative, numbered, exact) or intuition (felt, unnumbered,
open). The voice can change as the ledger moves, so a line is *added or reshaped*. The front speaks in the night half's
terms (what the spell cost or took), the back in the dawn half's (the same thing, given). Twelve sayings for spells 1-12;
THE PULL, THE WITCHING HOUR and THE HORIZON add none.

**The thirteenth line** is `{PLAYER}` and then one saying for the pole leaned on hardest of all four. The front's shows
only in the witching half-hour (3:30-3:59), the back's only in THE CREATOR'S HOUR (4:24-5:12); each is shown until it
has been read once, then never again (DRAFT detail: the brief's "one window ever" would almost never be seen).
