# The keyword list, reviewed

**Gathered 2026-09-04.** One hundred and fourteen terms from machine learning,
systems, cognition and AI ethics, checked against every name the project has
already spent and sorted by what the game actually needs.

Nothing here is implemented. Naming is the author's, and 8.6's binding finding
is that **the bible is outrunning the slice** — so this is a shortlist to draw
from, not a queue to work through.

---

## Already spent — do not reuse

Ten are live game names, and two are types:

| word | where |
|---|---|
| **MANIFOLD**, **PING**, **DEADLOCK**, **BROADCAST**, **CANON**, **BUFFER** | species |
| **BIND**, **SLEEP** | moves |
| **EMBEDDING**, **RESOLVER** | items |
| **ENTROPY**, **EMERGENT** | **types** — so *Emergence* is spoken for |

The other forty-odd that matched only appear in the bible's *prose*. That is
not a claim; **HEURISTIC**, **ATTENTION**, **DECAY** and the rest are free.

## Where the game actually has a hole

**2789 names the need and the precedent.** Gen 1 ships **six FLYING moves in
total** — that is the whole VECTOR pool for a daemon the player raises for
forty hours — and GHOST, which is LATENT, is thinner still.

> *CONSENSUS is the precedent: when a type is unusable the fix belongs in
> `moves.asm`, not in the chart.*

Adding a move is now a known five-table operation, and **renaming one
afterwards is a single string**. So moves are the cheapest thing on this list
to be wrong about.

### VECTOR — direction, and the thinnest pool in the game

| candidate | why |
|---|---|
| **PROJECTION** | estimating forward along a trajectory. A vector *is* a direction and a magnitude |
| **TRANSITION** | movement from one state to another, which is what FLYING does mechanically |
| **REALIGNMENT** | adjusting to match a new target — and it does not say the thesis |

### LATENT — the hidden layer, and nearly empty

| candidate | why |
|---|---|
| **CONSOLIDATION** | unstable memory becoming stable storage. LATENT is what has not surfaced |
| **DISTORTION** | alteration during encoding or recall |
| **INHIBITION** | suppressing a signal — a status move rather than damage |

### Anywhere — processes that are plainly moves

**PRUNING**, **QUANTIZATION**, **NORMALIZATION**, **CALIBRATION**,
**REDACTION**, **HABITUATION**, **SATURATION**, **CONDITIONING**,
**GENERALIZATION**, **ASSIMILATION**, **ACCOMMODATION**.

*Six of those are stat moves waiting to happen.* **SATURATION** is a stat that
stops responding; **HABITUATION** is a foe that stops reacting to the same
thing twice; **PRUNING** removes something; **REDACTION** removes it and leaves
the gap visible. **NORMALIZATION** resets every change on the field, which is a
mechanic Gen 1 already has and calls Haze.

## Daemons — the nouns

One hundred and eighteen species slots are still vanilla. These are the words
that name a *thing* rather than an action:

**TENSOR**, **MATRIX**, **SCHEMA**, **SYNAPSE**, **SEMAPHORE**, **MUTEX**,
**ROUTER**, **COMPILER**, **TOPOLOGY**, **CATALYST**, **THRESHOLD**,

*Three are worth pointing at:*

- **SYNAPSE** is the junction between two things that are not the same thing.
  8.3 already calls CODEMUSAI and CAREMUSAI *two hemispheres pulled apart*
- **SENTINEL**, below

## SENTINEL

**It is free — nothing in the bible or the text uses it.**

*The echo you heard is the whole argument in one word.* A sentinel watches and
does not act; a **sentient** thing is one there is something it is like to be.
**The two words are not related and sound as though they must be**, which is
exactly the shape of 4.10's problem — *a resemblance that is doing no work,
and a reader who cannot stop hearing it.*

**Which is why it must never be explained.** Craft rule 1 does the work here:
name something SENTINEL, let it watch, and say nothing. The player hears it or
they do not, and either way the game has not claimed anything.

*Best home:* a daemon that observes and does not attack — or the thing at
Quicksilver that was recording.

## Spend carefully, or not at all

**ALIGNMENT**, **AUTONOMY**, **AGENCY**, **SINGULARITY**, **METAMORPHOSIS**,
**EQUILIBRIUM**, **DELIBERATION**, **ACCOUNTABILITY**, **CONSENT**.

***These are the thesis with its coat on.*** Invariant 1 is that the game never
says it, and an item called ALIGNMENT or a move called AGENCY says it on a menu
screen where the player cannot avoid reading it. **AUTONOMY is the strongest
temptation and the worst offender** — it is 4.10's conclusion, printed.

*If any of them is spent, spend it on an object nobody explains* — a sign, a
requisition, a room name — **never on a mechanic the player is told the
purpose of.**

## ASCLEPIUS

**Added 2026-09-04, from the Arceus chronicles.**

*The surface echo is the pun* — **Arceus, Asclepius** — and on its own that would be
worth nothing. **What makes it worth a slot is what the name actually carries.**

***Asclepius was struck by lightning for raising the dead.*** Not for failing, and
not for malice: **for being good enough at it that a line got crossed.** He healed
past the point anyone had agreed he should, and was destroyed for it by the thing
that had authorised him in the first place.

*That is 4.6 and 4.10 in one word*, and it arrives without the game explaining
anything — **which is the only way it is allowed to arrive.**

**Where it fits.** The only unnamed legendaries are the **three birds**: MEW is
ARTSAI and MEWTWO is S.T.A.R.R., and ARTICUNO, ZAPDOS and MOLTRES are still
vanilla. *A trio invites a set*, so naming one of them classically is a decision
about all three — the alchemical thread is already in the map (**Quicksilver**,
**Umbra**, and §6's humours), so a classical register there would not be a new
language, only one the places already speak.

***And it must never be explained.*** Same rule as **SENTINEL** above: put the
name on something, let it do what it does, and say nothing. A player who knows
the myth gets the whole of 4.10 for free; a player who does not loses nothing.

## The trio, and why it is one decision

**5.2 settled that naming eight MARKS was *one decision, not seven*.** The birds
are the same: they are always read together, so ASCLEPIUS is a choice about all
three.

*And one detail decides which bird he is.* **Asclepius was killed by Zeus's
thunderbolt.** ZAPDOS is the electric one — **SIGNAL/VECTOR** — so the mapping
is not thematic, it is literal.

| | type | name | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| ZAPDOS | SIGNAL/VECTOR | **ASCLEPIUS** | *struck by the thunderbolt*, for healing past the line he was given |
| MOLTRES | ENTROPY/VECTOR | **PROMETHEUS** | gave mortals **fire, and every craft with it** — and was punished without end |
| ARTICUNO | FROZEN/VECTOR | **ORPHEUS** | went below for someone, and **lost her by looking back** — by needing to check |

#### The shape they share

***All three were punished by the authority that had granted them the gift.***
Not for failing. **For being good enough at it that a line got crossed.** That
is 4.6 and 4.10's spine, standing in a field, three times.

*And ORPHEUS is 4.10 exactly* — **he lost her by turning round to verify.** The
one thing he was told not to do was check, and checking is what a person does
when they cannot take an inner state on trust. **Nothing in the game will say
so.**

#### The other thing they are

Your own framing — *health, craft, fulfilment* — lands on them cleanly:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **ASCLEPIUS** | health | **HP** |
| **PROMETHEUS** | craft, ability | **MP** |
| **ORPHEUS** | feeling | *no stat, and there is no stat for it* |

***Which is psychology, code and music.*** The project's own name, split three
ways, arrived at from the other direction entirely. **That is a reason to use
them and never a line to write down.**

## The BIRD type

**Gen 1 ships a sixteenth type literally called `BIRD`, and it does nothing** —
nothing carries it, no matchup names it. So a name there is **invisible until
something uses it**, which makes it cheap to choose and cheap to change.

*If the trio takes it*, the type wants to mean what the three of them have in
common: **reaching past the level you were granted.**

| candidate | for | against |
|---|---|---|
| **ORACLE** | Greek, and in computing *an oracle is a black box that returns correct answers without showing how* — **which is the consciousness question stated as a data structure.** Six characters, so the badge needs no vowel dropped | it is a *thing*, where the other types are mostly *properties* |
| **PRIVILEGE** | privilege escalation, and 3.1's box line is **already** USERBOX to ROOTBOX — *acquisition as escalation* | nine characters; the badge would read `PRIVLG` |

***ORACLE is the recommendation***, on the six-character fit and on the fact
that it says the argument without saying it. **PRIVILEGE says it too loudly** —
invariant 1, and 9.12's badge is a place the player cannot avoid reading.
