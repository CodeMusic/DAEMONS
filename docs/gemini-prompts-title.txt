GEMINI PROMPTS - the front door
======================================================================

Two scenes the player sees before the world starts: the CODEMUSIC presents
scene, and the title face-off. vision.md 9.14 designs both.

WHAT GEMINI IS FOR, AND WHAT IT IS NOT

Gemini draws the two things that need an ARTIST: the face-off figures and the
CODEMUSIC wordmark. It does NOT draw the particles.

A musical note at sixteen pixels square is nine lit pixels, and no image model
places nine pixels. The particles, the 0 and the 1, and the bloom frames are
drawn procedurally by tools/gbatitle.py, the way the MARKS and the Index box
were - pixel-exact, in the palette, first time. Do not prompt for them.

THE ONE RULE, AGAIN

Colour is by TYPE (9.4), and 9.14 sanctions its third and last spend: colour
is what arrives when two things that disagree connect. CODEMUSAI is LOGIC and
its hue is COLD STEEL BLUE. CAREMUSAI is CONTEXT and its hue is MAGENTA.
These are the same hues their battle sprites were assigned. Do not change them
for the title - the title is where the player learns them.

POSE IS NOT SIDE

2.4, and it survives into 9.14: CODEMUSAI LUNGES, CAREMUSAI OPENS. One of
them is attacking and the other is not, and that IS the chart. The editions
swap which side each stands on, and the engine does that with a hardware flip,
so each creature is drawn ONCE, facing right. Never redraw one "facing the
other way" - the flip is free and the pose must not change with the side.

HOW TO USE THIS FILE

  1. Attach the reference named in the block.
  2. Paste the prompt.
  3. Save what comes back anywhere, then convert:

         python3 tools/gbatitle.py <saved>.png <asset>

  4. Then: make -C engineGba firered

DO THE TWO FIGURES IN ONE CONVERSATION, CODEMUSAI FIRST. They have to look
like they belong on the same screen, and a fresh conversation will not match
the light or the line weight of the first.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
CODEMUSAI - title figure
  hue     LOGIC (cold steel blue)
  attach  gfx/front/codemusai.png
  target  64x64 sprite, 15 colours + transparent
  convert python3 tools/gbatitle.py <saved>.png codemusai
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The attached image is our creature CODEMUSAI. Redraw it at higher fidelity in
full colour, in three-quarter view facing RIGHT, mid-LUNGE - weight forward,
committed to a movement it has already begun, reaching toward something off
the right edge. It is not angry and it is not a monster; it is certain. Keep
the same silhouette and the same readable features so it is unmistakably the
same creature. Build the colour scheme around cold steel blue as the dominant
colour. Draw it as a flat illustration with a hard pure black (#000000)
outline around the whole creature. Use AT MOST 15 flat colours plus a pure
white background, with hard edges between them and no gradients, no
anti-aliasing, no glow, no texture and no shading ramps longer than three
steps. The outline must stay pure black - it must not take the body colour.
Plain pure-white background, nothing behind the creature, no ground shadow.
Whole creature visible with a small even margin, filling the frame.

the reference so it is unmistakably the same creature. Build the colour scheme
around magenta as the dominant colour. Draw it as a flat illustration with a
hard pure black (#000000) outline around the whole creature. Use AT MOST 15
flat colours plus a pure white background, with hard edges between them and no
gradients, no anti-aliasing, no glow, no texture and no shading ramps longer
than three steps. The outline must stay pure black - it must not take the body
colour. Plain pure-white background, nothing behind the creature, no ground

CODEMUSIC - the wordmark

  target  144x16, ONE BIT - black on white, no grey at all
  convert python3 tools/gbatitle.py <saved>.png wordmark

The attached image is the CODEMUSIC wordmark from our Game Boy build, at
seventy-two pixels wide. Redraw the same word at higher fidelity for a wider
strip, keeping its letterforms recognisable: same geometric capitals, same
even weight, same proportions. Black on a pure white background. Wide and short: the whole word fits a strip nine times as wide as
it is tall. Clean geometric capitals with even stroke weight, no serifs, no
outline, no shadow, no gradient, no texture, no background shapes and no
decoration of any kind. PURE BLACK AND PURE WHITE ONLY - not one grey pixel,
no anti-aliasing on any edge. Letters evenly spaced, sitting on one baseline,
none touching. Nothing else in the image.

THE TITLE LOGO - CONTENT and CONTEXT
  attach  engine/gfx/title/pokemon_logo.png   (our Game Boy DAEMONS, 128x56)
          plus engine/gfx/title/red_version.png and blue_version.png
  target  256x64, 8bpp so colour is not the constraint here
  convert python3 tools/gbatitle.py <saved>.png logo-content
          python3 tools/gbatitle.py <saved>.png logo-context
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The attached images are our Game Boy title logo -- the word DAEMONS at
128x56 -- and the two edition words. Redraw DAEMONS at higher fidelity and
larger, KEEPING ITS LETTERFORMS: the same heavy squared capitals, the same
proportions, the same hard drop shadow to the lower right. Put the edition
word CONTENT beneath it [then a second image, identical treatment, reading
CONTEXT]. Pure white background. DAEMONS is the large word; the edition word is
smaller and sits under it. Heavy geometric capitals with a hard pure black
outline, flat fills, no gradients, no glow, no bevel, no texture and no
anti-aliasing. The two images must be identical in every respect except the
edition word, because the player will see one and hear about the other. Wide
and short - twice as wide as it is tall. Plain pure-white background, nothing
behind the letters.

WHAT COMES BACK, AND WHAT HAPPENS TO IT

Save at whatever size Gemini gives you - big is good, the downsampler wants
the detail. tools/gbatitle.py crops to the content, scales to the target,
quantises to the palette and writes the indexed PNG the build needs.

It will REFUSE work rather than guess: more than 15 colours after quantising,
a grey pixel in the one-bit wordmark, or an outline that is not pure black are
all reported instead of silently rounded. That is the same discipline as
3118 - rgbgfx rejects anti-aliasing, and finding out at build time is worse
than finding out here.
