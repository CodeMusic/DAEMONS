# THE DAEMICOM's four books -- the prompts (T-622 to T-625)

*The user's own prompts, 2026-11-24, as their ADDENDUM left them, with the four open points answered by multiple
choice the same day (every recommendation): **SHEWSTONE**, not PORTAL; a linked handheld acts as a SANCTUM's **WAND
and QUILL**; a QUILL and a WAND give **a full computer on the SHEWSTONE: the SANCTUM**; and the INVOCATION line as
written. The words are bible 9.39. Every word is DRAFT. The lore book's text is `docs/lore-of-the-daemicom.md`.*

**Shared by all four**: DAEMONS is said DAY-mons and the frightening word is never written; every character is an
animal; never SPELL or SPELLCRAFT (the third book's, named in public only as rumour); CIRCLE and BINDING are kept apart
from the third book's hour lines; never say the thesis -- the loop between the worlds is shown, never explained; each
book reaches the GBA's book reader and the app's book pages, and has its own voice chosen with the user from samples.

## 1. THE LORE OF THE DAEMICOM (T-622) -- the foundation

What was found was not a machine but a MIRROR. The daemons' world is a reflection of ours, and ours of theirs. Light
passing back and forth between the two -- each copying the other, each copy changing what it copies -- slowly settled
into hardware: a console you can commune through. That is why the parts of a modern device turned up with old magical
names: they are what the reflection made them on the other side. Told as a story: who found the mirror, what it
showed, how each piece came out of it. Lineage: THE PAINTED MIRROR.

How each piece came: the DAEMICOM, the mirror set into a body; the SHEWSTONE, the mirror's face, which shows everyone
the same thing; the FAMILIAR, a piece of the mirror that learned to walk away and come back; the TALISMANS, things
without minds that carry one power each (the SIGIL calls, the QUILL captures words, the WAND channels them); the
CIRCLE, everything gathered round one DAEMICOM; the THRESHOLD, the step before every working; the SANCTUM, your own
workroom behind the mirror; the LITANY, the call and response where words are spoken exactly.

The two workings and the loop: EVOCATION (the world appears in the SHEWSTONE; you stay in your seat), BINDING (a
daemon is bound -- wild ones are UNBOUND -- and comes home in your FAMILIAR), INVOCATION (the daemon lives in your
days), what it learns going back into the next EVOCATION, and DEPART (the licence to depart). The real history of each
word -- Socrates' daimonion, Dee's shewstone, Spare's sigils, the familiar of the household, the licence to depart --
as things this world's scholars half-remembered. It ends with the glossary (9.39's table).

## 2. THE DAEMICOM USER MANUAL (T-623)

Each chapter opens with a short passage of the lore, then teaches plainly; the instructions are exact and testable.
Setting up (plug the DAEMICOM into a SHEWSTONE); the THRESHOLD (choose an EVOCATION, an app, or the SANCTUM); an
EVOCATION with two screens (the game on the SHEWSTONE, the companion and the save's tabs on a FAMILIAR); SCRYING; the
SANCTUM (the game moves to your FAMILIAR and the SHEWSTONE becomes a full computer, worked with a WAND and a QUILL);
the LITANY and its INCANTATIONS; getting back (the button combination that leaves the SANCTUM if you are stuck, and
DEPART); joining the CIRCLE (linking FAMILIARS and TALISMANS); **linked in the app: the game's own menu shows which
DAEMICOM or FAMILIAR is in its SANCTUM, and lets you choose one to act as its WAND and QUILL**; care (power, storage,
saves).

## 3. THE BOOK OF FAMILIARS (T-624)

A FAMILIAR is a piece of the mirror that learned to walk away and come back. What it is (a controller with its own
screen and computer); in an EVOCATION, the game's second screen; where a BOUND daemon comes home after a BINDING;
INVOCATION (walking, training its song, keeping your goals); walking away and returning, told as the familiar coming
home; on its own, plugged into any SHEWSTONE, with its own THRESHOLD, SANCTUM, LITANY and DEPART; SCRYING from a
FAMILIAR; more players by SIGILS plugged into it; several FAMILIARS in one CIRCLE acting as one.

## 4. THE BOOK OF TALISMANS (T-625)

Things without minds that carry one power each, working only through a FAMILIAR or the DAEMICOM. Its theme is the
power of language: the SIGIL calls (each button a mark of intent; Spare's wish in a glyph), how you act in an
EVOCATION; the QUILL captures words (in the LITANY it writes INCANTATIONS, which work only when written exactly); the
WAND channels them (in the SANCTUM it arranges and opens). **Together, a QUILL and a WAND with a DAEMICOM or a
FAMILIAR give you a full computer on the SHEWSTONE: the SANCTUM.** When words are both captured and directed, the
mirror answers -- the process named, never the thesis. Covers connecting SIGILS by USB or Bluetooth, a WAND and a
QUILL, more players, pairing and re-pairing, a talisman gone silent, and the way out of the SANCTUM and DEPART.
