## 1 · Logical consistency

The bible is unusually good at catching itself — fourteen retracted readings is a record most projects would hide. What it is worse at is **the sweep after the ruling**: a decision lands in one section and three others keep arguing the old position. Ranked.

### BREAK — Scorn never met Crystal, except in the three places he did `4.4 · 4.10 · 4.20 · 7.5 · 4.21`

4.4 rests the whole character on it: *"He never met her. That is the detail that holds the whole character together… He did not initiate the procedure… the file reached him complete."* Then: 7.5 files track 07 as *Fit for Work (Scorn's **Directive**)* — a deadline of Sept 3 addressed to her. 4.20 puts *"Ty and Richard"* inside one field each and says the minutes record *"two present could not follow"* — which puts Richard in the room. 4.21 calls the pair *"two men conferring quietly and filing minutes."* 4.3 makes Ty his partner *before* the rebrand, i.e. while she still ran the lab.

"Scheming vs. procedural" you resolved by frame, correctly. **"Met her" is not a frame; it is a fact.** Either he was in the building and issued the directive — and the horror is that a man who sat across from her still only ever filed — or he arrived afterwards and 07's title, 4.20's "two present", and 4.21 all belong to Ty plus *somebody else*. The second is cleaner for 4.4 and costs the opera one attribution. The first is more frightening and costs 4.4 its favourite sentence. Decide before Ty's Quicksilver line is written, because "I knew" means something different in each.

### BREAK — PREEMPT is an item and a species `1.6 (2026-09-08) · 4.26 (2026-09-04)`

ICE HEAL became `PREEMPT` four days after MANKEY became `PREEMPT`. 4.26's collision check validated names against species, moves and types — **not items**. The battle log will read *PIP used PREEMPT on PREEMPT*, which is the exact failure 4.26 exists to prevent. The item is the newer one and the weaker fit (see §7 — a hung process is not fixed by preemption anyway). Add items to the checker while you are there; it will find `STUB` next time someone names a placeholder item.

### BREAK — PAYLOAD and ROOTKIT are off-thesis by your own rule `1.7 (09-09) vs 4.26 (09-04)`

1.7 draws a sharp line: *EXPLOIT · PAYLOAD · INJECTION · MALWARE · BACKDOOR · BREACH* are refused because they make the world a thriller. Five days earlier the PARAS line shipped as `PAYLOAD → ROOTKIT`, and the WEEDLE line ends in `INJECTOR`. Either the rule is about *people* (Corpus never intrudes; wild things may) — in which case say so in 1.7 — or three species need renaming. The parasite reading is apt enough that I would amend the rule: *intrusion words belong to creatures, never to the institution.* That is actually a sharper line than the one written.

### BREAK — The chart page argues fifteen types; the ROM fights with seventeen `2.2 · 9.3 · 9.15 · The Chart`

*The Chart* presents "all 83 relations under our fifteen names" and runs the one-clause test on them. The GBA build has `HARDENED` and `OPAQUE`, SENTINEL is SIGNAL/HARDENED, and 9.15 paints plates for eighteen. So: (a) the published chart is a 15×15 slice of a 17×17 table and does not say so; (b) CONTEXT's perfect 8/8 was scored without OPAQUE, which is *immune* to it; (c) 8.1 still says "full type chart, all 15". Re-run the test at seventeen. It will not hurt the thesis — it improves it (see §3, the LOGIC → OPAQUE → CONTEXT → LOGIC cycle).

### BREAK — Brazen's B-section was composed on a retracted transcription `7.10 · 7.11 · 7.13 · Decision log`

7.11 retracts "the procedure never descends" (re-scan: 4 up / 3 down) and calls it the document's best-defended claim. Two paragraphs later, "What it buys, mechanically" still says the motif *"rises and never comes down."* 7.13 then builds `Music_Brazen`'s B-section to *"climb, never descend, never resolve."* The decision log lists the claim **and** its retraction as Settled, four lines apart (5532–5536). The music in the ROM may still be right as music; it is no longer *derived* from anything. Re-source it or own it as a composition choice.

## 2 · Enjoyment

Your own second review said it and you filed it as binding: *the bible is outrunning the slice.* Since then the slice went from 9% to 34% yours, which is real. The risks below are the ones a playtester will feel before they can name.

### BREAK — The first fifteen minutes spend fourteen beats of confusion

1.5 accepts that BIND "costs a beat the first time." So does DAEMON, USER, BOX, INDEX, BENCHMARK, UNBOUND, REMOTE, DETACH, HALTED, ROUTINE, MP, CACHE, RUN-as-execute. Each is defensible alone; 1.5 counts *three* lessons in fifteen minutes and there are closer to fourteen. Beats are not free and they are not independent — a player who is still resolving DAEMON does not have the attention for REMOTE. **Set a vocabulary budget for the slice**: count first appearances per screen, and let the TUTOR (9.16, the one voice allowed to explain) front-load the two that matter — DAEMON and BOX — before the grass. The rest can arrive when they are used.

### BREAK — A game that refuses to celebrate needs somewhere else to put the dopamine

*LABL was BOUND.* is correct and the binding jingle now descends. But vanilla's loop runs on that fanfare, and forty hours of the system declining to be pleased with you is a real drain, not a texture. The fix is already in your thesis: **the system doesn't celebrate; the bond does.** Let the daemon react — the party sprite bounces, or the saturation ticks up one notch visibly on bind and on level — while the text stays flat. The player is rewarded by the creature, never by the Index. That is 8.6's saturation rule made into a feedback loop, and it costs one animation.

### BREAK — SENTINEL as a random encounter is a wall, not a scene `4.25`

"It does nothing, and you cannot leave" is a superb *once*. As a repeating wild encounter with Attack 5 and inflated defences it is a two-minute slog every time the grass rustles, and MEAN LOOK at level six means every one of them. Make the first one scripted (a fixed SENTINEL by the generator, the horror intact), and give the random ones MEAN LOOK later or not at all. Same note for QUORUM in Doldrum Cave, which is post-game and therefore fine.

### DRIFT — Craft rule 5 is unpaid for benchmarks 2–8

*"If a gym's lesson can be skipped by grinding, it is not a lesson."* CAIRN is built. The other seven have a concept and a one-line "mechanical lesson" and no mechanics — "punishes split focus" is a wish, not a design. This is the single place the bible's prose-to-ROM ratio is most dangerous, because the gyms are the spine of the fun. One paragraph per leader: party, the one move or ability that *is* the lesson, and what a grinder runs into. (Doldrum already has its answer in the name: a FLOW leader whose gym has no wild encounters and whose trainers heal you — *no slope*.)

### DRIFT — `SUS` is going to be read as Among Us by everyone under thirty

The tile will get a laugh you did not write. `ZZZ` is vanilla-legible and honest about being asleep; `HLD` (held) fits the storage register (4.20a). Pick either.

### DRIFT — The unsupervised starter cannot attack

Six VECTOR moves in the whole game and LATENT nearly empty; 8.2 knows. The GBA pool is bigger (Aerial Ace, Shadow Ball) but the starter that "is confusing early and excellent late" must at least be *playable* early. CONSENSUS's precedent applies: one reliable VECTOR move at level 7.

### FREE — HOTFIX now visibly costs colour

Heal Powder lowers friendship in Gen 3. With saturation keyed to friendship, **a HOTFIX greys your daemon a notch**. "Nobody is proud of it" became a mechanic the day 8.6 was settled, and nothing has to be written.

### FREE — The Index completion line is already written

On an empty INDEX Crystal says *"…It is empty. That is a result as well."* On a complete one, say the same sentence with one word changed: *"…It is full. That is a result as well."* Forty hours between the two, identical register, and the diploma vanilla hands out becomes a shrug. Cheapest ending in the project.

## 3 · Machine learning accuracy

The technical vocabulary is better than most published ML games. Daemonisation via orphaned fork, cascading failure, thrashing, keepalive, quorum, livelock, speculative execution, the Bayesian PRIOR on Hidden Power, EVs-as-bias, Goodhart in Strathern's phrasing, Information Foraging with the right citation — all correct. Where it is off, it is off in ways that are cheap to fix and usually make the joke better.

### DRIFT — Preemption does not end a hung process `1.6`

Preempting a hung task just gives the CPU to somebody else; the hung one stays hung. What actually ends HUNG is a **watchdog** — a timer that resets a system that has stopped responding. `WATCHDOG` is eight characters, works twice (a dog; a watchdog timer), rhymes with ROVER, and clears the PREEMPT collision in the same move. PRIORITY, which you already call the weakest, has a better word too: throttles are lifted by a **burst** allowance — `BURST`, five characters, a burst of speed and burst credits, which is vocabulary your day job runs on.

### FREE — FROZEN's most common ML sense is missing from its concept line

2.2 glosses FROZEN as "overfit, brittle, hard-coded." In practice the word means *weights that are not updated* — a frozen backbone, frozen embeddings. Add it and two inherited relations stop being inherited: **FROZEN ×2 GROWTH** becomes literal (frozen weights stop training) and **FROZEN ×2 VECTOR** gets a reading (frozen embeddings cannot move in the space). Two points on the one-clause test for one clause.

### FREE — Seventeen types close a loop the fifteen could not

OPAQUE (Dark) is immune to CONTEXT and beats it; LOGIC (Fighting) beats OPAQUE. So **LOGIC → OPAQUE → CONTEXT → LOGIC** is a cycle: rules open black boxes, black boxes shrug off framing, framing beats rules. That is mechanistic interpretability, the interpretability gap, and the thesis, in one triangle — and it arrived from Game Freak's chart. HARDENED (Steel) resists EMERGENT and is immune to CORRUPT: *hardening contains emergence and cannot be poisoned.* Both are sitting in the vanilla table and neither is in *The Chart*.

### FREE — Two relations have real names in the literature

**STRATUM ×0 VECTOR** — "the physical layer cannot reach what has no location" — is Marr's three levels: the implementation level cannot read the computational one. That is a psychology-and-ML citation for the relation you flagged as the hardest to explain. **VECTOR ×2 LOGIC** is Sutton's Bitter Lesson. **SWARM ×2 CONTEXT** is Asch on one side and ensembling on the other — many weak models beat one confident frame. None go in the game; all belong in the chart page's "why" column.

### DRIFT — Small accuracy notes

- **MOCK vs STUB.** In Fowler's usage a stub returns canned answers and a mock verifies interactions; "the shape of a thing, hollow" is closer to a stub. Ditto copying moves is nearer a *spy*. Nobody will notice; recorded because you like being right.
- **"Consistency is exactly what bias produces"** is the bias–variance tradeoff stated correctly and it is your best ML sentence. It deserves a mechanic (§7).
- **Penphin's "two hemispheres."** Logic-left / emotion-right is pop-neuroscience the field has abandoned. The game never says *hemisphere*, so craft rule 1 protects you; keep it that way in the Index entry.
- **"The sensation of red is the same for everyone"** (0.4) is contested — cone ratios vary about 40:1 between people with normal vision and colour naming is stable anyway. The accurate version is *better* for you: sensation varies wildly and perception is normalised by context. Sensation is the part that is not shared.

## 4 · Psychology accuracy

Sound throughout, and in three places sharper than the document knows.

### FREE — Vera's ladder is the Strange Situation

*"It is settled. It has stopped watching the door."* is Ainsworth's secure attachment, verbatim in spirit: the securely attached explore and stop monitoring the caregiver. The bottom rung — *"It would be anywhere else if it could"* — is avoidant. You wrote attachment theory as a grooming minigame without a clinical word, which is craft rule 3 performed on a research literature. Do not touch it.

### FREE — Halftone is disenfranchised grief

"A town that does not know whether it is allowed to grieve" has a name — Doka's *disenfranchised grief*, loss that the surrounding culture does not sanction. It gives the tower's NPCs a real vocabulary of behaviour (private rituals, defensiveness, the funeral held in a back room) without one line about feeling.

### FREE — The ERRATA exhibit is correctly described

"It only ever tested what it already held" is Wason's confirmation bias exactly; survivorship and argument from incredulity are named right; "we all saw the same picture at the same moment" is collective memory conformity. The one caution: "attributed to the machine, never to the reader" is the right move but it also lets the player off — one card that is about *the curator* ("This card was written by someone who liked the exhibit") would close the gap between craft rule 3 and complicity.

### DRIFT — The removal plot is handled with real care — keep the guard on Act 2

4.10's rule (procedural, never medical; process, never pathology) is the ethically correct handling and the game's own discipline enforces it. The one door still open is *Nine Scars*: the objection is about standing, which is right, but a "file she kept about him" that lists nine oversteps *by Ty* puts the son, not the institution, as the instigator. That is a psychological claim (the removal came from inside the family) and it collides with 4.4's "policy that triggered." Decide who filed, because it changes what the player is meant to feel at Quicksilver.

## 5 · The rock opera, as adapted

The transcriptions have been through more scrutiny than most published musicology and the harmonic readings are mostly sound: I–vi–IV–V shared by *Poly and Fields* and *Crystal's Reply* is real; the Dm|B♭|C|Am no-dominant reading is right in D minor; Ty 74→120 against Crystal's 128→128 is a genuine structural pair. Three notes.

- **7.3's physics is off by a semitone at the violet end.** 12·log₂(700/400) = 9.68, so a 400nm cut lands between A and A♯; at 380nm it reaches 10.6, which is A♯/B. Only B is unambiguously "no wavelength." The Halftone-on-the-invented-notes reading survives at half strength; say "one to two semitones" rather than two.
- **Where you start decides whether it resolves** (4.21) is the best musical sentence in the bible and it is *also* the thesis about frames — but it depends on assigning a tonic to a four-chord loop, which is exactly the ambiguity you are celebrating. Label it a reading, not a finding, or it joins the retracted.
- **Placement.** Fifteen tracks are transcribed and four have homes. The opera is "character music, not place music" (7.5), so the unplaced motifs are not a backlog — but *Crystal Clear or Crystal Crazy* (the rumour, 160 BPM, four repeated notes) has an obvious one: it is the Five Witnesses' music. Five people saying the same thing over and over until it drops.

## 7 · Outside the box

Ranked by how much argument they buy per byte. Every one obeys craft rule 1.

### IDEA — Make "IMPROVE RESPONSE CONSISTENCY" a mechanic

Gen 3 damage rolls 85–100%. **Scorn's daemons always roll 100 and never crit; their secondary effects never proc.** His numbers repeat. A player who has done the HEAT show ("damage wobbles") will notice the fight that does not — and nobody says why. Then the CC-7 pull at Quicksilver is the un-weighting: S.T.A.R.R. gets its variance back, and RECURSION is the loop that only runs on a machine allowed to be inconsistent. The bias–variance tradeoff as a boss fight, one flag in the damage calc.

### IDEA — The ability pass — the second teaching surface you paid for and have not spent

9.3 named abilities as a reason to port. Since then, one (Insomnia). But vanilla already wrote half your argument in them: **SYNCHRONIZE** (Abra line, Mew) passes your status to whoever inflicted it — *your frame becomes mine* — it is PERSPECTIVE as a passive and ARTSAI already has it. **TRACE** (Porygon) copies the opponent's ability — *the substrate takes on whatever runs on it*, which is SUBSTRATE's entire Index entry as a mechanic, already on the right species. **MAGNET PULL** (Magnemite) traps HARDENED — SENTINEL cannot be left by hardened things either. **LEVITATE** is "no location." **PRESSURE** drains MP — CYCLES — faster. Seventy-seven names, one register per type like the moves, and it is the surface a player reads on every switch-in.

### IDEA — Choice Band is Scorn's crime as a held item

FireRed ships it: +50% Attack, locked into one move. That is a weight raised and the alternatives discarded — CC-7 in miniature, holdable. Name it **CONVICTION**: a belief held to, and a sentence handed down. Give one to a Corpus grunt and nothing else needs saying.

### IDEA — The Five Witnesses differ by edition

Keep the one accurate spatial detail identical on both carts; let the *wrong* details differ. Two players comparing notes can triangulate in half the time — you literally need someone else's context, over a cable, to see the room. 8.4's structural argument, applied to the one puzzle whose subject is composite perception. Five `IF DEF` blocks.

### IDEA — Al's party desaturates over the game

Corpus daemons are grey at friendship 0; Al is not Corpus. But he was raised on pure content optimisation. Start his party in colour at the lab and step its friendship down at each rival fight, so by the incumbent battle his team is grey and yours is not. The transmission failure, visible, and no sneer — the colour was there and he optimised it away. (Scorn's own team, by contrast, might be *full* colour: the man loves his inventory. That would be the most uncomfortable choice available.)

### IDEA — CONSENSUS scales with the quorum

You wanted it boring. A quieter version keeps it reliable and makes the name true: power 50 + 20 per SWARM daemon in the party. One SWARM is a weak vote; six is 170. "A swarm needs to keep showing up" stops being a tuning note and becomes what the move *is*. Engine work, but small, and it is the one place a party-composition mechanic argues the chart.

### IDEA — The Schrödinger reading can be made mechanical, cheaply

You held it pending a mechanic. Here is one: the Index's "bound" sprite takes **the saturation the daemon had at the moment it was recorded, and never updates.** Your CAREMUSAI at max friendship is full colour in the party; the Index still shows the grey thing you caught at level 4. Observing through the instrument fixed the record at the instant of observation — a reproduction that loses the original, per daemon, and the player watches the two drift apart for forty hours. One saved byte per species.
