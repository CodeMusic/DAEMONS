SUNO PROMPT - the front door
======================================================================

7.14g decided this one already, and it decided it by getting it wrong first.
The title screen used to be repointed at Music_Dungeon3 -- F minor, the track
where Crystal goes into the system after the removal -- and it told the player
this is a grim game before a line of dialogue could say otherwise.

    C major. I-vi-IV-V. 150 BPM. Twice through.

That is not an arbitrary bright key. 7.4 gives Blanche C pentatonic, high and
open, and Slate is the same scale with both gaps filled -- so the title screen
is in the key of the town you leave from. Nobody notices; it just feels like
somewhere you have been.

THE OTHER CONSTRAINT IS THE ENGINE

This gets transcribed to MIDI and played back on THREE CHANNELS. A dense mix
does not survive that -- it arrives as mud. So the arrangement has to be
legible as three lines: a melody, a bass, and one voice in between.

Everything in AVOID below is there because it breaks the transcription, not
because it would sound bad.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
THE STYLE BOX
----------------------------------------------------------------------
instrumental game title theme, C major, 150 BPM, chord progression C - Am - F
- G repeating, bright warm and open, hopeful and unhurried, sparse trio
arrangement: one clear bell-like lead melody, one warm round bass, one simple
arpeggio between them, clean note attacks, steady tempo throughout, dry mix,
minimal reverb, no vocals

----------------------------------------------------------------------
LYRICS BOX
----------------------------------------------------------------------
[Instrumental]

----------------------------------------------------------------------
AVOID
----------------------------------------------------------------------
no vocals or vocal chops       - there is no channel for them
no pads or ambient wash        - a sustained pad transcribes as one wrong note
no heavy reverb or delay       - tails smear the note onsets
no drum kit beyond a light     - percussion is the noise channel and gets
  pulse, or none at all          dropped; it should not carry the groove
no key change, no rubato,      - the transcriber assumes one key and one tempo
  no ritardando
no cinematic build or          - 7.14g: this is a door, not a trailer
  orchestral swell
not dark, not epic, not tense  - craft rule 6, and the whole reason this
                                 track exists

----------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SHAPE TO ASK FOR
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Short and loopable -- sixty to ninety seconds. One eight-bar hook, stated,
then stated again with the arpeggio moving. It should end where it began so
the loop point does not announce itself.

The melody it is replacing goes:

    C  E  G  B  |  C5 A  A  G  |  F  A  G  D  |  E  G  C  B

Rising, then turning back, then rising again. Worth keeping that shape even if
every note changes: it is a phrase that opens rather than resolves, which is
what a front door should do.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN IT COMES BACK
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Check three things before it goes anywhere near the MIDI step:

  1. Is it actually C major at 150? Suno drifts, and a track in D at 138 is
     a different decision about the town you leave from.
  2. Can you hear THREE lines? Close your eyes and count them. If you cannot
     separate a bass from the middle voice, the transcription will not either.
  3. Does the last bar want to be followed by the first? If it lands, it is
     an ending, and a title screen does not end.

Then run it through the transcription prompt with the NO AUDIO gate. 4.9
records what happens when a model that cannot hear is asked to transcribe:
it hands back an earlier answer and admits, in its own DOUBT field, that the
transcription was a guess.
