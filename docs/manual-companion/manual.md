# The companion {#companion .t-CONTEXT}

DAEMONS is a game for the Game Boy Advance, or anything that pretends to be one. Your daemons live in it. But a daemon
does not have to stay in the game. **SEND** one from your party, and it comes out here, to **the companion**: an app on
your phone, a page in your browser, a watch on your wrist. You give it a drink, feed it, put it to bed and talk with it.
It helps you with one goal, one step at a time. It dreams at night. And when you **CALL** it **HOME**, it goes back
into your game with what it learned.

This book is about the companion: every tab, every page, and what each button does. It is written for everyone who
holds the app. The everyday parts come first, in short steps. The parts about the hub, its mind and the devices come
later, in their own chapters, for whoever looks after them.

## What you need {#need}

- **Your game of DAEMONS**, CONTENT or CONTEXT. Any way of playing it will do: an emulator on a computer, the app's own
  GAME tab, or a cartridge whose save you copy to a computer.
- **The companion.** It comes in three forms, and they show the same pages:
  - **the app**, called DAEMONS on the home screen, on an iPhone, an iPad or a Mac;
  - **the site**, the same pages in any browser at home;
  - **the devices**: the phone's home-screen widget, and, if you like, a watch, a handheld or a Tab5.
- **A hub**, for most things: the companion's own small server, on a computer or a little box at home that never
  sleeps. A household shares one. It reads your save, keeps your daemon's days, and answers every screen. A phone can
  also look after a daemon on its own, with no hub at all ([A phone on its own](#alone)).

{{diagram house | Your home. Every screen talks to the hub. The hub reads the game's save at each SYNC, and asks whichever mind can answer when your daemon talks.}}

## The tabs {#tabs}

Across the top of every screen is a strip of tabs. Tap one to open it. Tap the open one again to go back to its first
page. On a narrow phone the strip scrolls, and a **›** at the right shows there are more.

| tab | what it holds |
|---|---|
| **TODAY** | the day, the one thing to do next, and a glance at everything else |
| **GAME** | DAEMONS itself, played in the app (iPhone, iPad and Mac only) |
| **DAEMON** | your daemon away: its care, its talk, SYNC, NEARBY; your party, the INDEX, the CASINO, SOUNDINGS, DREAMS, MEETINGS |
| **JOURNEY** | your GOALS and your JOURNAL |
| **LIBRARY** | a bookcase: the books found in the game, the manuals, CALLOW SCHOOL, the NOTEBOOK and the SONGBOOK |
| **YOU** | you as the game draws you: MARKS, ROOTS, the maps, the story so far, THE ROAD AHEAD, TRADE |
| **PLACES** | your devices, the hub, the glasses, the house's lights and the TV |

Beside the tabs, at the top right, are two more:

- **⚙** opens **SETTINGS** ([Settings](#settings)).
- **? HOW TO PLAY** opens the three short books on playing the game ([HOW TO PLAY and WHAT'S NEW](#howto)). After the
  app has changed, it reads **✦ WHAT'S NEW** instead, until you have looked. On a narrow screen they shrink to **?**
  and **✦ NEW**.

::: note
**Nothing in the companion tells you what your game has not reached.** A MARK not earned, a book not found, a part of
the story still ahead: each shows as *not yet*, and nothing more. The game is yours to find.
:::

# First steps {#first .t-SIGNAL}

## Pairing the app {#pairing}

The first time you open the app, it asks to be **paired** with your hub, so the two know each other.

1. On the hub's site, open **⚙** SETTINGS, then **PAIR A PHONE**, and tap **Show a code**. (On a hub with a touch
   screen of its own, you can hold its screen for two seconds instead.) You will see a six-digit code and the hub's
   address.
2. On the phone, under **Pair this phone**, type the address, the code, and a name for the phone. Tap **Pair**.
3. When iOS asks to find devices on your **Local Network**, say yes. Without it the app cannot reach the hub at home.

A code works once, for ten minutes. If the phone ever loses the hub at home, pair it again.

::: tip
**Use the hub's own name in the address, not its number.** A name stays the same on every network; a number can
change, and then the phone cannot find home.
:::

## A phone on its own {#alone}

No hub? On the pairing screen, choose **Or host it on this phone alone**. The phone keeps a copy of your save and does
the companion's work itself. Almost everything works: care, goals, the LIBRARY, YOU, the CASINO. What needs a hub --
talking with your daemon in the house's voices, meetings written up, a board or watch at home -- waits until you pair.

If you play in the Delta emulator on the phone, the save comes over through Files, and goes back the same way:
SETTINGS, **THIS PHONE HOSTS ON ITS OWN**, **Take a newer save from Files** and **Give the save back to Delta**.

## Your save {#save}

The hub needs to know where your game keeps its save.

1. Open **⚙** SETTINGS. Under **YOUR DAEMONS SAVE**, tap **Choose a save…** and pick the `.sav` file.
2. Or type where it is under **OR TYPE THE PATH**, and tap **Save**. **Open the folder** shows where it lives.

From an emulator, the save is the `.sav` beside the game. From a cartridge, copy the save to the computer first.
Point the emulator and the companion at **the same file**, and everything else just works.

**The companion always keeps a backup** of your save before it changes anything in it, and never leaves a save half
written. If you play in the app's own GAME tab, there is nothing to set: the game's save *is* the companion's.

::: note
**The first save you SYNC becomes your companion's game.** A save from a different game is shown, but never written.
:::

## SEND, SYNC, and CALL HOME {#sync}

**Close the game before you SYNC.** A game left running writes its own copy of the save back over the companion's.

1. In the companion, open **DAEMON** and tap **SYNC**. The first time, it says *Linked. Your game now offers SEND in a
   daemon's menu.*
2. In the game, open a party daemon's menu and choose **SEND**. (Only a daemon in good health goes.) The game saves.
3. Close the game. The companion notices the save has changed and SYNCs by itself -- or tap **SYNC** yourself.
4. The daemon is now **AWAY**. In your party it is shown faded, *ON YOUR DEVICE*. What it was holding goes with it.

To bring it home: in the game, the same daemon's menu offers **CALL HOME**. Choose it, save, close the game, and SYNC.
What it learned while it was out -- the levels it grew -- comes home with it.

You can send as many as you like, one for each device.

## RECEIVED {#received}

When a SYNC brings something, a framed card says so, over whatever you are looking at: **Received**, the daemon (or
the item, and how many), and who it is **From** -- you, for a daemon you sent; another player, for a trade or a gift.
Tap it to put it away. If more than one came, the next follows.

The DAEMON tab also says what each SYNC did, in a line under the button: who arrived, who went home, what the CASINO
won, and *Synced. Nothing was waiting.* when there was nothing.

## The widget {#widget}

The quickest way to look after your daemon is on the iPhone's home screen, without opening anything.

1. Open the app once, paired. That hands the widget your hub's address.
2. Long-press the home screen, tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**, and choose **DAEMONS** (small, medium or large).
3. It says *Looking for your companion...* for a moment, then shows your daemon in its den.
4. Choose which daemon it hosts in **PLACES**, **YOUR DEVICES**: it is listed as **This phone's widget**.

Its buttons are **FEED**, **DRINK**, then one that follows the day: **TRAIN** in the daytime, **SLEEP** from dusk, and
**WAKE** while it sleeps. The last button, **JOURNEY**, opens the app on your goals.

A press answers at once: the button lights, the daemon hops, and a phrase of music plays. If the hub cannot be
reached, the widget says *Kept until home answers.* and sends it later. Nothing is lost.

# TODAY {#today .t-GROWTH}

**TODAY** is the first page, and the one to open each morning.

{{pictures today | TODAY: the day at the top, then THE ONE THING.}}

## The day {#day}

At the top: the **day of the week**, its **note** and the **season**, then the day's word in large letters and a line
under it. Each day of the week has its own colour, note and virtue, and the whole app is dressed in the day's colour.
CONTENT keeps the northern year's seasons; CONTEXT the southern.

Beside the note is a small **tuning fork**. Tap it and a **singing bowl** opens in the day's colour, patterned with the
day's centre and its virtue over its shadow, and it rings the day's note. Tap the bowl to ring it again; tap outside
it, or **[x]**, to close it.

## THE CALENDAR {#calendar}

Beside the date is a small **calendar**, in the month's colours: the months go round the wheel of the week's hues, red
in January to violet in December. Under the date, a line says the month's tone.

Tap the calendar to open **THE CALENDAR**. At the top is the month: its name, where it comes from, its colours and
its practice, and the month's own bowl to ring. Under it, **MONTH**, **WEEK** and **DAY** show the events in your
phone's calendar. The first time, the phone asks to share its calendar with DAEMONS; allow full access to see and
change your events. Tap an event to change it or remove it, or **+** to add one; a repeating event changes only at
the time you tapped. Your events stay on the phone. Said no by mistake? The phone's Settings, **Privacy & Security**,
**Calendars**, turns it back on.

The months follow your hemisphere: CONTENT reads the northern year, CONTEXT the southern, and SETTINGS, **THEME**,
**THE MONTH'S SEASON**, can choose the other.

## THE ONE THING {#onething}

Under the day is **THE ONE THING**: the very next step of your goal, and nothing else. When you have done it, tap
**Done**. The next step takes its place.

With no goal yet, it says *Nothing to do yet. Add a goal, and the first step will wait here.* Goals are made on
**JOURNEY** ([GOALS](#goals)).

## A glance at everything {#glance}

Under that, a card for each place, and each one opens its tab when you tap it:

- **YOUR DAEMON** -- the daemon your phone looks after, how it is, and how it has eaten and drunk today, with **Feed**,
  **Drink** and **Train** right there.
- **NEARBY** -- the last thing another daemon said or did.
- **YOUR GAME** -- where you last saved, how many MARKS you hold, your MONEY, and what the CASINO will add or take at
  the next SYNC.
- **IN THE LIBRARY** -- NOTEBOOK pages you have not yet read in the game, and the songs in the SONGBOOK.

# Your daemon {#daemon .t-FLOW}

The **DAEMON** tab is your daemon's home in the companion. Across its top is a row of choices: **Yours**, **Party**,
**INDEX**, **Routines**, **SOUNDINGS**, **DREAMS** and **MEETINGS**. It opens on **Yours**.

{{pictures daemon | DAEMON, Yours: the daemon in its den, its day, and the keys to care for it.}}

## Its den {#den}

Your daemon lives in a **den** that looks like its type: a FLOW daemon among waves, a STRATUM daemon among layers of
rock, a LEGACY daemon by a cairn, an OPAQUE one beside a box you cannot see into. It walks about, faces its way, and
hops when you tend it. The den is never labelled; it is only somewhere to be.

## Caring for it {#care}

Under the den are four keys, large enough to find without looking:

| key | what it does | it says |
|---|---|---|
| **Feed** | a meal; three a day is enough | *Eaten.* |
| **Drink** | a drink; three a day | *Drank.* |
| **Train** | a little exercise | *Trained.* |
| **Sleep** | to bed. Lit at bedtime | *Asleep.* |

When you feed it, a piece of fruit sits beside it and is eaten in bites; when it drinks, a small glass empties. While
it sleeps, the card says **ASLEEP** plainly and shows one key, **Wake**.

Put to bed from dusk, six in the evening, it sleeps the night, until you wake it or tend it in the morning, and at noon
at the latest. Put down in the daytime, it naps for an hour and a half.

After each care your daemon answers in a line of its own -- chosen by its mood and the day -- and the phone plays a
phrase of music on your game's own instrument: the **HARP** for CONTENT, the **FLUTE** for CONTEXT. It never nags.

::: note
**Its care is the same everywhere.** Feed it on the widget, and the app knows; put it to bed on a watch, and the phone
shows it asleep. The app looks again whenever you bring it to the front, and every half minute while it is open.
:::

## How it is, and how it has been {#feelings}

The card shows how your daemon is as a row of bars, each filled in the day's colour, with a word beside it:

| bar | it tells | its words |
|---|---|---|
| **MOOD** | how it feels now | good, fair, low |
| **REST** | the nights it was put to bed | rested, tired, worn out |
| **FED** | the meals of the last days | full, peckish, hungry |
| **WATER** | the drinks of the last days | watered, dry, thirsty |
| **COMPANY** | how near it feels to you | close, near, distant |
| **WEIGHT** | its balance: the middle mark | thin, lean, balanced, plump, heavy |

**Its face shows it too.** Glad after a meal, it brightens and a heart rises over it. Sad, it greys and droops.
Sleepy, its eyes half close. Asleep, they shut and a *zZ* drifts up. Short nights put shadows under its eyes; days
without meals make it thinner, and days eaten well with no training a little rounder.

None of this is kept as a score. It is worked out each time from what you have done together, so nothing runs down while
you are not looking, and a day or two of care puts any of it right. A feeling is never named on the screen. It shows.

## Grown here {#grown}

A daemon away grows. Every step of your goal you finish counts as a meal and gives it experience. When it has grown,
the card says so: *Grown here: level 12 to 13. It takes that home with it.*

## The SYNC card {#synccard}

Under the care is the **SYNC** card. It reads *Close the game, then SYNC to bring your save across.* When the game is
asking for something -- a daemon SENT, or CALLed HOME -- it reads *The game is asking. Close it first, then SYNC.*

If no save has been chosen yet, SYNC shows **FIRST, YOUR SAVE**, with **Choose a save…** and **Settings**, and walks
you through it.

{{pictures daemon-sync | The SYNC card, and NEARBY under it.}}

## Party {#party}

**Party** shows your party as the game draws it: each daemon's picture, its name, its kind and level, what it holds,
and how it feels in the game's own words. One that is away is faded and says **ON YOUR DEVICE**. One the game has asked
for says **ASKED HOME** or **ASKED TO GO**.

Tap one to open its card: its INDEX entry, and **Read aloud** on a phone.

{{pictures party | Party: the daemons in your game, and one away on your device.}}

## The INDEX {#index}

**INDEX** is every entry your save has seen, in your edition's own voice. Each card holds the daemon's number, its
types in their colours, its height and weight, the entries of both editions, and where it is **NATIVE** -- a region,
an edition only, its hours, its seasons, or not in the wild at all. If your bag holds OPUS, its notes in the **MARGIN**
are shown beside the entry.

A card stays grey until your game has come to understand that kind of daemon, then takes its colours.

**LISTEN** reads the entry aloud in the INDEX's own voice. While the voice is being made, a spinner turns, and **STOP**
stops it; while it speaks, **HUSH** quiets it.

{{pictures index | The INDEX: an entry, its NATIVE line, and LISTEN.}}

## Routines {#routines}

**Routines** holds **THE CASINO**, which has a chapter of its own ([The CASINO](#casino)), and **YOUR PARTY'S
ROUTINES**: the moves your party knows, as the game knows them.

## In its own voice {#own-voice}

Some daemons have something to say about themselves. On their card, the picture **shimmers**. Tap it, and the daemon
steps out over the card into its den, says *THINKING…* while its voice is made, and then tells you of itself in its
own voice, its mouth moving as it speaks. Then it shrinks back into the card. The INDEX entry stays as the record
keeps it; this is the daemon's own telling, and only the app's.

## A look inside {#its-mind}

**THE MIND ›**, under its card on **Yours**, is a look around your daemon's mind:

- **NOW**: its face, the colour of what it feels and the weather in its room. Tap a feeling for the words behind it,
  and what made it so.
- **BOTH DREAMS**: the last dream it had here, beside the one your save carries home to the game.
- **VOICE**: the voice it speaks in -- its kind's, as it feels today, or one you gave it -- with a line to hear.
- **SELF**: what a mind is given when your daemon answers, exactly as it is given.
- **REMEMBERED**: what it keeps of yesterday, and of the month.

## THE EASEL {#easel}

Under its card on **Yours** is **THE EASEL ›**, when the companion is with a hub. Type what it should paint, under
*What should it paint?*, and **PAINT**: your daemon walks to its easel and paints while you wait, the canvas filling a
stroke at a time. It paints in a style of its own, from how it is today -- how it feels, what it needs, your goals,
the weather on the way -- and gives the painting a title and a line about it. A painting takes a minute or two.

**THE GALLERY**, under the easel, keeps every painting, a wall for each daemon, each one framed. Tap a painting to see
it large; hold it to see **WHAT WAS ASKED**. **TAKE IT DOWN** takes one off the wall.

## SOUNDINGS {#soundings}

**SOUNDINGS** is one quiet card for each daemon away: when it was last heard, and how it was doing then -- *No signal
yet*, *Quiet today*, *Doing fine*, *Doing well*. Each SYNC writes it into your save for the game to read. There is
nothing to fix, no score and no streak: only what was heard.

## READS {#reads}

Once your game holds a certain book, a card called **READS** joins the DAEMON page. Now and then, on a watch, your
daemon notices something before it happens -- someone close by, rain on the way, a still night -- and gives you **a
read**. The companion keeps count of which came true, and never says one was right before it has.

# Talking with your daemon {#talk .t-EMERGENT}

**Hold to talk, let go, and your daemon answers** -- in its own words, as itself, and aloud.

## HOLD TO TALK {#holdtotalk}

On a paired phone, the DAEMON tab, **Yours**, has a card called **TALK WITH** and your daemon's name. (The site has
no microphone card; type to your daemon there instead, as below.)

1. Press and hold **HOLD TO TALK**. It turns to **LISTENING…**
2. Say something, as you would to a friend.
3. Let go. It says **THINKING…**, then shows what it heard, in quotation marks, and your daemon's answer under it.
4. A moment later the answer is spoken. **HEAR IT AGAIN** plays it once more.

The first time, the phone asks to use the microphone. If you said no, the card says *The microphone is off for this
app (Settings, then DAEMONS).* -- turn it on there.

A small grey line under the answer says which minds took part: where you were heard, where it was thought, and where
it was spoken ([THE MIND](#mind)).

## What it knows, and what it remembers {#remembers}

Your daemon answers as itself: its kind, its type, how it feels today, whether it has eaten and slept, and what it
dreamt. **It remembers** the talk you are having: tell it something, then ask about it. It keeps the thread for
fifteen quiet minutes, on that device, and nothing is written down.

Ask *what should I do next?* and it tells you your goal's next step. Otherwise it leaves your goal alone.

::: safe
**The recording never leaves your home.** Only the words go to be answered, and THE MIND says how far they may go.
And every voice says DAEMONS as it is said: **DAY-mons**.
:::

## Other places to talk {#talk-elsewhere}

- **On a watch or a handheld**: hold the dial, the ball or the face button a beat, as each device's own page says.
- **Typed**: SETTINGS, THE MIND, **TRY IT** -- type a line and **SAY IT**, and the daemon answers as if you had held TALK.
- **The books** that answer -- THE GUIDE and THE SUBTLE LAYER -- have their own **ASK** ([The books found](#found)).

# Nights and dreams {#nights .t-OPAQUE}

**Your daemon sleeps when you do, and in the morning it has had a dream.**

## A night {#night}

A night begins when you put your daemon to bed: **Sleep** on the DAEMON tab, on the widget, or on a watch. A watch
worn to bed can also notice by itself that you have gone still, between eight at night and four in the morning.

It ends when you **Wake** it, or tend it in the morning. A night has a shape: a long still one is **a deep night**, an
ordinary one **a quiet night**, a short or broken one **a restless night**. The phone notices, too, how often it was
picked up in the night, and counts that as stirring.

## The dream {#dream}

When your daemon wakes, it tells you its dream at once, in a line on its card. If the house has a mind to spare, the
dream is then told again, more fully, in the daemon's own words -- it takes its place within a minute.

## DREAMS, the journal {#dreams}

**DAEMON**, **DREAMS** keeps every night, newest first: its title, the dream, how long and how still the night was,
and whether the daemon was tucked in.

{{pictures dreams | DREAMS: each night's dream, with its title, and the lenses above it.}}

When the house's mind tells a dream, it tells it **twice**, as two halves of a mind would, and then makes the two one:

- **REASON**, in red: the night in order, from falling asleep to waking, one exact detail at a time;
- **INSTINCT**, in blue: all at once, one image becoming another;
- **WHOLE**, in purple, both lit together: the dream as it is kept, with its title.

Tap a lens to read that telling. **LISTEN** reads aloud whatever is shown. **SHARE** shows a dream to the other players
at your home ([PLAYING TOGETHER](#together-pointer)).

With no dreams yet, it says so: wear the watch to bed, or tuck the daemon in with **Sleep**, and the dream is here in
the morning.

## SLEEP SOUNDS {#sleepsounds}

Above the dreams, **SLEEP SOUNDS** is music made for your daemon as it is tonight, to fall asleep to together. Its kind
chooses the instruments, its feelings the key and the pace, and what it needs what the music carries -- rain for a
thirsty one, a hearth for a hungry one. **MAKE TONIGHT'S** asks the hub for it; it takes about three minutes, and is
kept on the phone once it comes.

**PLAY 30 MINUTES** plays it for half an hour, fading away over the last ten. **MAKE ANOTHER** makes a new one, and
**KEPT FROM OTHER NIGHTS** holds the ones before.

**DRIFT DOWN** is for headphones: a slow beat between your ears under the music, easing from alpha to theta to delta
as you fall asleep. Without headphones the music plays alone until they are on.

## The dream goes home {#dream-home}

At the next SYNC, the last dream goes into your save with the daemon: how the night went, which dream it was, the
hours slept. What the game does with it is for you to find there.

## How you live, it shows {#thrive}

Over a week, good nights and goals met show quietly in how your daemon looks: a soft **glow** behind it, its colours a
shade **richer**, a **gold star** for each goal finished this week, a **crescent moon** the morning after a deep night.
A poor week makes it a little paler, and drowsy until noon after a broken night. Nothing is kept beyond the week, so a
good week fades by itself, and a bad one does too.

# MEETINGS {#meetings .t-VECTOR}

Your daemon can sit in on a meeting -- a club, a class, a family plan -- and write it up
for you afterwards.

{{pictures meetings | MEETINGS: RECORD A MEETING, FROM A VOICE MEMO, and the meetings written up.}}

## Recording one {#recording}

On **DAEMON**, **MEETINGS**:

1. Tap **RECORD A MEETING**. On a phone it keeps recording with the screen locked; on the site, keep the page open.
2. When the meeting ends, tap **STOP AND WRITE IT UP**. (**STOP, DON'T KEEP IT** throws it away.)
3. It says **SENDING…**, then *Sent. It is being heard now, and its write-up will be here.*

Or tap **FROM A VOICE MEMO**: in Voice Memos, choose **•••**, **Save to Files**, then choose it here. On a watch, it is
**HOURS**, **RECORD A MEETING**.

If the phone cannot reach the hub, the recording is kept on the phone. **SEND THEM NOW** sends what is waiting.

## The write-up {#writeup}

The hub hears it a part at a time (*Listening to it…*), then writes it up (*Writing it up…*):

- **SUMMARY** -- what it was about;
- **TAKE-AWAYS** -- what to remember;
- **ACTION ITEMS** -- what to do;
- **THE WHOLE TRANSCRIPT**, every word, to read, and **HIDE THE TRANSCRIPT** to fold it away.

Your daemon day-dreams over it, too. If it could not be written up, **TRY AGAIN**. **FORGET IT** removes it.

## VOICE NOTES {#voicenotes}

A **voice note**, said into a watch, comes here as words. **MAKE IT A GOAL** turns one into your goal: *Added to your
goals.*

::: note
**Meetings are heard and written up on the hub.** A phone hosting on its own cannot, and says so. The recording stays in
your house.
:::

# JOURNEY {#journey .t-LOGIC}

**JOURNEY** holds two things: **GOALS** and the **JOURNAL**. Tap either at the top.

## GOALS {#goals}

The companion keeps **one goal at a time**, on purpose. A few words is enough -- *Clean the house* -- and simpler is
better.

1. Under **YOUR GOAL**, type it and tap **Set it**.
2. Then give it **steps**: tap **step**, type one -- *wash the windows* -- and tap **Add**.
3. A bigger goal can have **milestones**, each with steps of its own: tap **milestone**, type one -- *living room* --
   and tap **Add**. A milestone has its own line, **+ a step in** its name, to add steps under it.
4. Tick a step's box when it is done. **✕** removes a step or a milestone.

{{pictures goals | YOUR GOAL, its steps and a milestone, and WALKING under it.}}

The first step not yet done is **THE ONE THING** on TODAY. Each step you finish counts as a meal for your daemon and
gives it experience, which it takes home at the next SYNC. A goal met puts a gold star by it for the week.

## WALKING {#walking}

Under the goal is **WALKING**: the steps you have walked today, of a daily goal you choose -- **6k**, **8k**, **10k** or
**12k a day**. On an iPhone, your steps come from Apple Health (it asks once). You can also type them and tap **Save**.
Reaching the goal counts as activity for your daemon: *Reached: your daemon is glad of the walk.*

## The JOURNAL {#journal}

The **JOURNAL** is yours to keep. You can have several, each with a title.

1. Under **YOUR JOURNALS**, type a title -- *This autumn* -- choose **WHAT KIND** it is, **STANDARD** or **DREAM
   JOURNAL**, and tap **Start it**. A dream journal is marked **DREAMS**, and its button reads **+ NEW DREAM**.
2. Open it, and tap **+ NEW ENTRY**. Give it a title if you like, write what happened and what you noticed, and tap
   **Save entry**.

# When it goes wrong {#wrong .t-FROZEN}

| what you see | what to do |
|---|---|
| **The widget says *Looking for your companion...* and stays there** | open the app once, paired; allow the Local Network (iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network); remove the widget and add it again |
| **The widget says *No daemon here*** | the hub answered, but the widget hosts no daemon. SEND one, SYNC, and choose it in PLACES, YOUR DEVICES |
| ***Kept until home answers.*** | the hub could not be reached. Nothing is lost; it is sent later. At home, check the hub is on |
| **AWAY, at the top right, while you are at home** | the phone was probably paired by a number that has changed. SETTINGS, THIS PHONE, Leave this companion (tap twice), then pair again by the hub's name |
| ***This save belongs to a different game.*** | the save you chose is not your companion's game. Choose the right one in SETTINGS |
| ***That file is not a DAEMONS save.*** | choose the `.sav` the game writes, not the game itself |
| ***The game is asking. Close it first, then SYNC.*** | close the game, then SYNC |
| **SYNC did nothing you expected** | was the game still open? Close it, and SYNC again. Every SYNC keeps a backup first |
| ***The microphone is off for this app*** | iOS Settings, DAEMONS, Microphone |
| **Talk says nothing back** | SETTINGS, THE MIND: is any mind's dot green? LOOK AGAIN. A phone alone cannot talk without a mind of its own or an endpoint |
| **A journal chapter or a meeting is still being written** | it takes a few minutes; come back later. If it failed, TRY AGAIN |
| **The CASINO says to choose your save first** | the CASINO plays with your save's money; set YOUR DAEMONS SAVE in SETTINGS |
| **HOME says to set up Home Assistant** | it is set on the hub's own site, SETTINGS, HOME ASSISTANT |

Still stuck? The companion's wiki has a page called *Troubleshooting*, with more.
