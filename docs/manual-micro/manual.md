# What DAEMONS is {#what .t-CONTEXT}

A game about a world where the machines came alive. They grew into creatures called **DAEMONS** (say it
*DEE-muns*), and everyone you meet is an animal. You are a **USER**: you travel with DAEMONS, make friends with them,
and keep a record of every one you meet, called the **INDEX**. Eight town leaders test you along the way; pass a test
and you earn a **MARK**, like a badge.

The game never stops to explain itself: its stories are there for you to piece together, at your own pace. It comes in
two editions, **CONTENT** and **CONTEXT** -- one story, told two ways. Somewhere in the world are two books to find and
read, **THE PROGRAMMER'S GUIDE TO THE HUMAN MIND** and **THE SUBTLE LAYER**.

::: rumour
And some players whisper about a third.
:::

## Getting it going {#going}

DAEMONS is made by fans, so it is not sold in shops, and it is made from a copy of a game you own. **The first time,
ask whoever gave you this to set it up.** Then:

- **On an iPad or an iPhone**, open the DAEMONS app. On an iPad turned on its side, the game is on the left; on an
  iPhone, tap **GAME**. Touch the buttons drawn on the screen.
- **In a web browser**, open the playground address you were given, choose **CONTENT** or **CONTEXT**, and press
  **PLAY**. On a computer the arrow keys walk, [X]{.btn .key} is A, [Z]{.btn .key} is B and [Enter]{.btn .key} is
  START.

# Playing {#play .t-GROWTH}

1. **Start.** Choose **NEW GAME**. CRYSTAL CLEAR, a scientist, says hello: press [A]{.btn} after each line (a small
   red ▼ means there is more). She asks whether you follow **REASON** or **INSTINCT** -- there is no wrong answer --
   and then your name.
2. **Walk and talk.** The cross walks; walk into a door to go through it. Face someone and press [A]{.btn} to talk.
3. **Your first DAEMON.** In CRYSTAL CLEAR's lab, choose one of three. Any of them can go all the way.
4. **Battles.** In long grass, a wild DAEMON (the game says **UNBOUND**) may jump out. Choose **ROUTINES**, then a
   routine, with [A]{.btn}. The green bar is health: empty theirs to win. **DETACH** runs away. A DAEMON whose bar
   empties has **HALTED**, and needs a rest.
5. **New friends.** Weaken a wild one, then choose **POOL** and offer it a **USERBOX**. If it stays in the box, it is
   yours: that is **BINDING**.
6. **Rest.** In a **CHECKPOINT**, talk at the counter and answer **YES**: every DAEMON is well again, free.
7. **Save.** Press [START]{.btn .pill}, choose **SAVE**, answer **YES**. The game never saves by itself. Next time,
   choose **CONTINUE**.
