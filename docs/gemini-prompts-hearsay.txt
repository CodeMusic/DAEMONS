HOLT and VERA — the HEARSAY portraits
=====================================

64x64 full-body figures, the same shape as a trainer front pic: the whole
person, filling the height of a square frame, on flat green. Crystal's is
already cut from her intro art and needs nothing.

Save as gfx/characters/holt.jpeg and vera.jpeg. tools/gbachar.py does the
reduction -- no sizes in the prompt.

THE FABLE RULE, vision 9.4, applies as it does to the rest of the cast:

  1. INTEGRATED, never grafted -- paw hands, a tail, a head proportioned to
     the body. Never an animal head on a human body.
  2. DRAWN PLAINLY -- flat colour, cel shading, NO fur texture, no gloss, no
     musculature. This is what separates fable from furry.
  3. HEAD-TO-BODY RATIO CARRIES AGE. Left unstated everyone comes out cute.

HOLT -- an otter, and the name chose that before anyone asked
==========================================================================
4.20a: a holt is a den, one letter from HOLD, which is the storage verb. And
a holt is specifically an OTTER's den -- so the species was in the name from
the start. An otter is at home in two elements, which is the man who held two
perspectives in one address and could not be resolved back out.

He built the box system. Everything the player ever stores is inside
something he made, and he is the only person in the world who has been the
thing in the box. He does not brood about it. He is cheerful, precise, and
slightly too pleased to have a visitor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full-body character in the style of a Game Boy Advance RPG sprite. Flat cel
shading, thick dark outline, no gradients, no anti-aliasing. Plain flat green
background, no scenery, no shadow under the feet.

Subject: an adult male OTTER, an engineer in his thirties, drawn in the
classic cartoon funny-animal tradition -- animals through and through, built
from simple confident shapes, the style of Carl Barks or Disney's ROBIN HOOD.

Adult proportions: a head of ordinary size relative to his body, not large.
Warm dark brown, with a paler throat and muzzle. A blunt friendly face,
small round ears set low, bright attentive eyes. Simple rounded PAW hands. A
thick flat otter tail.

He is cheerful and slightly rumpled -- a man who has been working alone for
a long time and is genuinely glad someone came. Not sad, not haunted.

DRAWN PLAINLY. No fur texture, no individual hairs, no tufts, no gloss, no
muscle definition. Flat solid colour with simple cel shading.

Clothes: a practical dark jumpsuit with the sleeves pushed up, a many-pocketed
utility belt, soft indoor shoes.

Pose: standing, weight on one leg, one hand raised in a small greeting, the
other holding a coil of cable against his hip. Full body, feet included, in a
SQUARE frame. Under about fifteen colours.

VERA CLEAR -- a fox, and the third of the three
==========================================================================
2569: "Gran reads them with an instrument. I just look." She is CRYSTAL's
granddaughter and AL's sister, and her whole character is unmediated
looking -- she can tell how a daemon feels about you by watching it.

ATTACH gfx/characters/crystal_speech.jpeg AS THE FAMILY REFERENCE. 1619 wants
three foxes a palette apart, readable as family with no text: Crystal is
light-to-mid and silvered, AL is russet and between, and VERA is the one who
has not weathered yet.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Using the attached image as the family reference, draw a DIFFERENT character
in the same style and tradition: a young female FOX of about eighteen, her
GRANDDAUGHTER. Same flat plain rendering, same plain green background.

She is a fox all over -- warm gold, lighter than the reference, no silver.
Rounded PAW hands, a fox tail held close rather than a large brush. An adult
head-to-body ratio, not a child's. The same ear and muzzle geometry as the
reference, because they are family.

Calm, unhurried, and looking directly at the viewer -- she is the one who
watches things properly. Not shy, not perky.

DRAWN PLAINLY. No fur texture, no individual hairs, no fluff, no muscle
definition. Flat solid colour with simple cel shading.

Clothes: a plain long apron over a simple shirt, sleeves rolled, comfortable
shoes. Working clothes, well kept.

Pose: standing at ease, hands loosely together in front of her, head slightly
tilted as though she has been looking at something for a while. Full body,
feet included, in a SQUARE frame. Under about fifteen colours.
