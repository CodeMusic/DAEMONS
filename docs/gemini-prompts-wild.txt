GEMINI PROMPTS - the nine wild daemons
======================================================================

All nine are 40x40 fronts and 32x32 backs.

Paste one front at a time. Ask for a large flat image, never pixel art.
Then ask for the back IN THE SAME CONVERSATION, using the follow-up below -
a separate conversation invents a different creature.

Save to gfx/front/<name>.png and gfx/back/<name>-back.png, lowercase.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
NIBBLE  (front, becomes 40x40)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

A small rodent, low to the ground and quick-looking, three-quarter view from the side, standing alert with its head turned toward the viewer. Along its back are four small square plates in a row, evenly spaced and clearly separate. Two large front teeth, small round ears, and a thin bare tail. Draw it as flat illustration with a hard pure black (#000000) outline, 6 to 8 pixels thick, around the whole creature. Inside the outline use exactly four flat tones - pure black, dark grey, light grey, pure white - with hard edges between them, no gradients, no anti-aliasing, no glow, no colour, no texture. Plain pure-white background, nothing behind it, no ground shadow. Full body visible with a small even margin.

======================================================================
THE BACK FOLLOW-UP
======================================================================

Ask this straight after the front, in the same conversation:

  Now draw the exact same creature seen directly from behind, facing away
  from the viewer. Same style, same outline weight, same four flat tones,
  same plain pure-white background, no ground shadow. Keep it simpler and
  chunkier than the front - this one will be seen very small. What should
  read clearly from behind is [X].

and [X] per creature:

  NIBBLE     the four square plates down its back, the ears, and the thin tail
  PACKET     the folded wings and rounded back - the envelope is hidden in front of it
  PING       the swept-back wings, the single upright crest feather, and the tail
  CRAWLER    the eight body segments and the thin thread trailing away behind it
  PENDING    the smooth blank back of the capsule, unmarked
  SCRAPER    the eight segments and the bundle it is dragging - the hook is hidden in front
  BUFFER     the bulging strained shell and the hairline split running down it
  SPIKE      the two large ears and the zigzag tail, which is the whole silhouette
  SUSPEND    the round back, the two tiny feet, and the single curl of hair on top

Two notes.

SPIKE's zigzag tail is the whole silhouette - if the generator softens it
into a curve, say so and ask again. A curve is not a spike.

PENDING and BUFFER are the same object twice, one intact and one strained.
Generate PENDING first and attach it when asking for BUFFER, so the shell
matches and only the bulge differs.
