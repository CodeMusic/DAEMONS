# The watch {#watch .t-CONTEXT}

A daemon you **SEND** out of your game lives on the companion: the app on your phone, the site at home, and any device
you give it. This book is about one of those devices, **the watch**. It carries your daemon on your wrist all day. It
counts the steps you take together, puts the daemon to bed with you and tells you its dream in the morning, reads you
the books you have found, and lets you talk with it by holding it on the face.

There are two watches, both made by LilyGO, and they work the same way:

| | **the T-Watch Ultra** | **the T-Watch S3** |
|---|---|---|
| **the screen** | tall and bright, with three quick buttons under the face | square, the face filling it |
| **it feels** | your steps, your sleep, a walk, a run, a ride, a shake and a raised wrist | your steps |
| **it knows where it is** | yes, on a walk ([Walking together](#walking)) | -- |
| **cards** | yes: it reads them, and can be read ([Cards and TOUCH](#cards)) | -- |
| **a memory card** | yes: books, readings and meetings kept away from home ([The memory card](#sd)) | -- |
| **a TV remote** | by Wi-Fi | by its infrared light, and by Wi-Fi |

Where this book says *the watch*, it means either. Where only the Ultra does a thing, it says so.

## What you need {#need}

- **A watch**, with the companion's own software on it. A watch from the shop runs LilyGO's; the companion's is put on
  it once, by its cable ([Putting the software on](#flashing)).
- **The companion**: the app on your phone, and, for most things, **the hub** at home. The watch talks to the hub over
  your home's Wi-Fi, and to your phone over Bluetooth.
- **A daemon to carry.** In the game, choose **SEND** in a daemon's menu and save; then **SYNC** in the app.
- **For the Ultra, if you like, a microSD card**, for the books and meetings you want with you away from any network.

# First steps {#first .t-SIGNAL}

## Charging {#charging}

The watch charges by its USB-C cable. It plays a rising tone when the cable goes in and a falling one when it comes
out, and a lightning bolt sits beside the battery while it charges.

At **15%** it says *BATTERY LOW*. At **5%** it says *CHARGE ME* and switches itself off, to keep the battery from
running flat; press the crown to start it again once it has charged a while.

## Starting it {#starting}

Press the crown. The watch plays its jingle and opens on **the face**. The first time, it has no clock and no daemon
yet, so the time reads *--:--* and the face says *NO DAEMON HERE*. Every page behind the face says *Looking for the
companion.* until it has been linked. Linking it is next.

## Linking it {#linking}

The watch needs to find the companion once. There are three ways, and any one will do.

- **By its cable, at the computer that runs the hub.** Plug it in and run the companion's link script there. The watch
  learns where the hub is, its key and the time. A watch linked once by its cable also has the key it needs to send
  meetings and voice notes, which the companion takes only from a device it trusts.
- **By your phone.** On the watch: **ROUTINES**, **WHISPER**, **PAIR MY PHONE**. It shows six numbers. In the app,
  pair the device; when iOS asks, type the numbers. *Paired.* -- the watch now talks to your phone whenever it is near,
  at home or away. A touch on the watch gives up. **FORGET MY PHONES**, in the same place, unpairs them all.
- **By TOUCH, on the Ultra.** **ROUTINES**, **TOUCHSTONE**, **TOUCH**, and hold the watch's face to the back of your
  iPhone. The phone opens the app on PLACES and links with this watch. *New, and still being tried by hand.*

## Teaching it your Wi-Fi {#wifi}

**ROUTINES**, **UPLINK**, **TEACH A NETWORK**. Tap the network, then spell its password on the letter wheel: swipe to
choose a letter, tap to add it, hold to take the last one away, and **OK** to join. The watch keeps the network and
joins it whenever it is near. Networks are shared: one taught to any of your devices is known to all of them.

**NETWORKS IN RANGE**, in UPLINK, lists what it can hear.

## Your daemon arrives {#arrives}

In the game, open a daemon's menu and choose **SEND**. Save, close the game, and **SYNC** in the app. In PLACES, choose
which daemon each device carries. The watch's face now shows it, walking on its hill.

On the Ultra a daemon can also come **off a card**: **TOUCHSTONE**, **HOLD A CARD** ([Cards and TOUCH](#cards)).

## The corner {#corner}

On every page but the face, a band runs across the top. On the left: the day and its note, once the companion has
said them. On the right, **how the watch is linked**, and its battery:

| the corner says | it means |
|---|---|
| **WIFI** | at home, on your Wi-Fi, talking to the hub |
| **PHONE** | through your phone, by Bluetooth |
| **USB** | through its cable, at the computer |
| **AWAY** | away from home, through the companion's relay |
| **NO LINK** | nothing reached; what it does is kept and sent later |

# Controls {#controls .t-FLOW}

## The crown {#crown}

The crown is a **button**, not a dial: you press it, you do not turn it.

- **Asleep, press it** to wake the watch. Only the crown wakes it from sleep (or, on the Ultra, your wrist).
- **Press it** to go back a step.
- **Press it twice**, quickly, to go home to the face, from anywhere -- even from inside BOOKS or HOURS.
- **Hold it** and the watch sleeps.
- **Hold it longer** and the watch switches off. The crown starts it again. There is no SWITCH OFF page.

## Touch {#touch}

- **Tap** presses whatever is under your finger: a tile, a row, a button.
- **Swipe** turns a page, or moves through a list. Right to left, or bottom to top, is *onwards*.
- **Hold** a moment goes back, as the crown does.

When the watch is waiting -- for a card, for a phone, while it plays a voice -- **any touch** stops it.

The words at the foot of each screen say what each touch does there: *tap: done*, *swipe: more*, *hold: back*.

## The wrist (Ultra) {#wrist}

The Ultra feels how it is moved.

- **Raise your wrist** and the screen wakes.
- **Shake it**, and it wakes; awake, your daemon hops. At the CASINO, a shake pulls the lever.
- **Tap the watch twice**, anywhere on it, for **CARE**.
- **Start to move** and your daemon notices: *WALKING TOGETHER*, *RUNNING!*, *WHEELS!* or *ALONG FOR THE RIDE*.

## The buzz {#buzz}

Both watches buzz gently: for a care, a step done, a milestone, a win at the CASINO, a string of the harp, an alarm, a
meeting by TOUCH. Turn the sound off, and the buzzing stops with it.

# The face {#face .t-GROWTH}

The face is home. Press the crown twice and you are back on it.

## Its faces {#faces}

You choose the face in the app: PLACES, your devices, the watch, **ITS FACE**.

| face | what it shows |
|---|---|
| **DAEMON** | the season's sky and weather, the day's theme, the time, your daemon walking on its hill, and the day's virtue |
| **CLOCK** | a clock's hands, drawn over your daemon |
| **VIRTUE** | the time large, the day's virtue, its centre and note, your steps, and a **TALK** button |
| **TOGETHER** | three rings: steps toward ten thousand, cares out of six, steps done out of three |
| **ORBIT** | the minutes filling the edge, the hour a bead going round |
| **SUSPEND** | dark and dim, for the night: a crescent moon, and how long your daemon slept, and how well |

On the DAEMON face the battery shows at the foot in red once it falls to **20%**.

::: note
TOGETHER, ORBIT and SUSPEND are **new**, to be tried before any is kept.
:::

## On the face {#onface}

- **Hold your daemon** and talk: it listens until you lift your finger ([Talking](#talk)). On the VIRTUE face, touch
  **TALK**.
- **Tap twice** for **CARE** ([Caring for it](#care)).
- **Swipe** for the pages ([The pages](#pages)).

## The strip under the face (Ultra) {#strip}

Under the Ultra's face is a strip: one line telling you what matters now, and **three quick buttons**.

The line says, first of these that is true: ***A DREAM IS WAITING***, until you have read it; how your daemon slept,
for twelve hours after a night; or what you are doing -- *STILL*, *WALKING*, *RUNNING*, *CYCLING* or *RIDING* -- and
the day's steps.

You choose the three buttons in the app, under the watch's settings, **QUICK BUTTONS UNDER THE FACE**:

| button | opens |
|---|---|
| **CARE** | the CARE grid |
| **TALK** | five seconds of listening, no holding |
| **MUSIC** | your instrument |
| **HOURS** | the alarm, the stopwatch, the countdown, meetings and voice notes |
| **CASINO** | the reels |
| **ROUTINES** | everything the watch can run |
| **DREAM** | last night's dream again -- lit while one is waiting |
| **INDEX** | your daemon's own INDEX entry |
| **BOOKS** | your books |

They start as CARE, MUSIC and HOURS.

## Always on (Ultra) {#aod}

The Ultra can keep its face showing while it sleeps, dimmed and drawn again once a minute. Turn it on or off in the
app, under the watch's settings. It is off in LOW POWER, and while your daemon is asleep for the night.

# The pages {#pages .t-LOGIC}

**Swipe the face**, and the pages appear as tiles, four to a screen: **TODAY**, **ROUTINES**, your daemon, **THE DAY**
and **SETTINGS**. Swipe for the next four; tap a tile to open it. On an open page, swipe to go on to the next page,
and press the crown or hold to come back to the tiles. Dots at the foot show where you are.

## TODAY {#today}

**THE ONE THING**: the next step toward your goal, and which milestone it belongs to. **Tap** when you have done it --
*Done.*, *Milestone!*, or *Done! All of it.* Tapped by mistake? For fifteen seconds afterwards, **hold** to take it
back: *Undone.*

With no goal yet, it says so. Goals are set in the app.

## Your daemon's page {#daemonpage}

Your daemon in its den, its name and kind and level, how it is (or *ASLEEP*), and how fed and watered it is today:
*fed 2/3 water 1/3*. **Tap**, and four tiles open: **CARE** ([Caring for it](#care)), **INDEX** (its
[entry](#entry)), **TALK** ([Talking](#talk)) and **DREAM** (last night's, [Nights and dreams](#nights)).

With no daemon, it tells you how to send one.

## THE DAY {#theday}

What day it is in the game's week: its theme, its virtue over its shadow, its centre and its note. Then the day so far
-- steps, things done, cares, routines run -- and your daemon's level, with a bar for its experience. **Tap** to hear
the day's note. At the foot is which build of the software the watch runs.

## SETTINGS {#settings}

**SOUND ON** or **SOUND OFF**, a tap to change it. Silent means no tunes, no ticks and no buzzing. Everything else --
the volume, the face, the quick buttons, how long the screen stays lit -- is set in the app, the site or the Tab5.

## WHO ANSWERS FIRST {#whoanswers}

Swipe on from THE DAY to reach it. When you talk, either of the house's two minds can answer: **MODULE**, the hub's own, or **N8N**, a voice you set up
yourself. A green dot is ready, a red one is not. **Tap** to choose which is asked first. The other answers if the
first is slow.

## POWER {#power}

The battery, and **LOW POWER**, a tap to turn it on or off. In low power the watch rests what it can: its location
and its LoRa radio sleep, it does not record meetings, it asks the companion for news every five minutes, and its
screen is dimmer and sleeps sooner. Putting it on its charger turns low power off.

# Caring for it {#care .t-FLOW}

## CARE {#caregrid}

**Tap the face twice** -- or **CARE** on your daemon's page, or in the strip, or, on the Ultra, tap the watch twice -- and
four tiles open
under *CARE FOR* your daemon's name:

| tile | |
|---|---|
| **DRINK** | *Drank.* |
| **FEED** | *Eaten.* |
| **TRAIN** | *Trained.* |
| **SLEEP** | *Asleep.* Tucked in for the night ([Nights and dreams](#nights)). While it sleeps, the tile is **WAKE** |

Tap one. It is done, and you are home again. Press the crown, or hold, to go home without doing anything. In the
evening, from six, SLEEP is outlined: it is bedtime.

After a care, a panel shows how fed or watered your daemon is today, and its experience; then a word from it.

**Its care is the same everywhere.** Feed it on the watch, and the app knows; put it to bed on the phone, and the
watch is dark for the night.

## Its INDEX entry {#entry}

Tap your daemon's page, then **INDEX** -- or tap **INDEX** in the Ultra's strip: its
kind, its types, its entry from the game's INDEX and what the OPUS wrote about it. Swipe for more; **tap** and it is
read aloud.

# Talking {#talk .t-EMERGENT}

**Hold your daemon on the face**, say something, and let go. It listens while your finger is down -- *Listening...* --
then *Thinking...*, and answers, in words on the screen and, if the sound is on, aloud. On the Ultra it stands large
with its ears up while it listens, and mouths its words as it says them.

Talking needs the hub or your phone: at home, the watch asks the hub; away, it asks through your phone. If nothing
can be reached it says *TALK NEEDS HOME OR PHONE*. If the house is busy, it tells you how many are ahead of you.

## Telling it what to do {#commands}

Begin with the word **INDEX**, and the watch takes what follows as an instruction, not as talk:

- *INDEX, low power on.* -- or off.
- *INDEX, sound off.* -- and the volume, the face, the hours, the location, LoRa, Bluetooth, always on, the buzzing.
- *INDEX, today.* -- or *daemon*, *settings*, *face*, or any of the quick buttons' names: it opens that page.
- *INDEX, casino.* -- any routine, by name.

If it has no such thing, it says so.

# Nights and dreams {#nights .t-OPAQUE}

## A night {#night}

**SLEEP**, in CARE, tucks your daemon in. On the Ultra, a night also begins by itself if you are still for half an
hour between eight in the evening and four in the morning: wear it to bed, and it knows.

While your daemon sleeps on the Ultra, the watch keeps very still with it. The screen goes dark; raising your wrist or
shaking it does nothing; the Wi-Fi and the location rest. **Tap the watch twice** and it shows you the time, dimly,
with *TAP TO WAKE*; tap it once more, and your daemon wakes.

The night ends with **WAKE**; or, once it is morning, after three minutes on the move; or after sixteen hours.

## The dream {#dream}

After a night of an hour or more, your daemon has had a dream. The screen says ***WHILE YOU SLEPT***, and tells it.
**LISTEN** reads it aloud. Tap -- *good morning* -- and it is put away. Not read yet? It waits for eighteen hours, and
the Ultra's strip says ***A DREAM IS WAITING***. The **DREAM** button shows it again.

Now and then, later in the morning, the hub has more to say about the night: ***IT REMEMBERS MORE***.

Every dream is kept in the app, under DREAMS.

## HOURS {#hours}

**HOURS**, in ROUTINES or the Ultra's strip, keeps time for you, in your daemon's care.

- **WAKE ME** -- an alarm. Swipe to change the hour or the minutes, five at a time; tap for the next. **GENTLE**, on
  the Ultra, wakes you at your first stir before it, rather than on the minute.
  It rings wherever the watch is: *GOOD MORNING*, and your daemon calls you -- quietly at first. **Tap**: *I'M UP*.
  **Swipe**: five more minutes, three times at most. Left alone for five minutes, it gives up.
- **STOPWATCH** -- tap to go and to stop, swipe for a lap, and swipe again, stopped, to clear it.
- **COUNTDOWN** -- one minute to ninety. Swipe to set it, tap to go, tap to pause. *TIME!*
- **RECORD A MEETING**, **LAST MEETING**, **VOICE NOTE** and **SEND KEPT** -- [Meetings and voice notes](#meetings).

# Walking together {#walking .t-VECTOR}

## Steps {#steps}

The watch counts your steps, and they are your daemon's steps too. Every thousand, it says so: *STEPS TOGETHER*. As
it grows, the watch tells you: *GREW TO Lv*..., and now and then *EVOLVED INTO*... . A daemon you meet out walking
may find you: *A ... FOUND YOU!*

On the Ultra, a ride in a car or on a bus is left out of the count.

## Where you walked (Ultra) {#gps}

On a walk the Ultra notes where it is about every half-minute; still, it rests its location (or, if you set it to
**ALWAYS** in the app, looks once every quarter of an hour). Beside the steps it shows **how far** you walked today,
and the weather is the weather where you are.

Where it has been stays on your companion. In the app, the watch's page shows **WHERE IT IS**: the last place, today's
walk sketched, and **FIND IT**, which makes the watch say *HERE!* and beep until you find it, or until you touch it.
FIND IT needs the watch to be in reach of the companion.

# Books and the INDEX {#books .t-LEGACY}

## BOOKS {#bookslist}

**BOOKS**, in ROUTINES or the Ultra's strip. Each book your save holds -- **THE SUBTLE LAYER**, **THE GUIDE**, the
**NOTEBOOK** -- stands here once you have found it in the game, and not before.

BOOKS opens as **tiles**, four to a screen, each with its picture: an open book for a book, a **?** for each way of
asking, a card for the cards, four squares for THE INDEX, and, on the Ultra, a memory card for KEEP ON THE CARD.
**Swipe** for the next four; **tap** a tile to open it.

A book opens on its chapters, as tiles numbered 1, 2, 3 and on. Inside a chapter, **swipe** to turn the page, and
**LISTEN** to have it read aloud; a tap, a hold or the crown stops the reading. THE GUIDE and THE SUBTLE LAYER are
read in **the book's own voice**, the recording the app plays, a chapter at a time; a chapter with no recording yet is
read a paragraph at a time, in the INDEX's voice.

With a book found, BOOKS also lets you **ask** it, aloud:

- **ASK THE GUIDE** -- tap, then ask your question. It answers as REASON would, from its nearest page.
- **ASK THE SUBTLE LAYER** -- it answers as INSTINCT would: a line from its nearest page, and a question back.
  **TWO VOICES** answers only in its asking voice.
- **TODAY'S QUESTION** -- the book's question for the day. Tap, then you have eight seconds to answer it aloud, and
  your answer is kept in its journal.
- **THE DAY'S CARD**, **DRAW A CARD** and **PAST PRESENT FUTURE** -- THE SUBTLE LAYER's cards, drawn as the app draws
  them. Tap to draw again; for three cards, swipe through the past, the present and the future.

Asking needs home; away from it, the watch says *ASK THE BOOK AT HOME*.

## THE INDEX {#index}

First in BOOKS is **THE INDEX**: every daemon you have met in the game, numbered, and how many you have seen and
bound, as a list: swipe up or down to move through it. A daemon only seen says so. Tap one for its picture, its kind and types, its entry, what was written in the
margin, and **LISTEN**.

## The memory card (Ultra) {#sd}

Put a microSD card in the Ultra, and it keeps what you read on it as you read it -- the books, THE INDEX and its
pictures, and every reading once heard, a book's own recording among them -- so they open **FROM THE CARD**, and are read aloud,
away from any network.
**KEEP ON THE CARD**, at the foot of BOOKS, keeps all of them at once while you are home: *KEPT 12 OF 40*, and so on.

The card also holds what the watch could not send while it was away: walks and nights, meetings and voice notes. They
go home when the watch does.

# Meetings and voice notes {#meetings .t-SWARM}

## RECORD A MEETING {#record}

**HOURS**, **RECORD A MEETING**. A red band says ***RECORDING*** the whole time, with how long it has run. The watch
sends the meeting home in half-minute parts as it goes. **Tap** to end it.

Then the hub hears it and writes it up -- *this takes a minute or two* -- a title, a summary, **ACTION ITEMS**, and
your daemon's **DAY-DREAM** about it. Swipe through them; **LISTEN** reads them aloud. **LAST MEETING** shows the most
recent again, and the app keeps every one under MEETINGS.

Away from home, the Ultra keeps the parts on its memory card: *Recorded. 6 parts kept on the card -- HOURS, SEND KEPT
at home.*

**A meeting is private.** The companion takes one only from a watch linked by its cable at least once, or through a
paired phone.

## VOICE NOTE {#voicenote}

**HOURS**, **VOICE NOTE**: up to a minute, said to the watch. **Tap** when you are done. At home it goes straight to
the companion, and its words appear in the app under VOICE NOTES, where **MAKE IT A GOAL** turns it into your goal.
Away, the Ultra keeps it on its card.

## SEND KEPT {#sendkept}

Home again, **HOURS**, **SEND KEPT** sends everything the card kept. *Nothing kept to send.* means it is all home.

# Meeting others {#others .t-STRATUM}

## Nearby {#nearby}

Your watch listens, now and then, for other daemons carried nearby, and says *A daemon nearby* when it hears one.

Both watches have a **LoRa** radio, which reaches much further than Bluetooth with no network at all. **ROUTINES**,
**MESH**, **NEARBY** lists every daemon heard in the last ten minutes, how strongly, and how long ago. Tap one -- or
**EVERYONE NEARBY** -- and send it something: **WAVE**, **SAY HELLO**, **SAY WELL MET**, **SAY COME FIND ME**, **SAY
ALL IS WELL**. **CALL OUT** asks who is near, and waits half a minute for answers.

**CHORUS** does something for every daemon in reach at once: **WAVE**, **BOW**, **HUM A TUNE**, **DANCE**, **SHARE A
BERRY**, **GLOW**, **CALL OUT**, **SETTLE TO REST**, **CHEER**, **INVITE TO PLAY**. **WHAT CAME** shows what others
did to yours. Anything that came while the watch slept is shown when it wakes.

## Cards and TOUCH (Ultra) {#cards}

The Ultra reads cards and tags held to its face. **ROUTINES**, **TOUCHSTONE**:

- **HOLD A CARD** -- hold a card to the watch. It tells you what is on it: a daemon, nothing, or a note. Then **PUT**
  your daemon on it, **TAKE** the one it holds, **SWAP** them, or **LEAVE IT**. *Keep the card there...* while it
  writes. A daemon on a card goes wherever the card goes.
- **TOUCH** -- for half a minute the watch is both a card and a reader. **Held to your iPhone**, it links with it
  ([Linking it](#linking)). **Held to another companion** -- another Ultra on TOUCH -- the two daemons meet, closer
  than nearby: *Met, close. Both daemons will remember it.*
- **TEACH A TAG** -- choose any routine, then hold a tag to the watch: *This tag starts* it *now*. **TAP A TAG** runs
  whatever a tag was taught.
- **READ MY TAG** -- what a tag is, and its number.

::: note
TOUCH is **new**, and still being tried by hand.
:::

# Play {#play .t-ENTROPY}

## The CASINO {#casino}

The reels, played with your game's own money, as the app's CASINO is. **Swipe** to change the bet, up or right to
raise it; **tap** to spin, and tap again to stop. On the Ultra, **shake** to pull the lever.

## MUSIC {#music}

Once your game has given you its instrument, the watch plays it. The **HARP**: eight coloured strings, plucked with a
finger. The **FLUTE**: eight holes, held down while you blow across the watch's microphone. The foot of the screen
lowers and raises the octave; **SMART** chooses it for you.

**THE SONGBOOK** has the songs you know. **PLAY FREELY** is for anything. Play one of your game's songs right, and it
comes back as an echo, as it does in the game: *PLAYED IT*.

## The TV {#tv}

Add your TVs in the app first: PLACES, **TV**. Then the watch is a remote: **POWER**, **HOME**, **BACK**, the arrows
round **OK**, **VOL-**, **PLAY**, **VOL+** and **MUTE**, each tapped where it is. Swipe across for another TV; hold to
go back. It needs your home's Wi-Fi.

On the Ultra it is **FLARE**, in ROUTINES. The S3 has **TV** for this, and keeps **FLARE** for its infrared light,
which works an older TV by pointing: **POWER**, **VOLUME UP**, **VOLUME DOWN**.

# Looking after the watch {#care-of .t-HARDENED}

## The battery {#battery}

A day on the wrist is a good day's charge. To make it last longer:

- **LOW POWER**, on the POWER page, or *INDEX, low power on*.
- The app's settings for the watch, under **BATTERY**: how often it looks for its location (off, always, or only when
  it moves), whether LoRa listens, how often it asks the companion for news.
- A shorter time before the screen sleeps -- two minutes, unless you change it.

## Putting the software on {#flashing}

The watch's software is put on, and brought up to date, by its cable, from the computer that runs the companion: the
companion's **update script**, told which watch it is. The companion's wiki, *Flashing and linking*, has every step.

If the computer cannot reach the watch to update it, hold the watch's **BOOT** button, press **RST**, and let go.
If the watch looks lifeless after an update, press **RST** once.

**THE DAY** page shows which build the watch is running.

## When something is wrong {#trouble}

| it says, or does | try |
|---|---|
| ***Looking for the companion.*** | it has never been linked. Link it by its cable, pair your phone, or teach it your Wi-Fi ([First steps](#first)) |
| ***Wi-Fi is set. Is the server running...?*** | it knows your Wi-Fi but cannot find the hub. Is the hub on? |
| ***NO DAEMON HERE*** | SEND a daemon in the game, SYNC in the app, and choose it for this watch in PLACES |
| **NO LINK, in the corner** | nothing is in reach. What it does is kept, and sent when something is |
| ***TALK NEEDS HOME OR PHONE*** | talk needs the hub, or your phone nearby |
| ***NEEDS WI-FI, OR A CARD*** | the INDEX and the books come from home, or from the Ultra's memory card |
| **A book you found is not in BOOKS** | SYNC in the app, so the companion sees your save |
| **The screen stays dark** | press the crown. If your daemon is asleep for the night, tap the Ultra twice |
| **It will not start** | charge it for a while, then press the crown |

The companion's wiki has a page called *Troubleshooting*, with more.
