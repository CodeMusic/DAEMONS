GEMINI PROMPTS - the eight evolutions
======================================================================

These complete the six wild lines. Sizes differ - each block says which.

IMPORTANT: attach the earlier stage's image with each of these. Every one is
a grown form of something already drawn, and a fresh conversation invents an
unrelated creature.

Save to gfx/front/<name>.png and gfx/back/<name>-back.png, lowercase.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERFLOW  (front, becomes 48x48)   -- attach NIBBLE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

A larger, heavier rodent, three-quarter view from the side, standing squarely with its head turned toward the viewer. It is the grown form of a smaller rodent that had four square plates along its back - now there are far too many, crowded and overlapping, spilling past its shoulders and down its flanks in a jumble. Big front teeth, small round ears, a thick tail. Draw it as flat illustration with a hard pure black (#000000) outline, 6 to 8 pixels thick, around the whole creature. Inside the outline use exactly four flat tones - pure black, dark grey, light grey, pure white - with hard edges between them, no gradients, no anti-aliasing, no glow, no colour, no texture. Plain pure-white background, nothing behind it, no ground shadow. Full body visible with a small even margin.

======================================================================
THE BACK FOLLOW-UP
======================================================================

Ask straight after the front, in the same conversation:

  Now draw the exact same creature seen directly from behind, facing away
  from the viewer. Same style, same outline weight, same four flat tones,
  same plain pure-white background, no ground shadow. Keep it simpler and
  chunkier than the front - this one will be seen very small. What should
  read clearly from behind is [X].

and [X] per creature:

  OVERFLOW    the crowded overlapping plates spilling down its back, and the thick tail
  RELAY       the folded longer wings and the crest - the envelopes are hidden in front
  BROADCAST   the wings spread wide from behind, the fanned tail, and the swept crest
  FLOOD       the half-raised swept-back wings, the ragged crest, and the talons
  INDEXER     the four grid-panelled wings, which are the whole silhouette
  INJECTOR    the blurred wings and the tail spike - the needles are hidden in front
  SURGE       the two large ears and the long rising-and-falling zigzag tail
  HIBERNATE   the round back, the long droopy ears, and the curl of hair on top

Three notes.

INDEXER and INJECTOR are the same body twice - one ordered, one armed. Draw
INDEXER first and attach it when asking for INJECTOR, so only the forelimbs
and the bearing differ. That contrast is the CORRUPT typing.

SURGE's tail must never return to rest. SPIKE's rises to one point and stops;
SURGE's keeps going. If it comes back as a single peak, ask again.

BROADCAST carries nothing. PACKET held one envelope and RELAY holds three -
the joke is that the last one has let go of all of them.
