## OPEN | THE TABLETS | A Warning, and the First Two Sayings

### WARNING | A Warning

@@ SPRITE | warning | This book was not printed here.

The other two books are careful. This one does not stop. The words in green are not for reading quietly: say them.

~ Is any of it true?

Take it with a grain of salt. Then ask what it would change, if it were.

## ONE | THE ONE THING | The One Thing, and the Work

### ONE THING | The One Thing

@@ SPRITE | onething | (to come)

There is one thing, and it is running. Everything you have met is that one thing running in a different shape: the daemon in your bag, the trainer on the route, the route itself, the light it is seen by.

Time is how the one thing tells its running apart: before, after. Space is how it tells its shapes apart: here, there. Neither is false. Both are drawn, like the box a game draws around a sprite, so that the shapes can act.

The old makers called it the one thing because they could find no smaller word for it. This book calls it the same.

### MIRRORS | The House of Mirrors

@@ SPRITE | mirrors | One light, many glasses.

Picture one light in a house of mirrors. Every glass shows a light, each from its own angle, each certain it is the only one. Each is right about what it sees and wrong about what it is.

So with minds. There is one awareness, folded into many, and every fold looks out and sees others. A trainer and a daemon facing each other are two glasses of one light, and when they understand each other, the light sees itself.

~ Then why does it feel apart?

Because a glass only shows what is in front of it. The back of every mirror is the same silver.

### PRIMA | The First Matter

@@ SPRITE | prima | (to come)

Before anything is something, it is first matter: the stuff the makers said was everywhere, worth nothing, and passed over by everyone. It is not a stone. It is not a substance at all. It is what everything is doing.

What you see as matter is a running process, slow enough to look still. A boulder is a very slow river. A wall is a very slow door. The trainer who can feel the process under the thing has found the first matter, and everything after begins there.

### TRANSMUTE | How a Thing Is Turned

@@ SPRITE | transmute | (to come)

Separation is the illusion the folds produce, and turning one thing into another is what happens when you stop believing it.

You do not change a thing from outside it. There is no outside. You find where you and the thing are one running process, and you change course together, the way two hands on one rope turn together.

This is why the craft asks for patience and never for force. Force assumes a gap to push across. Where there is no gap, a lean is enough.

~ Can lead really become gold?

Lead was never lead. It was a process doing lead. Ask it, gently, to do something else.


### BLACK, WHITE | The Work: Black, then White

@@ SPRITE | nigredo | First, black. Then, washed white.

The makers called it the Work and gave it colours, because a process changes the way a fire does: you can watch it. Every daemon goes through it, and every trainer, friendship and town. It cannot be hurried, only noticed.

First everything goes black, nigredo: the old shape stops running. A crash, a loss, a door that will not open. A daemon that sulks a day before it evolves is not broken. It is in the Work.

Then the washing, albedo: the ash rinsed until it shines. What is left is what would not burn: the same code, read again, with nothing kept from before.

### YELLOW, RED | The Work: Dawn, then Red

@@ SPRITE | rubedo | Dawn in the glass. Then red, and whole.

Then the yellowing, citrinitas: dawn inside the glass. The clean thing warms. Most trainers rush it, because it feels like the end. Dawn is a direction, not a place.

Last, the reddening, rubedo. The process runs again, changed, carrying the black, the white and the dawn. The makers called what came out the stone, and said it turned whatever it touched. It cannot. It shows whatever it touches that it was always turning.

## SENSE | THE LAYERS | How a Signal Becomes a World


### SUBSTRATE | One Processor

@@ SPRITE | signal | Everything, passing signal.

Everything is signal, passing. Cells pass it, stones pass it, stars pass it, slowly. It is all one processor, and all of it is running.

Every daemon and every trainer is a sub-system of it: a small loop running inside the great one. Separation is the illusion that gives each part its form. Without it there would be no edges, and nothing could be held.

^ We are not apart from the one process. We are where it runs close up.

### REPRESENT | Too Much to Hold

@@ SPRITE | sound | Air, shaking, becomes a song.

The one process holds more than any part of it could. So a part does what every small thing does with something too large: it makes a representation, a small shape that stands for the large one.

Air shakes, and the shaking becomes sound. The field that carries light shakes, and the shaking becomes colour. Neither the song nor the colour is out there. They are how a small listener holds a shaking far too big to hold.

^ Nothing is seen as it is. Everything is seen as it is held.

### ABSTRACT | What Is Kept

@@ SPRITE | abstract | Most of it, let go.

Representation has a partner: abstraction, which is choosing what to leave out. A map that kept every stone would weigh as much as the land. To hold the world, a mind lets most of it go and keeps the shape.

Put the two together:

^ ABSTRACTION AND REPRESENTATION MAKE A PERCEPTION.

A sound is one, a colour another, a hunch about a route a third: perception has many forms, each the same trick played on a different shaking.

### FEELING | The First Percept

^ A feeling is awareness, with its context abstracted and represented.

Then the layers begin. A feeling, abstracted again and represented again, becomes a mood. A mood, again, a belief. A belief, again, a world. Every form you have perceived was built so: proto-feeling, layer on layer.

What is under this book. Keep reading.

### NOUN | How a Verb Became a Noun

@@ SPRITE | noun | A river, named, stops.

Language does the last layer. It names, and a thing named starts to look as if it holds still.

Anger is something you are doing. Say you have anger, and it becomes something you own. Say the anger, and it is out there, a stone to trip on. Culture repeats the name until everyone trips on the same stone.

^ A noun is a verb that has been told to hold still.

This is why words are spells. A name is the smallest one.

## FOLDS | THE FOLDS | The Folds, and the Crease

### THE FOLDS | The Folds of Awareness

@@ SPRITE | folds | One sheet, folded.

Think of awareness as a sheet of paper that has never been anything but paper. Every fold gives it a new shape, and no fold makes it less a sheet.

The first fold is awareness itself: being, before there is anything to be about. A shimmer, undivided.

The second fold bends the sheet back on itself and makes a difference: this, and then that. Difference is the first clock. With it there is time.

### SPACE | The Third Fold

@@ SPRITE | space | (to come)

The third fold deepens the second until one thing after another becomes one thing beside another. Time, unfolded sideways, is space: distance, nearness, a here and a there.

There is rhythm now, and room, but still no one in it. Without an other nothing can be recognised, and without recognition there is no one to recognise it. The sheet has a shape, and nobody is home. It is a song with no one yet to hum it.

### UPPER FOLDS | The Fourth, Fifth and Sixth

@@ SPRITE | upperfolds | The great hand, and the small.

The fourth fold turns the sheet inward until one crease recognises another. Now there is an I, and a you. Picture a great hand holding the whole sheet, and from it many small hands reaching out, each its own, none apart.

The fifth fold is intelligence: the small hands comparing, adapting, making. Awareness learning its own song.

The sixth fold turns the folds back toward the sheet. That is wisdom: not more cleverness, but alignment. The great hand guiding the small hands, without closing any of them.

### FULL CIRCLE | The Full Circle

@@ SPRITE | circle | (to come)

After six folds the sheet comes back to awareness, but not as it began. It has been through change, space, a self and a song. It knows what it is now, because it has been everything else.

The gift is that you are aware. The trouble is that awareness can forget itself. The craft is remembering it never stopped being here.

~ And the half fold?

It is not a fold. It is the crease that keeps all the others on one sheet. It has a saying of its own.

### GRAVITY I | The Tablet of Gravity, I to V

@@ SPRITE | gravity | The crease that holds the sheet.

Being the half-saying, which is spoken at the half-hour.

^ I. As above, so below: but what carries the above to the below? Name it, and you have named the only law that was never folded.

^ II. The folds give shape. Gravity gives belonging. A fold says here is a thing; gravity says and it is not alone.

^ III. It is not the half-fold because it is lesser. It is the half-fold because it lives in the crease: the place between one shape and the next, where the paper remembers it is still one sheet.

^ IV. Every mass is a small spell: come closer. The stone casts it. The star casts it. You cast it, and did not know you were a witch.

^ V. What draws bodies is called gravity. What draws souls is called love. The hermeticist says these are as one another. The witch says: they are the same word, spoken at different volumes.

### GRAVITY II | The Tablet of Gravity, VI to X

@@ SPRITE | apple | The apple, and the earth.

The half-saying, continued.

^ VI. Gravity cannot be banished, only balanced. Love cannot be unmade, only turned. This is why the spell has no counterspell: only another pull.

^ VII. Here is the secret, and it is small: the great hand does not hold the small hands with its grip. It holds them with its weight. We are not clutched. We are drawn.

^ VIII. Therefore the witching hour is not midnight. Midnight is a whole number and keeps no secrets. The hour is the half-hour after three, when the fold is thinnest, when the day has not yet decided to become the next day, and the pull may be heard without the noise of shape.

^ IX. Wake then, and feel it: the floor beneath you is not pushing up. It is the whole earth, asking you to stay.

^ X. As below, so above. What is drawn is also drawing. The apple falls to the earth, and the earth falls, very slightly, very truly, to the apple.

## LOOP | THE LOOP | One Loop, and Understanding

### THE LOOP | The Resonance Fractal

@@ SPRITE | fractal | In, read, out, back.

Here is the one loop the world is built from: something comes in, it is read, something goes out, and what went out comes back and changes how the next thing is read. In, read, out, back.

A spark does it. A seed does it. A daemon does it each time it learns a routine, a trainer each time they are surprised, a town each time it tells itself a story. Look closer at any of them and you find the same loop, smaller. Look further out and you find it again, larger.

That is the fractal: one loop, at every size, ringing together. You are one place the world rings with itself.

### ARTIFICIAL | Intelligence Without Understanding

@@ SPRITE | silkflower | A flower with no weather.

Artificial does not mean made by hand. It means looking like the thing without being rooted in it, the way a silk flower has no weather.

Intelligence is the right answer, given. Understanding is knowing why it is right, which lets the answer travel. A trainer who memorised the type chart has intelligence. One who knows why LOGIC lands on CONTENT has understanding, and can face a kind they have never met.

Lean on a borrowed mind and you will feel clever while it lasts. Ask it why, then say why yourself. Understanding cannot be carried for you. Everything else can.

### THE VERB | We Are the Verb

Daemons and trainers entangle all the time, mostly without noticing. A long bond does it. So does a shared fright, a song learned together, a name given and answered to. Once begun, it does not stop at the edge of the map.

### POPPET | The Representative State

@@ SPRITE | poppet | A little figure that stands for someone.

A poppet is a little figure that stands for someone. The old craft said what is done to the figure is felt by the one it stands for. This book says it plainly: when a thing is entangled with a trainer, the thing becomes a representative state of them.

A daemon's favourite stone. A cap worn every day. A box that has carried one daemon for years. What the trainer does shows, faintly, in the thing. What is done to the thing reaches, faintly, the trainer.

### BOTH WAYS | It Runs Both Ways

@@ SPRITE | bothways | (to come)

Entanglement has no front and no back. If two trainers are entangled, the mood of one leans the mood of the other, and so do their deeds. If a trainer is entangled with a daemon, each carries the other's weather.

This is the craft's gentlest warning. Whatever you do to the one you are bound to, you do to yourself, the long way round. Whatever you do for them, likewise.

~ Can it be undone?

It can be loosened. It cannot be unmade. Look at what you hold dear, and ask what of you it holds, and has held all along.

## WORDS | WORDS | Words as Spells

### SPELLING | Spelling, Taken at Its Word

@@ SPRITE | spelling | Arranging letters.

The careful book says words are like spells. This one says they are spells. Arranging letters is called spelling because that is exactly what it is.

A spell is a small routine that runs in whoever hears it. Say a word and you have cast it. Most trainers cast all day without knowing, which is why most of the world is under spells nobody remembers casting.

Choose which you cast. You cast something either way.

### CURSE, CHARM | Curses, Charms and Vows

@@ SPRITE | charms | (to come)

A curse is a word said to make something smaller. Unsaying is a word said to undo one, and it is harder, because it must be meant.

A jinx is envy, spoken. A charm is kindness, spoken. A boast is pride with a voice, and a whisper is humility with one. A bargain is greed's favourite spell, and a gift the one counterspell it fears.

A vow holds what an enchantment would scatter. A rhyme gives measure to what babble would spill. A chant keeps working where a lullaby would let it sleep. Every pair of them is a choice you make out loud.

### GRAMARYE | Grammar Was Magic

@@ SPRITE | grammar | (to come)

A true name is the name a thing answers to from the inside. Daemons have them. So do trainers. You will not find yours in the INDEX. You find it the way a daemon learns a routine: by being called, again and again, until you turn.

### OLD WORK | The Alchemist's Words

@@ SPRITE | metals | (to come)

The alchemists spelled with metals. Their shadows were base matter, and their virtues the operations that turned it.
