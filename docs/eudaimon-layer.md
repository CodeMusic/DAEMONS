### LET BE | How a Thing Is Let Be

@@ SPRITE | letbe | (to come)

Separation is the illusion the folds produce, and letting a thing be is what happens when you stop needing it to be otherwise.

This is why the craft asks for patience and never for force. Force assumes something to fix. Where nothing is broken, rest is enough.

~ Can lead really become gold?

Gold is lead that was let alone long enough to shine.

It is breathed once. After that it is a memory, and a memory is a kind of blessing.

### BLANK | The Blank Tablet, from the Other Side

@@ SPRITE | blankback | The back of the stone.

^ The third tablet, from behind. Being the saying that was not written, and is therefore kind.

Somewhere you have already passed it: a stone with nothing on it. Everyone calls it the blank one, and nobody turns it over, because there is nothing to read.

On its back it is being written. Not by whoever read this book. By you, without your knowing, in the only hand that was never borrowed. Two trainers who compare their stones find the same sayings in two voices: two cranes, one sheet.

Once, in the stillest hour, it shows a line it never shows again. Whoever reads it will know who it was for.
