SUNO PROMPT - the intro, where Crystal is standing
======================================================================

    A minor. 128 BPM. Built on her own hook, before the game has started.

WHY A MINOR, AND WHY IT IS NOT A CHOICE

7.6 transcribed Crystal's motif from *01. Crystal's Lament*: A minor, 128 BPM,
4/4, hook

    A4  E5  D5  C5  B4  C5  D5

and recorded the structural fact that matters here -- A MINOR IS THE RELATIVE
MINOR OF C MAJOR, which 7.4 gives Blanche. Her motif already sits inside
Blanche's theme with no modulation at all.

So the first music in the game is HER, and the first town's theme is the same
material in the major. The player hears the town later and it is familiar
without being a quotation. NOBODY SAYS THIS, and nothing in the game marks it.

That is also why this cue must not be written as sad. A minor is doing the
work already. Write it WARM: a scientist introducing a world to a child, in
the room where she has done this many times before. The wistfulness is in the
key, not in the performance.

WHAT THIS CUE IS

MUS_NEW_GAME_INTRO, behind the CRYSTAL CLEAR speech -- the portrait, the box
in her hand, the daemon that comes out of it. Sixty to ninety seconds, looping
under dialogue the player is reading at their own pace. It has to bear being
heard three times without becoming an event.

Two shorter cues sit either side of it (MUS_NEW_GAME_INSTRUCT and
MUS_NEW_GAME_EXIT) and are NOT in scope. One track.

THE CONSTRAINT IS STILL THE ENGINE

This gets transcribed to MIDI and played on THREE CHANNELS. Everything has to
come from the writing -- register, rhythm, the shape of the line -- because
production does not survive transcription.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
THE STYLE BOX
----------------------------------------------------------------------
instrumental video game intro theme, A minor, 128 BPM, chord progression
Am - F - C - G, warm and curious, gentle music-box lead over a soft
arpeggiated accompaniment, walking bass in quarter notes, unhurried, late-90s
handheld RPG opening narration, thoughtful and welcoming, clean note attacks,
steady tempo, dry mix, no vocals

----------------------------------------------------------------------
LYRICS BOX
----------------------------------------------------------------------
[Instrumental]

----------------------------------------------------------------------
THE HOOK IT IS BUILT ON
----------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the one prompt in the set where the melody is NOT free. Ask for a
lead line that follows this contour:

    A  E  D  C  B  C  D

Up a fifth, then a slow descent, then a turn at the bottom that goes back up
two steps and stops. It does not resolve to A. That is the whole shape --
a phrase that asks and then waits.

Let the harmony resolve underneath it. The melody should not.

If Suno gives you something that lands hard on the tonic at the end of every
phrase, it has misread the brief -- the descent must hang.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
AVOID
----------------------------------------------------------------------
no vocals or vocal chops       - there is no channel for them
no pads or ambient wash        - a sustained pad transcribes as one wrong note
no heavy reverb or delay       - tails smear the note onsets
no drum kit                    - percussion is the noise channel and gets
                                 dropped; the motion must be in the bass so
                                 it survives their absence
no key change, no rubato       - the transcriber assumes one key, one tempo
no swell, no cinematic build   - this plays under text, three times
NOT SAD                        - no minor-key lament, no strings, no rubato
                                 piano. It is warm. She is pleased to see you.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SHAPE TO ASK FOR
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Eight bars of the hook. Eight more with the bass moving underneath and the
accompaniment filling in. Then back to the top. End where it began so the
loop point does not announce itself.

Nothing should peak. This is the only track in the game that plays before the
player has done anything, and it should sound like a room rather than an
opening.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN IT COMES BACK
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Is it A minor at 128? 7.6's key is the whole structural point -- if it
     drifts to C or to D minor, the relationship to Blanche is gone.
  2. Can you hear THREE lines? Close your eyes and count. If the bass and the
     middle voice blur, the transcription will lose one.
  3. Does the melody REFUSE to land? Hum the last note of a phrase. If it
     feels finished, it is wrong.
  4. Play it three times without skipping. Does it wear?

Then the stems, then the transcription prompt with the NO AUDIO gate first.
4.9 records what happens otherwise: a model that could not hear handed back an
earlier answer and admitted in its own DOUBT field that it was a guess.
