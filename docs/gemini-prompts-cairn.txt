CAIRN — Benchmark 1
===================

WHO HE IS, because the sprite has to carry it. 5.1:

    A cairn is a heap of stones that physically encodes something -- a path, a
    boundary, a grave. His creed, said plainly and sympathetically: everything
    you know is sitting in something, stone, bone, tape, silicon, and if it is
    not written down, it did not happen.

    HE IS RIGHT, AND THAT IS THE POINT. Representation is a real position and
    he holds it well; nothing in the gym undercuts him.

So he is NOT a tough guy with his eyes shut. He is a man of record -- his own
trainer says CAIRN keeps a record of every match in here, every one -- and
when he loses he does not concede, he says the player encoded something he
could not read and calls it a format he does not have.

THE SPECIES IS A TORTOISE, and it is the same reasoning the Clears are foxes.
His type is LEGACY: old formats, still readable. A tortoise is long-lived,
hard-shelled, patient, and carries its own record on its back -- the growth
rings of a shell are a physical encoding of the years, which is his creed
standing in a field.

Drawn under the fable rule, vision 9.4:

  1. INTEGRATED, never grafted -- the animal is the whole body, not a head.
  2. DRAWN PLAINLY -- flat colour, cel shading, no scale texture, no gloss,
     no musculature.
  3. HEAD-TO-BODY RATIO CARRIES AGE. He is a man in his forties: a SMALL head
     in proportion to the body. Left unstated everyone comes out cute.

Full-body character in the style of a Game Boy Advance RPG sprite. Flat cel
shading, thick dark outline, no gradients, no anti-aliasing. Plain flat green
background, no scenery, no shadow under the feet.

Subject: an adult male TORTOISE, a records keeper in his forties, drawn in
the classic cartoon funny-animal tradition -- animals through and through,
built from simple confident shapes, the style of Carl Barks or Disney's ROBIN
HOOD.

HE IS NOT CUTE, NOT COMIC AND NOT A VILLAIN. Adult proportions: a head SMALL
in proportion to his body. Calm, level, unhurried eyes. Patient and entirely
certain. He is the most careful person you will meet and he knows the value
of that.

He is a tortoise ALL OVER, not a man in a tortoise head. Warm grey-green,
with a low domed shell across his back and shoulders, its plates marked with
faint concentric growth rings. Simple rounded CLAW hands, three thick
fingers. A short thick tail. Heavy, planted, slow.

DRAWN PLAINLY. No scale texture, no individual scutes beyond the plate
outlines, no gloss, no muscle definition. Flat solid colour with simple cel
shading, the way a newspaper cartoon animal is drawn.

Clothes: a hard-wearing dark canvas work apron over a plain shirt, sleeves
rolled, sturdy boots. NOTHING behind his ear -- a first version put a pencil
there and at 64x64 four pixels of pencil read as a bow. The chalk in his hand
already says he is a man who writes things down.

Pose: standing square and settled, weight even on both feet, holding a
SLATE -- a flat dark writing tablet -- against his chest in one hand, a piece
of chalk in the other, as though he has just written something down and is
waiting to write the next thing. Looking directly at the viewer, level and
unhurried.

Full body, feet included, in a square frame. Under about fifteen colours.
