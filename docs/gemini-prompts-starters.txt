GEMINI PROMPTS - the three starter lines
=========================================

Paste one at a time. Ask for a large flat image, never for pixel art.
Save fronts to gfx/front/<name>.png and backs to gfx/back/<name>-back.png

----------------------------------------------------------------------
ROVERCUB  (front, becomes 40x40)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

A small four-legged robot, three-quarter view from the side, standing, head turned toward the viewer. It is a bare exposed chassis: visible servo blocks at the shoulders and hips, thin limbs, and no fur or covering of any kind. Two small round sensor lenses for eyes. It should look compact enough to pick up and carry. Draw it as flat illustration with a hard pure black (#000000) outline, 6 to 8 pixels thick, around the whole creature. Inside the outline use exactly four flat tones - pure black, dark grey, light grey, pure white - with hard edges between them, no gradients, no anti-aliasing, no glow, no colour, no texture. Plain pure-white background, nothing behind it, no ground shadow. Full body visible with a small even margin.
