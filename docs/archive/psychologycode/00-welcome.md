Hello, and welcome to the exciting world of PsychologyCode!

 The brain has often been thought of as a computer, and while this is partly true it is not the entire picture. That being said, by understanding the similarities and differences between these two unique systems we can not only gain insight into ourselves, but also into to the machines that we work with on a daily basis.

 Before we dive into the idea filled rabbit hole that PsychologyCode has to offer I wanted to take a moment to describe the different types of articles which will be presented. Sometimes a write up will come from a strictly psychological point, and will make use of its unique terminologies. Some of these articles will form a basic understanding which will be used later to describe some of the more exciting (Computer Science, and Psychology) hybrid ideas.
