Three rounds against each: seed 1917 and seed 7 at denoise 0.45, then one round at 0.32 with each observed failure

WHAT THE RESTYLE DOES TO THIS CAST, measured rather than guessed:

  * IT SATURATES THE FUR. Holt's brown otter comes back ORANGE at every strength and every seed, which is the Clears'
    colour -- an otter that reads as a fox breaks 9.4's fable where it is loudest.
  * IT DISSOLVES A LIGHT GARMENT ON LIGHT FUR. Al's cream shirt came back as a BARE CHEST in three of his four poses
    at 0.45. Naming it in the negative and dropping to 0.32 fixed it, which is the one thing round 2 proved.
  * IT DISSOLVES A SMALL HELD OBJECT. Cairn's slate and Al's monitor, both at 0.45; both survive 0.32.
  * IT LOSES A FACE'S ONE MARK. Init's dark muzzle goes; he comes back a generic ginger dog.

AND THE COMPARISON THAT SETTLES IT (style_compare.png), WITH ITS CLAIM CUT BACK TO WHAT WAS MEASURED. The ticket
opened by calling these nine "the oldest art in the game". Cut at 64x64 beside three of T-169's spriteforge portraits
they are plainly no worse: Cairn, Scorn and Vera hold a solid outline where BEAUTY's hands and CAMPER's fingers come
apart. But three cells are an impression, not a measurement, and the two measures that would turn it into one BOTH
CAME BACK NULL across all 75 portraits:

    lone pixels (a figure pixel matching none of its four neighbours):  spriteforge 28.0%, hand drafts 33.0%
    loose pieces (figure fragments that are not the body):              spriteforge  0.2,  hand drafts  0.4

So the general claim -- that the spriteforge portraits are the speckly ones -- IS NOT SUPPORTED, and the two worst
portraits on both measures are hand drafts (the aroma lady, the ruin maniac). What survives is the narrow version:
there is no quality argument for restyling THESE NINE, because the art they already have is at least as clean as what
the restyle returns, and the restyle costs identity.

RECOMMENDATION: keep all nine, on the record rather than by default. CAIRN's round 2 is the only cell that beats what
is in the game -- the in-game tortoise is olive on olive and the restyle separates shell, shirt, apron and boots --
and it costs the clean outline, so it is the user's call and not the tool's.
